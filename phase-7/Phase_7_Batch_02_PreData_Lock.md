## Controls and tests

1. Use G0–G9 unchanged. Record actual failed gates, unresolved gates and later conditional diagnostics separately. Unknown is not proof of failure. A hard veto may be established even while other gates are unresolved.
2. Do not simply repeat all-unknown screening: identify concrete product preferences, matched transaction candidates, alternative offers, quantifiable switching points and exactly which missing evidence can change action.
3. Keep seller asking, auction reserve/sold, registered price, effective developer basis and achieved/asking rent separate. Verify event dates against the 6 October cutoff and retain source access boundaries.
4. Price bands may be explicitly provisional research ranges if supported, but cannot be called normalised unit value before identity, comparability and motive checks. No fictitious unit, adjustment percentage, owner ratio, title expiry or current MC health.
5. Use the same synthetic capital mandate as Batch 01: RM500,000 cash including RM150,000 protected reserve; RM20,000 gross monthly household income; RM4,000 existing debt; 80% illustrative LTV, 30 years, 4.0%/5.5% base/stress, five-year hold. It is not the user's actual finances or a bank quote. New cost/rent inputs must be labelled and linked to evidence or explicitly assumed.
6. Retain macro, rent, vacancy, forced-hold, levy, valuation and marginal-future-buyer stresses. Do not infer remaining lease from completion date.
7. Record initial-expectation reversals and unavailable evidence honestly. No Core promotion unless all six original conditions are met. Use SOP for demonstrated execution improvements.

## Predeclared false-negative audit

For each Defer/Reject, state the strongest feasible alternative explanation and a reversal trigger. Separate unavailable evidence from adverse evidence. A later sale above today's ask does not itself prove a missed buy: availability, actual acquisition terms, costs and knowable condition matter. A deployable case requires a real unit and sufficient gates, not optimistic assumptions inserted solely to exercise the Deploy label.

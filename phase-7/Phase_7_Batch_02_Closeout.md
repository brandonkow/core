## SOP additions demonstrated by this batch

1. **Compute effective all-in basis before reading an auction gap.** Split bid, transaction allowance, buyer-borne inherited charges, possession/repair costs, ongoing carry and prospective levy. Keep liability allegations separate from legally allocated debt. An auction result and current availability are independent questions.
2. **Resolve governance at the applicable entity and date.** Record exact tower/MC, incident date, reported versus independently established condition, funded remediation, completion/acceptance and current service. A council-hosted newspaper clipping remains the same newspaper evidence. Apply the existing severity ladder only when the evidence supports its level.
3. **Separate family capability from adoption and premium.** Inspect room arrangement, circulation, stairs, wet areas and bay geometry. Then test actual secondary adoption and what premium it supports. A positive plan cannot prove willingness-to-pay; investment positioning cannot erase a usable household layout.
4. **Translate product premiums into total-budget switches.** Show extra acquisition cash/monthly cost and the realistic alternative. Record both prices when header/body disagree. The switch remains conditional on condition, governance and availability.
5. **Distinguish research completion from gate/outcome validation.** A complete report may end Defer. A modelled adverse scenario is not an observed Reject. A funding pass is not Deploy. Log missing outcome coverage without broadening Core or renaming the result.

These extend the local SOP supplement only. They are execution controls supported by examples in this batch, not new causal principles or evidence of improved realised returns.

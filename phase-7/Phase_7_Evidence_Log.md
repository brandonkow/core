Research cutoff: 6 October 2026, Malaysia time. All URLs below were searched or opened during this run. **S** means search-index text only; **O** means page text opened. A failed direct open is disclosed. Portal listings establish an advertised offer, not availability, title, condition or an agreed transaction. No agent/owner/MC was contacted. Evidence labels: **E** = observed source statement; **I** = our interpretation; **U** = unresolved. ## Coverage and reliability conclusion

Primary project and transport sources strengthened identity and mechanism. Public transaction fragments helped set research ranges but did not establish normalised prices for any exact selected unit. Management evidence is either old, absent or misattributed; there is no basis to grade any current MC as healthy or failed. Financial scenarios are auditable but cannot repair missing facts. The evidence supports differentiated research/allocation decisions, not an executable purchase mandate.

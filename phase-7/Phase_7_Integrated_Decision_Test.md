## P7-01 — Kiara Park high-rise, TTDI

### G0 — Reference-price validity: STOP

K1 identifies an advertised high-rise expression; K2 covers mixed project transactions. K3 establishes that the development includes materially different housing forms. Therefore normalised clearing price for the selected block/layout/one-bay unit is **U, low confidence**. Developer price, effective package, achieved rent and auction sold price are not established. A low auction headline seen in a search result is excluded: reserve/status/date cannot be reconciled to this unit. The apparent distance below a mixed-project PSF statistic is not a discount.

### G1 — Market mechanism: plausible, not premium-growth proof

I: TTDI's recurring local commerce, services, social familiarity and regional accessibility support people choosing to remain in the node. Kiara's mature grounds and established strata product offer a different housing task from a landed home. The ownership proposition is usable family space in an established setting at a lower absolute quantum than many newer premium products. **No TTDI landed household is counted as a future condo buyer without demonstrated adoption.**

Structural stage: mature / potential premium preservation. Price-recognition stage: **unresolved**, rather than automatically under-recognised because it is old. Dominant factors: usable family space and recurring location utility are Drivers; current management/physical safety are Gates; parking is a potentially dominant Ceiling/Modifier; freehold is a secondary Floor; future area catalysts are low importance. The 2021 management evidence supports historical survivability, not present liquidity or compounding.

### G2 — Buyer universe and substitutes

Primary hypothesis: local/regional households already willing to own legacy strata and accepting the actual parking arrangement. Secondary: downsizers only if lift, stairs and accessibility work; organic long-term rental investors at a sufficiently low basis. False borrowed buyers: TTDI landed loyalists, all wealthy TTDI residents, or buyers paying courtyard premiums in the low-rise blocks. Tenant source is family/professional residential use, with no verified tenant mix.

Primary comps: same high-rise block, comparable size, one versus two legally attached bays, similar condition. Secondary: matched high-rise Villa Flora family units (K5). Diagnostic: Kiara low-rise courtyard stock, used to explain product segmentation, not raise the subject's fair value. Rejected: TTDI landed prices and tiny new TOD units simply sharing an address. Same-weekend inference is strongest for Kiara/Villa Flora buyers doing the same legacy family-condo task; it is not behaviourally confirmed. Recorded sales do not disclose buyer motive; organic demand remains a hypothesis.

### G3 — Project quality: partial historical support; current gate open

K3 is unusually useful evidence of an organised resident/management relationship. It is also five years old. Current collection rate, reserves, major works, lift uptime and committee continuity are U. K7 is a reason to inspect lifts, not grounds to declare failure. Management severity is **U**, not governance grade G0. Observe refuse, visitor parking and evening arrivals before calling the project well run today. No comprehensive effective-supply count is available; same-node legacy substitutes and newer quality substitutes matter more than all nearby units.

Six mixed-project registrations cannot establish high-rise time-to-sell or sell-through. A low turnover rate may reflect content owners, low demand, missing registrations or all three. Do not choose the favourable explanation without listing/withdrawal and buyer evidence.

### G4 — Specific unit: parking changes the thesis

The advertised single bay conflicts with the most obvious two-car family buyer hypothesis. It is not an automatic rejection: a one-car household exists, and the actual discount/parking solution may compensate. But I cannot call this the best family expression without addressing it. A rented bay is not equivalent to an accessory parcel that transfers at exit. Floor/stack, direction, traffic noise, privacy, lift adjacency, seepage and internal circulation are U. A five-storey location by itself establishes neither quietness nor easy access.

### G5 — Mispricing: blocked

Validated discount = U; recoverable share = U. Cosmetic refurbishment could be reversible; bay entitlement, tower circulation and noise are persistent. No distress motive is verified. Do not label a normal one-bay clearing price a special situation. The acceptable executable entry band is U. At the ask, the model tests feasibility only; it does not endorse paying it.

### G6 — Exit architecture

Carry the same parking-tolerant strata buyer into exit. Future comps remain similar legacy high-rises plus the best newer home obtainable at the same total budget; a newer premium asking price is not a migration bridge. K4/K5 give alternative shopping leads, not an adjusted ceiling. Both the rental fallback and owner exit rely on operational condition, so they are partly correlated supports. Freehold preserves one financing attribute but does not create a cash floor.

Removing the strongest support: if quiet family ownership appeal weakens, assumed rent alone does not cover base debt and costs. Under model assumptions the five-year nominal break-even sale is about **RM965k**. A RM850k resale produces about **RM112k pre-tax loss**, even though principal has amortised. This is the hurdle a future buyer must meet, not an appreciation forecast. No target exit is locked above it without evidence.

### G7 — Terminal risk

The combined annual shock requires approximately **RM69k**. Entry plus that shock leaves roughly RM168k, only RM18k above the protected reserve. A RM50k levy instead of RM30k makes the mandate fail. Current lift/structural works can therefore change both value and capital feasibility. Freehold is source-reported; title and accessory-parcel verification remain necessary. Severe current governance impairment would override location appeal.

### G8 — Allocation

RM262.5k illustrative entry cash and about RM3,246/3,861 monthly base/stressed debt service. The capital model favours investigation over Westside at current asks, but this is not a quality ranking. Foregone option: a two-bay legacy family unit with less terminal friction, even at a higher clean purchase price. The model has too little reserve slack to accept unknown major works as routine.

### G9 — Defer

Radar: **Investigate**. Decision confidence: high that current evidence does not permit deployment; medium-low on market attractiveness; low on exact unit value. Proposed role: Defensive Utility, not Compounder.

Conditional underwriting sentence: *I would buy from a verified ordinary or motivated seller only at a matched high-rise clearing basis that compensates for one-bay limitations and ageing costs, expecting an existing strata-oriented household to buy later for recurring utility; neither acceptable price nor exit clearing value is established yet.*

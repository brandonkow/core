# Phase 7 — SOP execution addendum

6 October 2026. This supplements the handoff's SOP for this batch. The original SOP master was not located and is not claimed to have been edited. **No Core change, new causal mechanism, failure class or revised historical validation rule is introduced.**

## Controls demonstrated useful in this batch

1. **Resolve the exact asset before G0.** Match developer, road, completed/future stage, tenure, building form and residential parcel type. Applied to Amber/TWL Aster, Kiara mixed blocks and M City residential/SOHO separation. A scheme match is not yet a unit match.
2. **Separate access to evidence from quality of evidence.** Record search-index-only versus opened document, event date versus crawl date, and direct-fetch failures. An accessible portal is not an inspected title or SPA. Used where transaction pages returned 403 and listing snapshots changed.
3. **Normalise by source lineage and housing task.** Repeated rows from one registration feed count once; aliases need parcel reconciliation. Distinguish gross/strata/advertised area, full bedroom/study, parking entitlements, high-rise/low-rise and residential/SOHO. No mechanical adjustments without empirical support.
4. **Attribute operating problems to the right entity.** Separate MC/JMB, facilities manager, short-stay operator, landlord and unit contractor. Applied to M City: the hotel complaint cannot establish governance failure. Historic management awards establish a dated observation, not permanent health.
5. **Check whether a catalyst is operating.** Nearby-station labels require operator/project-stage verification. M City's future MRT3 and Aster's existing Kajang Line are different underwriting inputs. Current route length, opening hours and noise still need observation.
6. **Preserve gate stops and conditional diagnostics.** After unresolved G0, continue G1–G8 only to locate mechanisms, failure modes and sufficient independent vetoes. Do not mark a downstream scenario pass as an upstream evidence pass. G9 Defer is appropriate for unknowns; structural Reject needs positive failure evidence. A clear portfolio constraint can independently justify Preserve Capacity.
7. **Reconcile capital survival and total return.** Carry acquisition cost, refurbishment, rent shortfall, costs, amortisation and disposal allowance through exit. Count debt principal once. Compare higher-LTV monthly stress with lower-LTV cash drain. Used in all four cases and six pair scenarios.
8. **Audit outcome coverage honestly.** Initial decision expectations are test hypotheses, not output quotas. This batch could not demonstrate Deploy or structural Reject. Record that coverage gap and the failure to identify an executable opportunity; do not relax G0/G3/G4 to fill it.

These are execution controls with demonstrated examples, not evidence that real-world investment accuracy improved. No arbitrary 'three sales equals proof' rule or universal factor weight has been added.

## Batch 02 demonstrated controls - appended 6 October 2026

The [Batch 02 closeout](Phase_7_Batch_02_Closeout.md) records the evidence for these execution refinements:

9. Calculate auction all-in basis with separately identified inherited cash liabilities before treating a reserve as cheap. Verify debt allocation and current sale status; neither an agent warning nor a reserve is a completed legal/market conclusion.
10. Match adverse and remedial governance evidence to the actual tower/MC, date, scope and works acceptance. A newspaper article and its council-hosted copy count as one source. Undated contractor claims cannot prove current remediation.
11. Inspect actual family configuration, then separately test secondary adoption and the premium supported. Treat project positioning, layout capability and revealed preference as three different evidence statements.
12. Show the marginal cash/monthly cost of a preferred product against its realistic cheaper alternative. Resolve headline/body price conflicts, added partitions and sale-versus-auction changes before defining a switch price.
13. Report completed research and validated outcome coverage separately. Four completed gate sheets ending Defer do not demonstrate Deploy or structural Reject. Preserve those misses without changing Core to force a result.

No evidence of current MC solvency, achieved rent, title expiry, physical inspection or executed purchase was obtained in this batch. These controls improve execution discipline; realised investment accuracy remains untested.

## Post-Batch 02 Red Team execution correction - 6 October 2026

14. A residential-high-rise investment universe does not restrict the buyer's substitute universe to high-rise. At entry AND required exit quantum, identify plausible same-weekend landed alternatives where relevant; compare all-in repair/finance/possession costs and housing utility. Use them as diagnostic competitors, not automatically recommended assets, pooled valuation comps or borrowed buyer demand. Applied after P7-RT02 exposed the omitted Aster landed-budget challenge; no exact price ceiling has been validated.
15. Separate completion age, observed condition, current management finances and remaining title term. A newer building cannot inherit a superior management grade, lower capex bill or longer remaining lease by assumption. Applied to Sterling (publicly reported 2008 completion) versus Kelana Mahkota (2005); actual maintenance and title evidence remain pending.

These enforce existing Core/SOP principles. See P7-RT02 in the [Red Team register](Phase_7_Misses_and_Red_Team.md) for evidence, changed priorities and limitations.

## Evidence-Judgment Divergence execution binding - 6 October 2026

Apply the cross-module control in the single [master framework](../Residential_Investment_Framework.md) at evidence intake, each relevant G0-G9 stage, material evidence changes and before the final capital decision. Use its reusable flag block only for material conflicts; complete all four explanation families, a falsifiable hypothesis, supporting/opposing evidence, existing-mechanism routing, status, decision impact and review trigger. Keep populated records in case workpapers.

Unresolved conflicts increase uncertainty, required protection and evidence burden while lowering conviction. Existing gate stops and the four G9 decisions remain authoritative. No intuition, flag, lower offered price or smaller position independently authorises investment. The full control lives in the master; this SOP binding does not define a second divergent copy. Earlier source/case history remains unchanged and is not retroactively re-labelled as validation of this newly authorised control.

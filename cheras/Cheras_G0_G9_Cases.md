# Cheras - Nine advertised expressions through G0-G9

Cutoff: 6 October 2026. Apply the unchanged [master](../Residential_Investment_Framework.md). Evidence IDs resolve in [the register](Cheras_Evidence_Register.md). Calculations, input quality and sensitivities are in [financial underwriting](Cheras_Financial_Underwriting.md). These are complete gate-by-gate desk assessments, not certified completed purchase due diligence. Advertised expressions are not uniquely identified legal parcels unless stated. No case is treated as a successful prospective prediction yet.

## Common decision binding

G0 remains unresolved in every case: **STOP discount claims**. G1-G8 below are conditional diagnostics, not a workaround that passes G0. A price-to-ask gap, broad project median, construction award or rental advertisement cannot clear it. At deployment obtain identifiable relevant voluntary secondary transactions, net incentives, rights, parking, condition, floor/stack and seller motive; normalise transparently. Reject requires an evidenced structural gate failure, not missing documents. The independent portfolio choice can still be Preserve Capacity.

For G4, every unvisited unit needs original approved plan, actual alterations, orientation/daylight/noise, outlook permanence, neighbouring uses, lift/circulation, car-park route and accessory-parcel confirmation. For G7, every unit needs title/encumbrances/transfer eligibility, actual bank valuation/terms and current management balances/budgets/works. Leasehold expiry is never inferred from completion year. A bank valuation is financing evidence, not proof of demand. Engineering, flood and slope safety are unverified, not assumed defective.

Every case uses the five-year horizon and synthetic mandate from the pre-research lock. A future buyer has a purchase motive described below; no unsupported percentage of investor/family ownership is assigned. Stated required exits are model burdens, not forecasts. There is no validated numerical terminal floor; a hard asset-specific ceiling also remains unproved. Current substitutes indicate resistance and reference-set migration, not a permanent universal cap.

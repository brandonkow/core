# Cheras - Regional residential investment underwriting

Decision date: 6 October 2026. Residential high-rise investment; five-year comparison horizon. English artifact, Mandarin discussion. The existing [master](../Residential_Investment_Framework.md), Core freeze and G0-G9 remain authoritative.

## Investment view

My current preference is to investigate low-quantum, usable three-bedroom units at M Vertica and Maxim Residences first. Aster Type B and Riana South follow. Windows on the Park deserves serious family-quality research, but the selected RM900k large unit receives Preserve Capacity under the comparison mandate. These are differentiated judgments, not nine interchangeable unknowns.

The central regional insight is that Cheras does not have one location premium or one exit buyer. A buyer may pay for inner-city access, a familiar local family routine, or proximity to an institution. Those markets overlap only when a particular household can substitute one daily routine for another. More central can now mean a lower advertised purchase quantum than some peripheral family products. More space can mean a thinner future buyer pool rather than more recoverable value.

This is a completed regional desk-underwriting exercise with ten functional catchments and nine selected expressions taken through all ten gates. It is not an exhaustive census of developments, a site inspection, a lender valuation or certification that any advertised unit is still available. Mahkota Cheras, Balakong and several developments have screening-level coverage only. No case has established normalised, unit-specific clearing at G0. Consequently, no discount claim or Deploy is authorised; downstream analysis is explicitly conditional. See the [nine gate sheets](Cheras_G0_G9_Cases.md), [financial workpaper](Cheras_Financial_Underwriting.md), [evidence register](Cheras_Evidence_Register.md), and [divergence/misses register](Cheras_Divergence_and_Misses.md).

## Comparable discipline and demand transfer

The primary comparable is the same actual development, tower/type and rights package, adjusted for floor, condition, parking and sale circumstances. A secondary comparable must solve the same household's housing task. Aster and Maxim can compete for some Connaught households; M Vertica enters that choice only when the household's commute and lifestyle make central relocation viable. Their PSFs are not directly interchangeable.

Landed homes are diagnostic alternatives for total family budget, upkeep and convenience. They are not high-rise valuation comparables, and land PSF cannot be compared with condo built-up PSF. BTHO evidence includes different house sizes/conditions across a broad quantum range. It establishes a substitution question, not a universal RM700k condo ceiling. [BTHO recorded-price leads](https://www.brickz.my/transactions/residential/selangor/cheras/bandar-tun-hussein-onn/landed/terrace-house/intermediate/).

UTAR and UCSI establish institutional presence. They do not establish the number of off-campus renters available to any condo, future ownership demand or willingness to pay a family premium. Likewise, tenancy can help fund a hold without providing an independent buyer exit. Owner and investor exits must be supported separately; both can still contract together when credit tightens.

## Entry discipline and future buyer burden

The synthetic mandate is RM500k cash with RM150k protected, RM20k gross monthly household income and RM4k existing debt. Base financing is 80%, 30 years, 4%; stress rate 5.5%. Allowances are 5% acquisition and 3% sale costs; rents have eleven paid months. Results are pre-tax and exclude transaction-specific RPGT/income tax and lender exceptions. These assumptions permit comparison and are not the user's personal balance sheet.

None of the nine selected asks is supported by income alone at an illustrative 8% equity hurdle with flat resale. C01 requires about RM592k in year five; C05 RM587k; Aster RM664k; Riana RM725k. Those figures ask a market question: who pays that price for the then-older unit, against what alternatives, with what financing? They do not predict appreciation.

Holding the terminal sale at today's selected ask, the model's 8% entry ceilings are approximately RM415k for C01 and RM403k for C05. These are sensitivity outputs, not fair-value opinions, executable opportunities or automatic buy prices. A cheaper unit with impaired parking, title or condition may be worse. If the seller will not offer a defensible entry and independent evidence cannot support the required future exit, the correct action remains Defer or Preserve Capacity.

The nominal break-even numbers are weaker tests: they merely recover the modelled capital and carry without time-value compensation. Even first-tier research candidates would lose money if resold at the same price after the assumed costs and carry. A research ranking is not a claim of positive expected return.

## Financing, terminal and portfolio risk

Every single selected expression survives the base one-year forced-hold scenario, but their headroom differs. Windows leaves about RM155k against the RM150k reserve; zero rent, a lower bank valuation or a larger levy separately breaches that reserve. Its Preserve Capacity decision reflects fragile capital headroom and uncertain large-unit economics, not an invented base-case failure or project-wide structural Reject.

All 36 two-property combinations fail the simultaneous reserve test. Different Cheras addresses do not remove shared credit, commuter, tenant and investor-exit exposure. This mandate should resolve one strong candidate before considering a second correlated acquisition.

Freehold does not remove physical ageing, major works, valuation or loan-tenure risk. Leasehold expiry is unverified for the relevant units and is never calculated from building completion. No numerical terminal floor is established. Landed competition and new supply suggest possible resistance; they do not prove permanent ceilings. Flood/drainage, slope, noise, lift waiting, parking usability and current MC liabilities remain unit/project checks, not silently assumed passes.

## What changes the decision

The highest-value next evidence is a clean matched sale/rent and operating comparison for C01 versus C05, including exact title/parking/condition. Evidence supporting one is not allowed to repair the other's gaps. Aster moves up if its actual access and ownership demand justify the extra capital. Riana moves up if family resale adoption, rather than rental demand alone, supports its exit burden. Windows moves up under a separately modelled smaller layout, materially better entry, or a different capital/own-use mandate.

Four material conflicts are logged with all four explanation families and predeclared falsifiers. Wrong-project bargains, bad geocoding and unstable portal dates are recorded as source failures; no market prediction has yet matured. No new causal mechanism meets the promotion threshold. Execution lessons remain in the case-level SOP record.

The market-level proposition most worth Red Team challenge is specific: at these advertised quantums, usable compact three-bedroom stock in Maluri/Connaught deserves earlier investment investigation than paying a substantial family-product premium farther out. This is conditional on acceptable actual operations and buyer fit. If informed market observation says those first-tier products repel the target ownership buyer even after price adjustment, that is a material divergence to investigate, not a reason to quietly preserve my ranking or automatically trust the objection.

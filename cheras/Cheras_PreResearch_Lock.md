Date: 6 October 2026, Asia/Kuala_Lumpur. Saved before new regional web research. The existing master framework and Evidence-Judgment Divergence control are authoritative. No further theoretical phase or new Core mechanism is proposed.

## Scope and known information

Study greater Cheras across KL and Selangor as functional housing markets, not one postcode, price series or administrative unit. Investable expressions remain residential high-rise; landed homes, commercial nodes and employment/institutional anchors are diagnostic demand/substitution evidence only. The universe includes Maluri/Cochrane, Shamelin/Pandan fringe, Midah/Mutiara/Taynton, Connaught/Len Seng, Alam Damai/Damai Perdana, Batu 9/Suntex/Cuepacs, Bandar Tun Hussein Onn/Cheras Selatan/Balakong, and Bandar Mahkota Cheras/Sungai Long. These are initial analytical catchments, not asserted official boundaries.

Prior practitioner-informed knowledge: Aster has family-capable layouts despite investment-oriented overall positioning; its higher family-unit quantum can face landed-house competition. Venice Hill must be investigated by tower and current governance, not project-wide historical stigma. These are not new blind discoveries. Existing Phase 7 source and financial models remain frozen.

## Initial propositions to falsify

1. The strongest region-wide urban access/anchor depth is likely nearer Maluri/Cochrane, but entry pricing and investor supply can make a less central mature family product the better investment expression.
2. Mutiara/Taynton/Midah may offer the strongest blend of local daily utility and residential identity; a mixed-use TOD product must earn its own family premium rather than inherit the area's demand.
3. Connaught has real commuter/education/local demand, but high-rise supply competes across distinct housing tasks. Rail adjacency, rent and room-rental activity do not prove family ownership premium.
4. Alam Damai/Damai Perdana and the outer family belts may contain better family formats at lower PSF, but weaker last-mile access and landed substitution can cap exit quantum.
5. Batu 9/Suntex, Bandar Tun Hussein Onn and Balakong are not one interchangeable catchment. Functional road access and the actual walk to rail matter more than a Cheras label.
6. Sungai Long/Mahkota require separation of local-family ownership from university-linked investor demand. Neither source should automatically be transferred to every condo.

## Decision and evidence rules

- Record primary/secondary/diagnostic/rejected comparables and actual buyer tasks, absolute quantum and current versus future reference sets.
- Separate registered transactions, asks, auction reserves/sold, developer headline/effective packages and asking/achieved rents. Never infer a discount from a project median alone.
- Prefer official developer/operator/institutional sources for identity and operating anchors; listings and registered-price aggregators retain their limitations. Do not treat multiple syndicated rows as independent sales or raw listings as units available.
- Identify family layout capability, actual resale adoption and premium separately. Management is unknown without relevant current evidence; resident ethnicity is not intrinsic quality.
- Use explicit material divergence flags and all four explanation families. Lock confirming/falsifying evidence and review triggers before subsequent outcome evaluation.
- Continue with meaningful relative preferences even where a specific unit cannot Deploy. State what changes a rank or price conclusion; avoid all-unknown screening as a substitute for market interpretation.
- Scenario mandate carried forward for comparability, not asserted as user finances: RM500k cash including RM150k protected reserve, RM20k gross household income, RM4k existing monthly debt, 80% LTV, 30 years, 4% base/5.5% stress, five-year hold. Entry/disposal allowances 5%/3%; all rent, operating, repair and tax boundaries must be labelled. Regional ranking should also explain how a different capital/holding mandate would change it.
- No field visits, private MC documents or achieved tenancies are presumed; no contacts, purchases or alerts without authorisation.

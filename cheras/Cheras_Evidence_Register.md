### Retrieval/version note

The M Vertica E15 ID has different refresh dates in opened and indexed/language versions (including 2 October, 8 September and 3 August 2026). This does not establish original days on market. The advertised RM489k/850-sf expression is consistent across those observed versions; availability and exact furnishing/parking remain unresolved.

### Overall evidence quality

The study establishes useful differences in product, price asks, demand anchors, competition and financial burden. It does not establish a normalised unit-specific clearing price for any of the nine expressions. Site-specific flooding, drainage, slope stability, night noise, facade/rail proximity, lift waiting times, parking manoeuvrability, title restrictions and MC liabilities remain unverified; none is silently rated safe or failed. Portal popularity, list counts and generic AI-style neighbourhood copy were not used to prove buyer depth.

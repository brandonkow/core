## Why this is not yet a G0 pass

A broad observed range is not automatically a normalised target-unit interval. For Eve, the published 689 sqft records do not establish how the advertised 680 sqft expression, bay entitlement, condition and consideration package compare. A 1.3% area-label difference may be harmless, but that has not been established; no linear PSF adjustment is invented. The observed spread does not prove that unobserved differences are safely bounded. Recent data also end in April 2026.

For Maxim, timing is a larger additional problem: much of the visible near-size evidence is from 2023-24. A visible June 2025 850 sqft record is RM350,000; the March 2026 829 sqft record has no publicly visible price in the inspected extract. Missing rows do not establish missing demand.

This is a price-reference problem, not a requirement to obtain all purchase documents. A borrower approval, personal portfolio statement or signed tenancy is not required to establish the narrower price reference. Public plans, transaction particulars or defensible triangulation may resolve it. Conversely, moving the unresolved price differences into G4 would not make the G0 comparison valid. See [gate and uncertainty dispositions](Gate_Review.json).

## Counterexamples and implications

If Eve's future sale merely matches the recent observed upper value of RM405,000, the replacement-base five-year result remains about -RM7,521. At RM375,000 it is about -RM36,144. These are persistence sensitivities, not predictions that current observations must cap future prices. Its RM444,000 target is an assumption requiring an independent future-buyer mechanism.

Maxim's old RM440,000 observation would give about +RM11,933 if that price were achieved after five years. The date and configuration prevent treating this as the probable exit. At RM400,000 it is about -RM26,231.

Eve remains the preferred privacy-oriented research lead within this pair, not a cleared investment or universal winner. Maxim remains a different household-task challenger. Privacy utility and negative full-cost carry can coexist, so no artificial Divergence Flag is raised merely to reconcile them. The retention proposition and all four rival-explanation families are recorded in Gate_Review.json. No intuition receives a resale premium.

## Finite reopening and monitoring

There is no automatic date-based upgrade. Reopen when a materially better exact offer, authenticated lease, governance/works evidence, approved component/plan, matching transaction set or actual catalyst-to-demand transmission arrives. Re-test unfavourable evidence too. A signed lease with incentives, an occupied unit that cannot be reset immediately, a lender valuation belowprice or a common-property levy can reverse an apparently favourable entry.

Unresolved divergence increases proof requirements and demanded margin of safety; a lower chosen number does not make the source conflict resolved. No arbitrary extra discount is claimed sufficient while the clearing interval and structural costs remain unknown.

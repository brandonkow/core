## PJ-D01 - Hidden mispricing versus component/financeability error

**⚠ DIVERGENCE FLAG**

**Observed Evidence:** Amcorp225k/1600and Centrestage150k/1500scenarios produce unusually strong income arithmetic; they are the only two positive annual base-carry rows in this PJ set.

**Contradicting Judgment / Observation:** A residential investor cannot assume that advertised suites, office-related addresses, rental models and financing rights describe equivalent eligible parcels. Outlier yields may be authentic discounts or mismatches.

**Possible Explanations:**

1. Data limitation: stale asks, sale/rent component mismatch, area/furnishing differences, syndicated leads and unquoted liabilities.
2. Judgment bias: distrust of old mixed-use stock could discard genuine income value; attraction to a high yield could suppress identity checks.
3. Omitted variable: permitted use, charge allocation, works, remaining lease, bay rights and actual lender/buyer access.
4. Structural change: changed management, use permissions or ordinary tenant demand could improve income; no such change is validated.

**Falsifiable Hypothesis:** The apparent excess yield disappears after exact parcel/use, collectible ordinary rent and full liabilities are matched.

**Investigation Completed:** Reviewed original sale/rent descriptions, component inconsistencies, older Amcorp transfer leads and corporate asset/use context. Ran full-cost and adverse rent/credit/works scenarios. Did not infer residential illegality from unrelated business addresses.

**Evidence Required:** Exact strata/approved use and current terms; parcel-specific lease/lender position; ordinary comparable leases/collections; itemised fees and works.

**Falsification Conditions:** If lawful eligible residential use, effective entry and comparable ordinary income survive these checks with affordable full costs, the mismatch hypothesis is falsified and a hidden-mispricing thesis may proceed through G0-G7. Conversely, an incompatible component or materially lower net income invalidates the apparent residential yield, not all units in the development.

**Core Module Routing:** G0 reference validity; G2 buyer universe; G4 unit/asset fit; G5 mispricing; G6 exit buyer/liquidity; G7 financing/terminal risk.

**Current Status:** Unresolved. Identity quarantine retained. Numerical surplus is not evidence of a proven discount. No investable ranking.

**Contradicting Judgment / Observation:** Neither smaller size nor a low PSF reliably predicts better investment economics. The larger apparent bargain also has tenure/bathroom/rental mismatch and an unverified effective internal layout.

**Possible Explanations:**

1. Data limitation: stale/non-executable sale, high rent outlier, different floors/baths/furnishings, area measurement and misleading room headers.
2. Judgment bias: previous preference for low quantum could overlook larger value; excitement at a rank reversal could over-adopt3500rent.
3. Omitted variable: balcony/internal space, parking, family utility, service cost per usable area, title and loan acceptance.
4. Structural change: a seller-specific exit or altered household demand could create temporary relative value; no time-series proof exists.

**Falsifiable Hypothesis:** A genuine larger-unit entry discount provides stronger ordinary income than the studio after like-for-like title, usable layout, furnishing and charges are reconciled.

**Falsification Conditions:** Larger preference fails if lower sustainable rent/costs remove the advantage, the low price is non-executable or different rights/defects explain the discount. If matched rents and rights support a durable advantage, studio-first ranking is rejected.

**Core Module Routing:** G0 comparables; G2 buyer universe; G4 asset fit; G5 price discovery; G6 exit; G7 financeability.

**Current Status:** Partially Resolved. The automatic studio-first inference is not supported; actual investability remains unresolved. No portfolio position.

## PJ-D03 - Product preference versus investable family pricing

**⚠ DIVERGENCE FLAG**

**Observed Evidence:** At current scenarios Sterling640k/2000has markedly weaker coverage than Mahkota499k/2400. Public developer records establish different configurations; relative age supports only a product-age comparison.

**Contradicting Judgment / Observation:** The user's prior view that Sterling is newer may support qualitative preference, but management quality was explicitly unknown. A better-feeling or younger product can still be a worse income investment at a given price.

**Possible Explanations:**

1. Data limitation: mismatched condition, furnishings, room count, advertised versus collected rent and stale entry price.
2. Judgment bias: newer-product/brand preference, familiarity or anchoring to older low rent; alternatively choosing the cheaper project solely for yield.
3. Omitted variable: actual management/major works, usable third room, parking and household willingness to pay.
4. Structural change: improved management, changed neighbourhood routines or tenant mix could alter relative rents; none verified.

**Falsifiable Hypothesis:** Sterling's premium is primarily product/owner-use willingness rather than enough sustainable rental premium to justify an ordinary-income allocation.

**Investigation Completed:** Added Mahkota, separated age from governance, kept restricted/auction comparables out, modelled common finance and multiple rent/price conditions. Management superiority is not claimed for either.

**Evidence Required:** Equal-condition approved layouts, ordinary leases/charges/works and rights-matched voluntary buyer evidence.

**Falsification Conditions:** A verified stronger Sterling net rent or attainable lower entry sufficient to compensate its extra cost invalidates the current income ranking. Confirmed materially worse Mahkota liabilities could also reverse it. Mere age preference does not.

**Core Module Routing:** G2 owner desirability/buyer universe; G3 project quality; G4 asset fit; G5 price; G6 functional substitutes; G7 costs/terminal risk.

**Current Status:** Partially Resolved. Product attractiveness and investment attractiveness are distinct; the actual quality premium remains unresolved. Sterling current-shortfall tier stays lowest without a structural Reject.

## PJ-D04 - Positive headline performance versus hidden exit risk

**⚠ DIVERGENCE FLAG**

**Observed Evidence:** A Midtown portal narrative presents positive price performance and infers family preferences from listing composition. Simultaneously, public915sftransfer amounts and current offers are widely dispersed.

**Contradicting Judgment / Observation:** A thin/mixed sample and listing supply cannot establish matched price growth, owner-buyer depth or support for the required future exit. Historical headline strength may conceal financing or willingness-to-pay limits.

**Possible Explanations:**

1. Data limitation: small sample, mixed product/rights/incentives, transaction lag and asking/clearing confusion.
2. Judgment bias: attraction to a redevelopment story; alternatively anchoring to the lowest offer and calling all earlier values wrong.
3. Omitted variable: bays, view/floor, furnishing, management, owner demand and direct substitutes.
4. Structural change: post-delivery normalisation or changed local demand/supply might genuinely alter price discovery; no matched series demonstrates direction.

**Falsifiable Hypothesis:** The advertised strength is mostly sample/product composition and does not establish an independently supported915sfexit premium.

**Investigation Completed:** Verified brochure layout, separated650kand690koffers, retained lower700kand higher2025transfer observations without averaging, modelled flat/declining exits and adverse investor required yields. Corrected Atwater residential completion to2022; no2026VP story used.

**Evidence Required:** Matched effective voluntary transfers across time, unit/package details, independently observed buyer/lease depth and current competing stock.

**Falsification Conditions:** A stable matched series plus repeated equivalent buyer transactions at higher effective prices would falsify the composition-only explanation. Persistent discounts/weak depth after matching would strengthen the hidden-risk concern; unadjusted aggregate movement settles neither.

**Core Module Routing:** G0 reference validity; G1 lifecycle/structural change; G2 buyer depth; G5 mispricing; G6 exit ceiling/liquidity; G7 terminal risk.

**Current Status:** Unresolved. No growth extrapolation or owner premium. Higher proof burden; no unsupported percentage safety margin.

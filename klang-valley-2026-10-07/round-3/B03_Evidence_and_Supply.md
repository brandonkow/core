## Search stops and evidence gaps with consequences

No comprehensive deduplicated listing inventory, owner/tenant ratio, current lender acceptance, sold-day history or project-level achieved rental panel was obtained. Thus neither a region-wide scarcity premium nor a liquidity ranking is established. Current management/works are not inferred from awards, newer age or absent complaints.

Auction PDFs/portal reserve leads were found for selected PJ stock, but no complete proclamation, arrears, possession and rights package was inspected. No reserve is treated as a clearing transaction. Distressed strategies remain separate research gaps and cannot lower the ordinary-entry reference.

Repeat public listing searches now have low marginal value for the current Defer outcomes. The finite routes are exact parcel documents, like-for-like completed transfers/leases, fee/works evidence and current competing inventory. No contact or paid record was commissioned. Reopen on those observations or a material delivery/financing change, not merely to obtain more listing counts.

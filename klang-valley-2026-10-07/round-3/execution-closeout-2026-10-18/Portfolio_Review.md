# Current-SOP portfolio and terminal review

This closes the **numerical portfolio-refresh gap**, not actual G8 allocation. The 8 October portfolio report remains historical. Its synthetic cash/reserve and superseded stress magnitudes are not current decision inputs.

## Scope and method

Retain all seven original study portfolios P0-P6, not a newly optimised basket. Reuse the 138 historical operating expressions, their 122 reconciled project groups, and the unchanged 16 October financial model/SOP. Current-source overlays (including Neo's 265k/280k branches) are **not silently merged** into historical bases. P4 therefore still includes the quarantined original Centrestage, Amcorp, Neo and Arc inputs; it is not an executable set of current offers.

Six paths: BASE (+20% target, 60 months); FLAT; J1 completed-property cost/rate/fee stress; J2 demand/delayed-exit stress; J3 their union; and separate S3 resale valuation. J1/J3 use 4.25%, one RM10k fit-out addition per parcel, and 5% annual fee increases from year 3. J2/J3 use 80% rent, two initial void months, sale at month 66 for 90% original price. No construction delay is inserted into these possession-based examples. Brightwood remains separate.

S3 lends 90% of a valuation at 90% of agreed price (81% of price), with 4%/35 years unchanged. It is an actual-financing sensitivity, not a new standard or an extra J3 shock. Loan-related fees are recalculated. Its lower interest can increase nominal profit while demanding more initial equity; that is not improved property quality or a reason to lower leverage to rescue ordinary coverage.

Monthly aggregate cash is the sum of member cashflows. Peak funding is the deepest cumulative deficit before any sale, including entry. It is **not** the sum of individual peak amounts and does not spend future sale proceeds early. All hypothetical purchases and shock calendars align; actual dates may change funding. Unknown bills, personal tax/final RPGT, future finance acceptance and real commitments remain outside these dated planning numbers.

## Complete two-expression aggregation

There are 9,453 pairs before identity reconciliation, 17 same-project pairs excluded, **9,436 different-project expression pairs / 7,381 unique project-pair groups**. The six paths produce 56,616 pair aggregations, alongside 828 individual paths and 42 selected basket paths.

All 9,436 pairs have negative flat-exit profit under these historical inputs; the least negative is about RM2,079. The +20% target produces 9,190 positive pair profits. These are scenario counts, not eligible investments, probabilities, forecasts or successive funnel stages. A single quarantined positive flat-exit case does not imply a profitable two-project portfolio.

No synthetic reserve-pass count is published. Each scenario's funding/profit distribution and project-variant funding spread are in [results](Portfolio_Results.json). Variant spread can be large; selecting a cheaper expression silently would distort project-level comparisons. Same-project alternatives are not simultaneous unit inventory.

## Terminal review and actual G8 interface

The results retain all 138 nominal recovery prices and the future buyer's equity/payment under 85% LTV, 4.5%, 30 years, with a separate 10% valuation haircut. Buyer equity excludes acquisition/fit-out costs. Six-percent **gross** income references are not the older 6% operating-yield valuation and are not clearing-price evidence. A seller's recovery price does not establish buyer willingness to pay.

Actual allocation remains blank until candidates are admitted and the following are available:

- Liquid cash available on the relevant dates, independently ring-fenced reserves and committed capital payments.
- Existing borrowing commitments and actual applicable loan offers/eligibility; no universal DSR threshold invented.
- Actual unit price/cost schedule and available funding during delays and lower-rent periods.
- Shared tenant/employment, location, management/works, catalyst, rate and future-buyer exposures.
- A comparison with retaining capital and the best admissible alternative.

For a chosen dated scenario: funding headroom = genuinely available cash after other commitments and protected reserves minus the aggregate peak requirement, adjusted for actual timing. This is a cash test only, not an automatic G8 or G9 pass. No balance-sheet questionnaire is needed now while no candidate has cleared G0.

Different project names do not remove shared credit, tenant or exit exposure. These aligned stresses are selected deterministic bounds, not estimated covariance or a maximum possible loss.

## Decision view

Kuchai Avenue and Endah Promenade are the most useful conditional income leads among the identified eligible expressions; their small simplified surpluses still turn negative after normal vacancy and operating allowances. Neither is a proven best asset. A practical family layout in Kuchai may retain ownership utility that a large Endah studio cannot reproduce, while Endah's lower price reduces capital and exit quantum.

The newer two-bedroom controls are not automatically superior investments. Desa Green715, The Tropika732 and M Oscar708 increase useful space or product appeal but require rents/entry prices better than their tested base cases to meet the user's standard. A two-bedroom is eligible on equal terms; it is not preferred by default. Studio comparison remains valid only after whole-parcel, original-layout and permitted-use checks.

## Why the apparent winners can fail

**Kuchai:** 926-sf recorded prices span substantially different amounts within October2025. The low ask may be a valid opportunity, an inferior rights/condition package, an incentive difference or a listing lead that cannot be bought. Present evidence does not select one explanation. Price certainty and practical household utility outrank a nominal6% result.

**Endah:** a739-sf studio/one-bedroom can cost less while offering less flexible household use than a real two-bedroom. Its assumed rent is not guaranteed. The base standard surplus is152/month, but the complete annual scenario is about-1,930. Lower rent, higher actual fees or a special collection can remove the advantage. An investor exit requiring a higher yield can reprice the asset even if occupancy is stable.

**Newer small units:** M Oscar and Tropika may be better fits for certain owner households than older compact stock. That can support a product-quality thesis, yet does not demonstrate the exit price required by a leveraged investment. Tenant willingness to pay for fresh furnishing may not convert into the same buyer willingness to pay for ageing fixtures five years later.

**Old Klang Road family substitute:** the investigated Residence8 listing has conflicting consideration and studio/master features. It remains a screen, not a new numerical case. Refusing that contaminated input does not remove larger-family stock from the regional market; it prevents an unsupported apparent bargain from setting the ranking. [Attributable listing](https://www.propertyguru.com.my/property-listing/residence-8-for-sale-by-chris-choo-500466310).

## Supply and forward market structure

M Aspira introduces future two-/three-/four-bedroom competition to Taman Desa. Its current marketing starting price and permitted gross price are unreconciled; do not choose whichever basis makes Desa Green look cheapest. M Aurora is a separate Old Klang Road development from Bukit Jalil Aurora SOVO. M Oscar and Trion2 are already completed in the source evidence, so their rents/available stock matter today.

The [NAPIC context](Official_Market_Context.md) shows why a general shortage narrative needs proof, but state overhang is not the vacancy rate of any of these projects. No stock count in this report establishes take-up, duplicate-free active listings or months to clear. Published developer 'sold out' and completed status do not mean owners have occupied or rented every unit.

Existing retail and rail are present utility. Future MRT/road proposals receive no base rent or exit uplift without a dated transmission mechanism and recognition test. Employer headcount is not automatically the residential tenant pool.

## Financial conditions and capital decision

Across the eleven B02 expressions in the current comparison, no tested base case has positive cost-complete annual carry. Current-shortfall cases remain the lowest income-research tier under the user's rule. Passing6% is preferred, not an unconditional decision rule; G0/quality/credit/exit remain required.

The full financial table includes debt amortisation and flat-price exit losses. Even Endah's relatively light carry still needs about333,084 after five years to recover modelled capital at the298,000 entry. That figure is not a predicted resale price. A higher cash deposit would change investor cashflow but does not replace the standard90% comparison.

No correlated portfolio allocation is approved. Exposure to the same credit cycle, ordinary-rental households, overlapping new stock and strata cost inflation persists across neighbourhood labels. The previous synthetic portfolio tests remain comparisons; new controls must join the final portfolio replay before a combined recommendation.

Current G9: Defer for selected expressions. Quality, income attractiveness, investigation priority and capital allocation are kept separate. No site/operating evidence supports declaring a district or entire development structurally bad.

## Remaining scope and next action

Material limits remain in upper-quality family stock, standalone compact parcel identity, executed distressed evidence, micro-location externalities and private operating/lease records. They prevent full-market value claims. For existing cases, exact identity and operating proof have higher decision value than another generic ad list.

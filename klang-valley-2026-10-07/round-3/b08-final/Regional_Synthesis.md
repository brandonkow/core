## What the evidence supports

Outer-corridor affordability is heterogeneous. A low total price can support ordinary income, but net terms, whole-title identity, actual costs and tenant access matter more than location branding. The Arc is the only nonnegative annual base among these twenty conditional inputs; its RM820 surplus can disappear with one additional RM100 monthly expense. Solstice and MKH Boulevard II are near annual neutrality under their assumptions but retain specific identity and price-discovery holds. Neither a university, airport, public employer, mall nor data centre allocates a tenant to an individual unit.

The research now distinguishes completed stock from future projects and active advertisements. An operating building may still have developer stock. A fully sold project can compete for tenants and future resales, while its original affordable price may be inaccessible to an ordinary investor. No advertisement count is treated as unique supply, completed absorption or liquidity.

The strongest favourable counterexample to a compact-first interpretation is Savanna: its costed upper furnished-rent branch becomes annually positive. That branch is an asking scenario with extra capital, not an observed investment result. The strongest adverse counterexample to a township-premium narrative is that higher household desirability can coexist with persistent negative ordinary-income carry in Amber, Habitus, GAIA and several family controls.

**Price, income and alternatives.** R351's 549sf historical transfer improves the reference investigation but does not establish an adjusted current clearing interval. **Exit and counter-thesis.** Compact investors remain eligible exit buyers if sustainable income and their financing support the ticket. Family buyers can compare larger rented or owned homes, but their willingness to pay for this exact tower is unmeasured. Better amenities and rail proximity may be real; they cannot rescue unverified net prices or mismatched rent. A dated net offer, original rights/plan/bays, current bills, ordinary lease and condition-adjusted transfers would change the decision. Additional generic township pages would not.

## Allocation and terminal discipline

The twenty cases produce 306 conditional evaluations, 190 synthetic joint first-year stress paths and 135 unequal-rent cells. Thirteen of fifteen selected pair comparisons change annual-cash leader somewhere across the rent grid. That sensitivity is a warning about unsupported ranking, not a probability forecast.

All 190 pair paths preserve the assumed RM150k reserve within the synthetic RM500k cash budget. That is not the user's affordability, actual lending or proof of diversification. Alanis alternatives, Habitus alternatives and GAIA alternatives share project risk; they must not be treated as independent opportunities. Student/provider, airport/transport, developer-package and remote-family-demand exposures can also correlate across different names. Programme-level portfolio integration remains unfinished.

The model includes a 12-month zero-rent path, unquoted-cost buffer, works levy, valuation/term stresses and a delayed exit. Finite no-sale tests cannot guarantee unlimited holding liquidity. Five-year nominal break-even and 8% required exits are demands placed on future buyers, not appraised values. Investor exits at illustrative operating yields are separate from the user's 6% gross preference.

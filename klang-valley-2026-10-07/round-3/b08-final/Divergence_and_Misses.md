## F-D01 - family utility versus weak lower-rent economics

⚠ DIVERGENCE FLAG

**Observed Evidence:** R360's RM350k/RM1,500 scenario has negative monthly and annual carry; another unit advertises RM2,300 furnished rent. The costed upper branch becomes annually positive.

**Contradicting Judgment / Observation:** A functional three-bedroom/two-bay family product might be investable even where a lower generic rent screen appears weak. This possibility challenges a blanket compact-first ranking.

**Possible Explanations:**

1. **Data limitation:** rent condition, date, block and bay differences; no achieved lease or effective clearing price.
2. **Judgment bias:** preference for family utility or desire to rescue a low-yield case by selecting the highest ask.
3. **Omitted variable:** actual furniture quality, room privacy, bays, tenant utility bills and turnover costs.
4. **Structural change:** a genuinely different ordinary tenant cohort might pay more, but no measured absorption or new lease stream is established.

**Falsifiable Hypothesis:** A specified fit-out and original family layout can repeatedly achieve rent above the full-cost hurdle without excessive voids or capital cost.

**Evidence Required:** selected inventory/capex quotations; comparable executed whole-unit leases and paid months; actual bills; rights and effective purchase terms; adjusted transfers.

**Falsification Conditions:** ordinary achievable rent stays below roughly RM2,002 before further costs; fit-out or voids consume the improvement; annual income cannot support an exit buyer's required yield. The upper branch's positive carry does not eliminate its flat-price five-year equity loss.

**Core Module Routing:** Asset Fit, Buyer Universe, Comparable Set, Mispricing, Financing Risk, Exit Buyer.

**Current Status:** Unresolved. Base ordinary-income priority stays low; favourable branch remains quarantined. Required margin of safety and proof rise, not fall.

⚠ DIVERGENCE FLAG

**Observed Evidence:** XMUM's first-year foreign-student campus rule, HWUM provider-specific shuttle rights and BPH quarter allocation process constrain different pathways. Airport trains have operating hours and a dated future fare increase.

**Contradicting Judgment / Observation:** Nearby institutions or transport may support reliable private rents, but the relevant accessible household pool can be much narrower than the headline population.

**Possible Explanations:**

1. **Data limitation:** aggregate enrolment/jobs, old accommodation tariffs and incomplete route/shift information.
2. **Judgment bias:** either automatically assigning institutional demand to an address or excluding the entire market after finding one restriction.
3. **Omitted variable:** year of study, contract eligibility, household formation, quarter waiting status, shift and total travel budget.
4. **Structural change:** genuine intake, provider terms, employment or transport changes can alter accessible demand; currentquantum/timing remain unproven.

**Falsifiable Hypothesis:** An identified eligible tenant cohort can use this exact private unit and access arrangement at a total cost competitive with its real alternatives.

**Evidence Required:** current ordinary leases/tenant profile, relevant contract/eligibility and route rights, door-to-work costs and shift compatibility.

**Falsification Conditions:** tenant must use another accommodation route; shuttle/bay unavailable; total travel/housing cost exceeds alternatives; ordinary lease evidence fails to appear.

**Core Module Routing:** Buyer Universe, Asset Fit, Externality Ceiling, Catalyst, Exit Buyer.

**Current Status:** Partially Resolved. Certain institutional boundaries are documented; actual private tenant depth remains unresolved. No government/student guarantee or permanent area rejection.

As of 12 October 2026. Apply the existing Evidence-Judgment Divergence control; no new Core module or SOP rule. The pre-existing MJ-F01 was committed before this investigation. This turn's finite design was recorded in session RAM before browsing and is preserved in Financial_Inputs.json; it was not a separately committed preregistration.

## Falsifiable hypothesis

Pines' particular product and daily-living ecosystem produce stronger household adoption, retention and financeable demand than the selected East Lake and Subang Avenue operating expressions, after actual condition, total housing cost and package are matched. A separate claim is that delivered and adequately funded management service helps preserve that demand.

Evidence capable of supporting this: independently matched ordinary household leases, paid collections, renewals and actual buyer decisions; useful room/bay/route configuration; documented service outcomes with audited finances and works funding. School context and marketing are weaker, distinct evidence.

Falsifiers: no retention/buyer-depth advantage after matching; inferior usable space, noise, parking or school/work journeys; renovation and capital works consuming the apparent benefit; demonstrably comparable or better household capture at a challenger. Absence of authenticated leases is inconclusive, not a negative outcome.

## Divergence and uncertainty

Available observations do not establish a material conflict on the same market proposition, so no full DIVERGENCE FLAG is fabricated. Authentication is not a prerequisite for activating the existing control: credible, material contrary observations can trigger investigation before they are fully verified. The user's comparative family preference and a cheaper rival's conditional cash advantage can both be true. A leasehold/freehold contradiction in an advertisement is a source-quality conflict requiring title resolution; it does not itself disprove a market mechanism.

If household, renewal or buyer evidence materially contradicts MJ-F01, use the master's full flag, including observed evidence, contradicting judgment, explanations, falsifiable hypothesis, evidence required, falsification conditions, Core routing and status. Unresolved uncertainty cannot reduce entry standards. No personal conviction, area label or unauthenticated premium justifies Deploy.

## Recorded misses and evidence limits

1. **Family-capacity inference remains corrected.** The previous family queue overextended usable space into actual adoption. This packet records whole-unit offers but does not promote them to family leases.
2. **Core hypothesis not validated.** No representative tenant composition, renewal panel, collected-rent audit, owner-occupier purchase sample or current MC financial statement was authenticated. Continued Defer is an evidence result, not a finding that the projects lack demand.
3. **Furnished-rent/original-sale gap made concrete.** P-S1 expressly calls for renovation. The existing 40k allowance is not proof it can produce the P-R1 package or any premium rent. No zero-cost jump to renovated 7,000 asking rent.
4. **Mixed sale/rent copy.** P-R3 describes sale 780k below a 3,800 rental header. P-R1 separately confirms a 3,800 asking context; neither is an executed lease. They are not automatically independent physical units.
5. **Lease length can masquerade as price evidence.** S-R1's 2,299 header and 2,300/2,400 body must be read with two-year versus one-year terms. The concession may be economically sensible but retention and payment are unverified.
6. **Cheaper entry has a source hold.** E-S1's 330k price carries contradictory tenure wording and unconfirmed bays. Keep the arithmetic as a conditional branch; do not advertise an executable 8% investment or change the historical 350k input.
7. **Management evidence improved only one step.** JL Facilities' own client list supports an association. It does not establish audited reserves, satisfactory actual service or lower future levy risk. Similar age cannot cancel repair differences.
8. **Wrong-entity governance contamination avoided.** Subang mall consultancy is not residential MC evidence. The Ong Maju archive's adjacent Avelon AGM/audit material does not belong to East Lake.
9. **Access/recency boundaries retained.** Pines room detail failed; a historical 3,300 rented page later redirected; a 700k furnished sale and a renovated Subang 3,000 rent redirected to project indexes. No redirect was interpreted as a sale/lease outcome.
10. **School existence does not establish capture.** Both M'KIS and AISM provide official geographic context. No project-specific enrolment, school-run route or identical neighbourhood utility was established.
11. **No relative management grade or empirical return ranking.** 49 checked model evaluations are calculations, not 49 units, leases or realised outcomes.

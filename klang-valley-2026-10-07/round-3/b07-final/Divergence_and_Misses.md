## ⚠ DIVERGENCE FLAG — central Puteri utility versus income

**Observed Evidence:** R349600000/1950 produces3.9%gross,-771standard monthly balance and-13602annual cash. Even the favourable older580000/2300 FF pairing remains-341standard and-8795annual.2Rio creates a current/newly-completed classification question and additional comparable household choice.

**Contradicting Judgment / Observation:** central Puteri small-household convenience and a future commercial ecosystem may warrant a premium and conceal a temporary rent lag.

**Possible Explanations:**
1. Data limitation: asking rather than cleared prices, old FF rent, furnishing mismatch, unavailable incentives, different sizes in transfer data.
2. Judgment bias: attachment to centrality, newer amenities or a preferred address; selecting an old low sale and high rent together.
3. Omitted variable: true work route, bay convenience, usable rooms, maintenance, competing2Rio and household willingness versus Skypod/Kinrara.
4. Structural change: actual Rio City delivery and employment/retail absorption might increase rent; new competing landlords could absorb the gain instead.

**Falsifiable Hypothesis:** a verifiable net entry and same-condition ordinary rent, after funded fit-out/carry, can materially close the income gap because central utility attracts households not captured by the current asks.

**Evidence Required:** matched861sf transactions and actual net terms; signed leases/renewals and inventory; charges/works; household substitutes; delivered catalyst and absorption timeline if relied upon.

**Falsification Conditions:** no attainable lease near the required rent; full cost remains negative after the defined transition; competing stock captures tenants; incentives disappear after normalisation; utility premium supports sale price but not rent or funded exit.

**Core Module Routing:** G0Comparable Set;G1Lifecycle/Catalyst;G2Buyer Universe;G3Supply;G4Asset Fit;G5Mispricing;G6Exit Buyer;G7Terminal/Carry.

**Current Status:** Partially Resolved. Favourable condition/price sensitivity alone fails to repair standard coverage. Future rent transmission remains unproven. Defer, lowest current-shortfall research tier; higher required margin of safety and proof, lower conviction. No2030 uplift in the base.

**Contradicting Judgment / Observation:** useful mature family space and moderate entry quantum may offer better long-term value than studios; alternatively ageing liabilities, rights and a one-bay constraint may make the apparently strong ratio misleading.

**Possible Explanations:**

3. Omitted variable: leasehold term/restrictions, usable rooms, bay limitation, capital works, household eligibility for UPM housing, actual routes and competing supply.

**Falsifiable Hypothesis:** an exact rights/condition-matched parcel can sustain ordinary rent above its authenticated full-cost threshold and attract a funded resale buyer without relying on temporary furnishing premium.

**Evidence Required:** plan/title/term, actual charges and works programme, ordinary lease/renewal/collections, matched transfers, eligible tenant alternatives and future buyer finance at supported exit price.

**Falsification Conditions:** achieved rent remains below full cost; higher asks do not let; material levy/works absorbs apparent discount; rights/credit or one-bay utility narrows buyers; cheaper substitutes capture the intended households.

**Core Module Routing:** G0Price Discovery/Comparable Set;G2Buyer Universe/Financing;G3Governance/Supply;G4Asset Fit;G5Mispricing;G6Exit Liquidity;G7Terminal.

**Current Status:** Unresolved. It is an income-evidence priority, not validated hidden mispricing. Raise proof and margin-of-safety requirements; no subjective confidence premium.

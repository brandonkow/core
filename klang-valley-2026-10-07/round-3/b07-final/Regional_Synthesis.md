## Scope and comparison boundary

One project has one canonical area. Puchong, Serdang, Subang, Shah Alam and Klang are connected but do not share a single interchangeable tenant or resale market. Work/school route, household size, cars, privacy and eligibility define the substitution set. Institutional beds, rooms, office components, landed houses and future projects are diagnostic alternatives; none is quietly promoted into an ordinary residential high-rise investment.

Budget ranges below describe the worked expressions, not percentiles of all available housing. Both new and older stock, studios through larger family units, distressed/identity-held leads and future price-only controls are considered. There is no quota requiring a studio in every catchment.

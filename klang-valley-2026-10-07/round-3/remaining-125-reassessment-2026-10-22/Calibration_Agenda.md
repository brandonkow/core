## Correct execution before loosening rules

1. **Research evidence is not the full purchase file.** Old G0 text sometimes conflates missing rent, fee invoices and borrower approval with price research. Current overlays move these to the gates whose conclusions depend on them. Examples: Pacific Place KV08 collected lease; First Residence KV09 fee bill; Saville R227 sale/rent mismatch; Gravit8 R236 replacement lease. Such evidence can still matter to G0 if the adjustment itself is material.
2. **Investor-led exits are eligible already.** Old inherited R201-R206 and R237-R248 G2 language demanded a surviving non-investor exit. Current reviews remove that erroneous universal requirement. No causal Core change is necessary.
3. **Rounding is not a rejection mechanism.** Near-size examples R3071065/1068, R3361108/1105 and R3451065/1066 need interpretation, not automatic exclusion. Material plan/tower/rights differences still matter.
4. **Do not demand price perfection.** A supported broad interval can satisfy the current SOP. Missing data is not proof a project is bad, but an unbounded adjustment cannot be silently declared immaterial.
5. **Do not manufacture freshness.** Historical studies and prior numerical results are retained. Four focused public counterchecks are not125 refreshed offers. No realised investment miss is proved by this process audit.

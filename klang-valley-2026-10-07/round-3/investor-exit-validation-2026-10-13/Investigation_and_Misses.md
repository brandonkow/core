# Investigation, falsification and misses

As of 13 October 2026. MJ-X01 was committed before this pass. The finite design in Financial_Inputs.json was recorded in session RAM before new public searches, not as a separate committed preregistration. The existing Core and SOP remain authoritative.

## Hypothesis and tests

The user's informed judgment is that Zeva has more diverse demand and better investor resale appeal than Solstice, even at similar rent. The test is whether the selected compact captures less correlated tenant sources and repeatable collections, and whether funded buyers pay enough to compensate for entry and operating costs.

Support would require matched ordinary leases and paid collections, renewal/replacement records with dates and denominators, actual source dependence and practical journeys, and completed financeable investor transfers. Regional employers, advertised tenant categories and the number of listings cannot supply those denominators.

Falsifiers include equally concentrated Zeva tenants; impractical worker journeys; no repeatable collection/renewal advantage; a premium consumed by fit-out, fees or credit limits; or a robust Solstice student/operator route and funded resale at its lower entry cost. Missing evidence is inconclusive, not a negative market outcome.

## Divergence control

**Current status: Unresolved hypothesis; no material same-proposition Divergence Flag established.** Cheap entry, higher possible qualitative appeal and unequal exit burdens can coexist. A professional-targeted advertisement does not falsify a claim about actual student predominance. A formal flag does not require fully authenticated evidence first: sufficiently credible contrary observations can trigger investigation when material.

If such a conflict appears, use the full master template: observed evidence; contradicting judgment/observation; possible explanations; falsifiable hypothesis; evidence required; falsification conditions; existing Core routing; and Resolved / Partially Resolved / Unresolved status. Investigate all four families. Neither intuition nor incomplete data overrides evidence. Unresolved uncertainty raises the evidence and margin-of-safety burden and lowers conviction; no invented discount percentage replaces missing underwriting.

## Misses and limits retained

1. **Exit-depth gap remains unresolved.** The original cash comparison did not establish actual buyer funding or secondary-market depth. The new arithmetic quantifies the required advantage; it is not that advantage's validation.
2. **Student concentration was not measured.** A real university channel is stronger than a generic nearby-campus story, but neither it nor professional marketing establishes dominant occupancy. Multiple campuses can still share a common shock.
3. **Rail transmission is unverified.** Station/route context does not complete a real journey. Portal walk times, old operator tables and individual historical disruptions were not used as current journey measurements.
4. **Source refresh limitations.** Zeva's original sale and several indexed rental details failed direct reopening. The correctly identified project index returned a September snapshot while search showed October. No delisting, absorption or price trend was inferred.
5. **Wrong initial project slug.** A guessed Zeva project URL failed; it was discarded and the correct index found independently. The failed URL supplied no evidence.
6. **Operator fee is not landlord receipt.** The university price PDF was readable as extracted text, but screenshot rendering failed and schedule dates vary by provider. No owner rent upgrade, deposit income or guaranteed lease was manufactured.
7. **Cost records remain absent.** An old Solstice fee article and broad project metadata cannot update the 180/month proxy. Actual charges, reserves, works, taxes and fit-out quotations remain unknown.
8. **Asking spread is not a return.** Zeva 210k partly furnished versus 280k furnished does not measure a 70k improvement value. Its lower advertised basic rent is not an achieved collapse or proof of a repeatable 350 premium.
9. **Carry parity is not return parity.** The 43/month comparison covers the debt-service gap only. It does not recover extra initial cash or prove the buyer will accept the required resale price. Equal-rent capitalisation is a sensitivity, not equal fair value.
10. **No downside waiver for stronger narrative.** Both original scenarios lose money at five-year unchanged-price exits. More paid months, better rent or higher resale must be observed, not assigned to the preferred project.
11. **Historical overconstraint not revived.** R231's old workpaper contains a non-investor-exit condition. Apply the later approved investor-exit SOP and current overlay; do not require an owner buyer as a universal prerequisite. The historical document remains intact.
12. **Search contamination and time mixing excluded.** Project-labelled landing pages containing other buildings, a 2025 offer with a different-city address, multi-year transaction aggregates and a 2025 auction reserve do not authenticate current clearing values.

# B05 Evidence-Judgment Divergence and misses

Cutoff: 7 October 2026. No Core amendment, G10, universal screening rule or causal promotion. These flags operate within existing G0-G9. Contradicting judgments below are the analyst's hypotheses or source observations, not invented statements by the Senior Market Red Team.

Divergence is a trigger for investigation, not permission to override evidence. An investment thesis must ultimately be explainable through an identifiable market mechanism rather than personal conviction alone. Strong conviction without a falsifiable mechanism increases risk rather than analytical confidence.

Aligned positive evidence/judgment still requires normal underwriting; aligned negative evidence/judgment generally deprioritises. Both conflict directions below require investigation. No flag itself creates a thesis or earns Deploy. Unresolved conflict raises uncertainty, required proof and the justified margin of safety, while lowering conviction. It never relaxes the income, reference-price or exit burden.

## B5-D01 - Lucentia: attractive gross income versus charge uncertainty

**⚠ DIVERGENCE FLAG**

**Observed Evidence:** 600k/454-sf sale lead and3400rental lead imply6.8%gross. Sale copy states1165maintenance without the period. Secondary per-square-foot information is much lower but not a current invoice.

**Contradicting Judgment / Observation:** A seemingly attractive central studio may have no ordinary-income coverage if1165is monthly. Treating it as quarterly merely because that fits the thesis would override the evidence.

**Possible Explanations:**
1. **Data limitation:** Listing may omit billing period or combine charges/arrears; syndicated copy is not independent confirmation.
2. **Judgment bias:** Yield anchoring and preference for a newer integrated development encourage selection of the lowest fee.
3. **Omitted variable:** Sinking contributions, ancillary services, parcel share units, furnishing and ordinary versus managed lease costs could differ.
4. **Structural change:** Fee revisions or post-delivery operating changes could make historical fee estimates obsolete. No such revision is confirmed.

**Falsifiable Hypothesis:** The1165figure is a quarterly recurring invoice rather than the ordinary monthly charge; if so, sustainable net income could be materially stronger.

**Evidence Required:** Dated parcel-specific invoice covering an explicit period, charge/sinking/arrears breakdown and ordinary whole-unit tenancy with matched furnishing/bay.

**Falsification Conditions:** Invoice confirms recurring monthly1165, lower ordinary rent, or further costs remove the quarterly-branch surplus. An unauthenticated per-square-foot review is insufficient confirmation.

**Core Module Routing:** G0 comparable/cost validity; G3 management; G5 price/mispricing; G7 carrying cost; G6 income-led exit.

**Current Status:** Unresolved. Monthly branch yields about-8032annual carry; conjectured quarterly branch about+1288. Both remain Defer. No fee estimate is declared correct and no gross-yield ranking is executable.

**Evidence Required:** Phase-specific completed/active stock, repeated ordinary leases/absorption and tenant budgets, actual costs, effective offers and a funded carry plan. Projected worker totals are not substitutes.

**Falsification Conditions:** Persistently weaker ordinary leases, falling capture as competing phases deliver, or annual-neutral rent remaining unattainable at feasible entry.850-sf base needs roughly9568monthly under the assumptions. No unbounded waiting period is accepted.

**Core Module Routing:** G1 catalyst/lifecycle/structural change; G2 buyer universe; G5 capitalised premium; G6 exit-value burden; G7 financing/carry.

**Current Status:** Unresolved; lowest ordinary-income investment-research priority at current inputs. An investor cannot use future district conviction to clear G0 or capital deployment.

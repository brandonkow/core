# Programme divergence investigation and misses

Date: 8 October 2026. This is an integration of existing evidence and common scenarios, not new primary market observation. Prior regional flags and misses remain immutable. All flags below route through the existing G0-G9 mechanisms; none creates G10 or promotes a causal Core rule.

## ⚠ DIVERGENCE FLAG PI-D01 — Strong apparent income, weak admission evidence

**Observed Evidence:** Five annual-positive scenario expressions: Centrestage R208, Amcorp R309, Neo R219, Parkview R321 and Arc R353. All retain source-level identity, current-offer, package or ordinary-lease holds. Only R208 has a marginal flat-price profit.

**Contradicting Judgment / Observation:** A yield-driven ranking would call these the best opportunities, while the corresponding offer and product evidence is among the least settled.

**Possible Explanations:**
1. Data limitation: stale or inconsistent asking prices, unmatched area/condition, incentives, short-stay versus ordinary rent and missing charges.
2. Judgment bias: desire to produce a winner; anchoring on low ticket and headline gross yield; alternatively excessive distrust of a previously troubled product.
3. Omitted variable: permitted use, approved layout, package obligations, repair liability, financeability and actual net income.
4. Structural change: genuine post-guarantee normalisation, improved management or refreshed leases could improve Arc or another case, but current contracts and ordinary performance must demonstrate it.

**Falsifiable Hypothesis:** At least one apparent annual surplus survives authentication of the exact residential parcel, unrestricted effective purchase price, ordinary lease and full cost.

**Evidence Required:** The source-specific title/plan and net-offer route, current ordinary lease/receipts, invoice/works exposure, lender acceptance and adjusted effective transactions. Existing regional papers specify which conflict applies to each case.

**Falsification Conditions:** Surplus disappears after net-price/condition/fee correction; selected component or restrictions do not match the assumed investable asset; rent relies on a non-transferable guarantee, operator or incompatible package.

**Core Module Routing:** G0 Reference Price Validity; G3 Project Quality; G4 Unit Quality; G5 Price/Mispricing; G6 Exit Architecture; G7 Finance/Terminal; G9 Capital Decision.

**Current Status:** Unresolved. No surplus becomes a purchase thesis before mechanism and evidence resolve. Higher uncertainty requires more evidence and a higher safety margin against a validated reference; no arbitrary percentage is fabricated while G0 remains stopped.

## ⚠ DIVERGENCE FLAG PI-D02 — Family usefulness versus compact capital efficiency

**Observed Evidence:** Pines, Windsor and East Lake produce stronger annual carry than several newer compact alternatives, yet their entry capital, one-bay or works limits and rents differ. Eve and Maxim have near-identical annual deficits; 19 of 20 chosen rent grids change leaders.

**Contradicting Judgment / Observation:** A categorical belief in family owner demand or in small-unit yields would give a stable ordering that the conditional comparisons do not support.

**Possible Explanations:**
1. Data limitation: advertised bedroom labels, unlike-condition rents, dated listings and uncertain fees distort comparisons.
2. Judgment bias: personal household/lifestyle preference, familiarity with mature areas, or a wish to defend the prior compact selection.
3. Omitted variable: original usable rooms, privacy, bays, lifts, circulation, tenant household composition, repair needs and real functional substitutes.
4. Structural change: household composition, delivered competing family stock or changing work routes could alter willingness to pay; no automatic rent credit is given.

**Falsifiable Hypothesis:** A particular original family unit earns enough repeatable incremental net rent or evidenced funded resale demand to justify its extra all-in capital versus the relevant compact/local substitute.

**Evidence Required:** Matched ordinary leases and condition, actual bays/routes and costs, original plans, like-for-like resale terms, borrower funding and competing household budgets.

**Falsification Conditions:** Claimed premium requires superior refurbishment not included in entry cost, does not recur in matched leases, or is outweighed by works/fees and the buyer's alternatives. A cheaper compact must also fail if its form/use or lease cannot support the assumed task.

**Core Module Routing:** G1 Market Mechanism; G2 Buyer Universe; G3 Project Quality; G4 Unit Quality; G5 Price/Mispricing; G6 Exit Architecture; G8 Portfolio/Opportunity.

**Current Status:** Partially resolved. Universal layout ranking is rejected by the comparison; project-specific willingness and execution remain unresolved. Investor-led exits remain eligible.

## ⚠ DIVERGENCE FLAG PI-D03 — Pair funding pass versus programme resilience

**Observed Evidence:** 7,730 of 9,436 different-project expression pairs preserve the synthetic reserve in the 12-month common shock; only 1,735 do so with 24-month hold plus lower valuation/shorter actual term. The same expressions form only 7,381 unique project-pair groups. P1 and P3 pass selected shorter tests but fail longer ones.

**Contradicting Judgment / Observation:** Different district labels and a large pair-pass count can create confidence in diversification and sufficient liquidity.

**Possible Explanations:**
1. Data limitation: repeated layouts inflate counts; single horizon and optimistic lender value omit funding duration; modeled shocks are not observed probabilities.
2. Judgment bias: counting buildings or regions as independent, selecting only passing pairs, or stopping at a convenient test horizon.
3. Omitted variable: correlated income/rate/credit exposure, concurrent works, lender constraints, shared institutional tenants and protected cash needs.
4. Structural change: a common lending repricing, slower absorption or prolonged sale market could extend carry beyond the original horizon.

**Falsifiable Hypothesis:** A specifically funded portfolio can retain its protected reserve without sale, refinancing or salary under the stated joint duration and credit stress.

**Evidence Required:** The investor's actual liquid capital and commitments only at allocation stage; unit-level costs/credit quotes; cash timing; documented income and relevant exposure concentration. No user financial profile is assumed here.

**Falsification Conditions:** Minimum monthly cash breaches protected reserve or becomes negative; actual entry financing is unavailable; shared risk removes the supposedly independent recovery routes.

**Core Module Routing:** G6 Exit Architecture; G7 Finance/Terminal; G8 Portfolio/Opportunity; G9 Capital Decision.

**Current Status:** Resolved for the stated synthetic scenarios, unresolved for an actual investor. P0 preserves nominal cash; no interest/inflation comparison is claimed. A project-pair group with one passing expression is not wholly resilient.

## ⚠ DIVERGENCE FLAG PI-D04 — High rent / location strength versus recoverable exit

**Observed Evidence:** 40 expressions reach 6% gross in the base scenario, but 137 lose money at a flat five-year exit. Ordinary research leads generally require a buyer accepting a lower operating yield than the illustrative 5-7% investor-exit scenarios, or independently evidenced rent growth/owner willingness.

**Contradicting Judgment / Observation:** Good rent coverage, prestigious location, new infrastructure or a familiar household product can feel sufficient to support capital recovery.

**Possible Explanations:**
1. Data limitation: gross rent omits vacancy, fees and costs; asking rents do not prove future leases; mixed historical transactions do not validate an exit price.
2. Judgment bias: anchoring on purchase price or required break-even as if a future buyer owes it; assuming capital gains to justify a deficit.
3. Omitted variable: future buyer equity/loan term, competing new stock, ageing works, exit costs, investor yield and functional landed/rental alternatives.
4. Structural change: new jobs/access, post-VP absorption or improved management might raise net income, but transmission, timing and funded downside remain to be demonstrated.

**Falsifiable Hypothesis:** An identifiable market mechanism supports a fundable future buyer at the required exit burden after competing supply and costs, independently of the seller's invested capital.

**Evidence Required:** Matched future/current ordinary income where available, actual buyer funding and adjusted resale evidence, phase delivery/absorption, cost trajectory and substitute budgets. Present threshold prices are not forecasts.

**Falsification Conditions:** Required rent or buyer quantum exceeds evidenced alternatives; delayed improvement exhausts carry; required investor yield compresses without support; owner willingness is asserted rather than observed.

**Core Module Routing:** G1 Market Mechanism; G2 Buyer Universe; G5 Price/Mispricing; G6 Exit Architecture; G7 Finance/Terminal; G9 Capital Decision.

**Current Status:** Unresolved at unit level. Current weakness remains researchable at lowest priority; a catalyst narrative alone cannot raise the capital decision.

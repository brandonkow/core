## Decision

The declared 61-catchment public desk programme is complete, including cross-region comparison and programme portfolio/terminal integration. **No purchase is cleared. All 138 operating expressions remain G0 STOP / G9 Defer.** This is a purposive sample of 122 project groups, not every Klang Valley development or a representative market survey. It cannot establish that the market has no opportunities.

There is no defensible universal winner from these inputs. Research priority, useful accommodation, conditional investment arithmetic and an executable capital decision are separate. Five positive annual base scenarios all carry material unresolved identity, offer or lease issues. Choosing the highest numerical return would concentrate the least settled assumptions.

## Comparable investments are not always comparable homes

Eve, Maxim, Zeva and Solstice compete for an investor's capital, but households do not necessarily substitute Ara Damansara for Cheras, Serdang or Cyberjaya. They must not enter one valuation-comparable panel. A useful cross-region comparison asks what cash, tenant task, operating evidence and exit mechanism each investment requires. Within-region evidence determines whether local households will pay.

Likewise, a larger original family unit can outperform a compact on ordinary rent without being a better investment at its acquisition price. A studio can support an investor-led resale without a proven young-owner buyer pool. No layout, age, TOD label, family preference or prestigious address receives a universal bonus.

## What the numbers actually discriminate

- 56 of 138 meet the user's simplified monthly standard; 40 have scenario gross yield at least 6%. These are expression counts, not independent projects or cleared opportunities.
- Only R208, R309, R219, R321 and R353 have nonnegative base annual cashflow. None has investment-ready evidence.
- Only R208 has nonnegative five-year flat-price profit, approximately RM431 before tax and unquoted costs. It is the quarantined Centrestage expression. A tiny model surplus is not a margin of safety.
- None retains nonnegative annual cashflow at its case-specific lower rent plus 20% higher fee. These are chosen scenarios, not probabilities.
- 19 of 20 preregistered cross-task rent comparisons reverse their annual cash leader. Arena/Tropika does not reverse within the selected grid; that is not proof of superior product quality or a reliable exit.
- The common 90%/4%/35 baseline does not promise actual loan approval. Lower lender valuation can materially increase cash required even when the smaller loan reduces monthly debt service.

[Financials](Financial_Underwriting.md) contain all 138 base rows and thresholds; [portfolio/terminal risk](Portfolio_and_Terminal_Risk.md) tests cash durability and exit funding. Do not promote a favourable historical branch to the aggregate base.

## Cross-region explanations and counterexamples

**Small units are not automatically inferior.** Solstice/Zeva reduce entry capital and support a plausible income-investor exit; household owner redundancy remains unproved. Conversely, Univ360, Centrestage and SOVO/SOHO or partitioned products show why low quantum cannot resolve legal form and financeability. This is Asset Fit / Buyer Universe / Exit Architecture, not a new rule against studios.

**Family utility is not automatically an investment premium.** Pines/Windsor/East Lake challenge blanket compact preference. Prima Midah, Sterling and several outer family products demonstrate that extra space, newer age or total rent can still fail at the selected price and cost. Landed substitution must be matched by total budget, condition, tenure and actual routines, not invoked as a universal ceiling.

**Maturity can improve observability without proving operating health.** Older stock has possible repair and buyer-finance exposure; newer stock can face delivery/fit-out competition. Allevia, Laurel, SWNK, M Arisa, ANYA and other delivered components cannot remain hypothetical future catalysts. Institutional facilities and malls establish alternatives/access, not automatic tenant allocation.

**Current weakness can be temporary, but the transition must pay for itself.** Rent and furnished-upgrade branches in the regional files preserve possible improvement. They also charge refurbishment and vacant months. Savanna's favourable annual branch still loses at a flat five-year sale; a positive rent bridge does not validate recovery.

**Apparent diversification can be duplicate exposure.** The 138 expressions collapse to 122 project groups. Distinct projects still share rate, credit, employment, institutional accommodation, developer supply and strata-repair risks. The programme tests simultaneous shocks and does not infer empirical correlations from names or geography.

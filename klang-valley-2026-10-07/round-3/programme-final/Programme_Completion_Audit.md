| G9 Capital Decision | Every current expression remains Defer; conditional diagnostics continue without pretending G0 passed. No permanent Reject from current shortfall alone. |

Evidence-Judgment Divergence operates across stages, investigates both hidden mispricing and hidden structural risk, and never independently justifies investment. Unresolved conflict increases uncertainty, proof burden and required safety margin against a valid reference. It cannot lower underwriting standards.

## Explicit residual limits

Not every development, price tier, restricted parcel, street, original compact form or new phase has a matched valuation. Actual net offers, parcel rights, original plans, current ordinary paid leases, management accounts, works liabilities, physical routes/noise/lifts, legal/tax treatment and loan approval remain unverified where stated. Source dates range historically; an 8 October report does not refresh an old observation.

Those gaps have specific decision consequences and finite evidence routes in regional workpapers. No universal regional winner, scarcity premium, observed occupancy, bankability or project-quality grade is inferred. Public ledgers can reopen on a material new fact; desk completion is not permanent market truth.

No source demand or financing claim is advanced merely because the model checks. No actual transaction, field visit, third-party contact, paid data, unattended monitoring or future-outcome observation is performed by this integration.

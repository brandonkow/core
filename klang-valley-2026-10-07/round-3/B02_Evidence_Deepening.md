### Divergence investigation: R3-D01

Observed evidence: the R205 numerical proxy suggests 6% gross and monthly standard coverage. Contradicting observation: the physical/legal product identity used for that residential proxy is not established and primary developer descriptions distinguish office suites.

A - Data limitation: component aliasing and conflicting portal classifications are observed; unmatched price and rent leads may describe different products. B - Judgment bias: pressure to find a compact product that passes the income standard may have led to accepting a 'studio' label without tracing the original product. C - Omitted variable: permitted use, original parcel and lender acceptance affect both rentability and resale buyer depth. D - Structural change: a lawful conversion or changed approval is possible in principle, but no evidence has been found; furniture or residential marketing does not demonstrate it.

Falsifiable hypothesis: R205 combined a compact SOVO-related lead with residential-suite assumptions, making its rank unsuitable for the residential universe. Confirm with the original listing parcel/plan matched to approval and lender category; falsify if the exact 384-sf unit is independently established as the eligible residential product and the rent/price match it.

Routing: G0 identity/reference, G2 buyer universe, G4 asset/unit fit, G6 exit buyer, G7 financing, G9 Defer. Status: **Partially Resolved** at project-component distinction; exact parcel unresolved. No numerical safety-margin haircut can repair a mistaken identity.

**Contradicting Judgment / Observation:** treating that gap as investable residential mispricing is unsupported: the RM221,100 body explicitly identifies an auction and says images are not of the actual unit. An investor cannot yet establish eligibility, possession, effective basis or a matched reference. This conflicts with the investment inference, not with the existence of the advertisement.

**Four explanations tested:** A — data limitation: sale-category/reserve distinction obtained; exact lot, auction date and outcome remain unavailable. B — judgment bias: cheap-price anchoring was challenged by reading the body and seeking another auction source, rather than calculating a headline discount as alpha. C — omitted variables: rights restrictions, condition, vacant possession, arrears, bid competition and financing could absorb the gap; specific liability allocation is unknown. D — structural change: forced-sale composition could create recoverable mispricing, but no project deterioration, new demand or recovery mechanism is established.

**Falsifiable hypothesis:** a genuinely transferable residential auction parcel, after bid price, liabilities, repairs, possession delay and financing, could retain a defensible discount against matched voluntary clearing. Confirm only with the exact POS/COS/title, eligibility and possession facts, actual outcome/effective offer and adjusted comparable records. Falsify this candidate if the parcel cannot be acquired in the mandate, the reference is wrong or documented all-in costs remove the proposed protection. Missing documents remain unknown, not a failed investment outcome.

**Core Module Routing:** Reference-Price Validity, Comparable Set, Mispricing, Asset Fit, Financing Risk and Exit Buyer through G0/G4/G5/G6/G7. **Current Status: Partially Resolved.** The ordinary-sale interpretation is rejected; hidden mispricing and hidden structural/cost risk both remain possible. No discount percentage, buy decision or reduced evidence burden is granted. A higher required safety margin cannot be quantified credibly until the unknown liabilities and reference are bounded.

The RM221,100 and RM245,700 listings are not assumed to be the same parcel or a sequence of auction reductions. No outcome of the 7 October auction has been established. The compact R229 SOVO hold remains separate and unchanged.

# B08 Working02 — Evidence–Judgment Divergence and misses

This is cross-gate execution of the frozen principle: **Divergence is a trigger for investigation, not permission to override evidence.** An investment thesis must ultimately be explainable through an identifiable market mechanism rather than personal conviction alone. Strong conviction without a falsifiable mechanism increases risk rather than analytical confidence. No G10 or new causal module is introduced.

The judgments below are explicit analyst hypotheses, not attributed user beliefs. Every flag is material to price, rent, eligibility, exit or funded carry. Sources are in [Evidence.json](Evidence.json), cases in [Cases_G0_G9.md](Cases_G0_G9.md).

## D02-01 — apparent airport/university demand versus accessible tenant pool

⚠ DIVERGENCE FLAG

**Observed Evidence:** An original compact family exists and advertised rents appear able to cover simplified monthly costs at some prices. Listings market student/airport demand and high ROI.

**Contradicting Judgment / Observation:** A large nearby institution or airport may support housing use, but the eligible pool and total shift-compatible travel budget can be much smaller than the headline population.

**Possible Explanations:**

1. **Data limitation:** Listing dates/identities vary; no leases, unique vacancy count or actual travel measurement.193kproject floor is not a selected ordinary parcel.
2. **Judgment bias:** The analyst may favour small cheap units after being invited to include studios, or overcorrect by dismissing all student/airport demand.
3. **Omitted variable:** XMUM first-year foreign-student hostel requirement, per-person alternatives, bay rights, travel/parking and work shifts.
4. **Structural change:** Announced2027 rail fare adjustment changes travel costs; actual employer rosters and accommodation capacity could change, but no rent effect is established.

**Falsifiable Hypothesis:** An original450/452sf whole dwelling can sustain1200 ordinary rent from a specifically eligible local/later-year tenant pool, without first-year foreign-student allocation or short-stay income.

**Evidence Required:** Approved original plan/title, current net ordinary offer, inventory/bays, comparable signed long leases, tenant eligibility and actual door-to-work route/cost; ordinary size-matched trade adjustments.

**Falsification Conditions:** Most inquiries require excluded hostel eligibility, unlawful partition or short stays; condition-matched ordinary rent is lower or voids longer; selected low offer is unavailable/restricted; total travel cost removes its advantage.

**Core Module Routing:** Buyer Universe; Asset Fit; Comparable Set; Financing Risk; Exit Liquidity; Price Discovery.

**Current Status:** Partially Resolved. Original small-unit family and institutional rule found; selected income/value still unresolved.

**Observed Evidence:** Aman1 has an original870sf2+1 family and a338k non-Bumi claim, but base rent1500 does not meet standard coverage; third room is marketed as a bedroom.

**Contradicting Judgment / Observation:** A useful family product in a desirable township could have resident appeal even when its current investor yield is weak.

**Possible Explanations:**

## D02-03 — smaller Elmina ticket versus component and service-cost burden

⚠ DIVERGENCE FLAG

**Observed Evidence:** Habitus565sf360k studio is cheaper than some larger choices, yet its350 maintenance claim and1300 rent produce substantial shortfall.

**Contradicting Judgment / Observation:** An original compact unit might be the budget-efficient way to access township amenities, but this could be office-use confusion or weak rental economics.

**Possible Explanations:**

1. **Data limitation:** Portal451/565/685size and room labels differ;350fee inclusions not billed;1300rental index older,1200two-room label mismatched.
2. **Judgment bias:** Choosing a low cost proxy to recover a preferred yield; treating a residential advert as legal-use evidence; assuming a branded township ensures apartment desirability.
3. **Omitted variable:** Component/title use, actual sinking/management obligations, usable plus rooms, legal alterations, bay access and competition from larger rentals.
4. **Structural change:** Kanopi changes the future apartment choice set. Its marketed prices/cohorts and2027/2028dates are unresolved; topping-off does not generate current rent.

**Falsifiable Hypothesis:** Any proposed Habitus compact advantage survives authenticated residential use, original layout and complete fee costs, while achieving condition-matched rent sufficient for its funded holding plan.

**Evidence Required:** Approved plan/title/use, actual management and sinking bills, fit-out inventory, current ordinary leases and selected net terms; Kanopi SPA/incentive/delivery reconciliation separately.

**Falsification Conditions:** Office-only/incompatible use;350orhigher recurring cost confirmed without adequate rent; no material rent premium over larger substitutes; later supply competes at the same budget. Even1400rent/200fee branch remains negative.

**Core Module Routing:** Asset Fit; Financing Risk; Supply Structure; Comparable Set; Lifecycle; Externality Ceiling.

**Current Status:** Unresolved. Fees better specified but not authenticated; no automatic small-unit preference.

## D02-04 — low GAIA headline prices versus ordinary resale and exit needs

⚠ DIVERGENCE FLAG

**Observed Evidence:** Historical250k developer pricing,202kreserves,380kcompact ask and500k750sf ask coexist; some646sf transfers are near480k.

**Contradicting Judgment / Observation:** The price spread could look like hidden mispricing or project deterioration, but the compared rights, sizes and sale mechanisms differ.

**Possible Explanations:**

1. **Data limitation:** Historic brochure versus current page; affordable50unitcohort; mixed sizes; batch transfers; reserve not realised outcome.
2. **Judgment bias:** Anchoring to250k launch floor or480kmixedtransfers; treating every low auction as a market crash; using amenities to justify a predetermined500kentry.
3. **Omitted variable:** Eligibility, consent/lease, ordinary versus distressed seller, fit-out/bays, actual2+1utility, landlord competition and nearby landed households.
4. **Structural change:** Township amenities and new supply can change use and recognition. No observed ordinary rent/transaction velocity yet validates a premium.

**Falsifiable Hypothesis:** After separating affordable/auction cohorts and normalising750sf rights/condition, ordinary resale supports the chosen entry and a future buyer can fund an exit consistent with sustainable income or evidenced resident utility.

**Evidence Required:** Recent normalised ordinary750sf transfers, effective incentives, leases/fees, selected title/bays and household-substitute evidence.

**Falsification Conditions:** Normalised ordinary prices remain materially below asking;2400upperrent requires unavailable bays/cost and still negative carry; investor-required yield or landed alternatives cap exits below funded break-even.

**Core Module Routing:** Comparable Set; Buyer Universe; Price Discovery; Mispricing; Exit Buyer; Financing Risk; Supply Structure.

**Current Status:** Partially Resolved. Historical price cohort separated; selected ordinary value and exit demand unresolved.

## D02-05 — future Kanopi amenity/launch strength versus unearned rent and timing

⚠ DIVERGENCE FLAG

**Observed Evidence:** Developer marketing and reported take-up are strong; current590888headline,675888brochureminimum,449launchcohort and499scheme count coexist. Press targetQ32027differsfrombrochureNovember2028.

**Contradicting Judgment / Observation:** A delivered integrated high-rise could attract residents; equating take-up or topping-off with cash income would understate timing and incentive risk.

**Possible Explanations:**

1. **Data limitation:** Different publication dates, cohorts, gross/net prices and milestones; no selected SPA, actual CCC or achieved rent.
2. **Judgment bias:** Treating launch popularity as rental absorption; prematurely labelling legitimate legal/marketing differences as delay or misrepresentation.
3. **Omitted variable:** SAMM eligibility, Bumi/package conditions, progressive payments/interest, fit-out timing, actual school/employment access and competing delivered stock.
4. **Structural change:** First high-rise delivery changes the area's product mix; it may deepen demand or expand tenant competition. Both are hypotheses.

**Falsifiable Hypothesis:** After price/contract reconciliation, actual legal delivery and a costed tenancy launch produce sustainable rent meeting the selected funded underwriting path.

**Evidence Required:** Selected net schedule/incentives, SPA milestone, progressive finance path, delivery evidence and matched post-delivery leases; estimate extra carry for later delivery.

**Falsification Conditions:** Stabilised ordinary rent remains below the3191headline-price annual-neutral requirement under stated proxies; incentives unavailable; financing/fit-out/delivery carry exhausts reserve; competing supply weakens absorption.

**Core Module Routing:** Lifecycle; Catalyst; Structural Change; Comparable Set; Financing Risk; Supply Structure; Exit Liquidity.

**Current Status:** Unresolved, forward control only. No operating rent forecast or current income case.

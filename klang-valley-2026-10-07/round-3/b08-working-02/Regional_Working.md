Domestic students, later-year foreign students, staff, airport/local workers and small families remain separate hypotheses. Compare the tenant's complete monthly housing-plus-travel budget and actual shift; do not assume20 less rent automatically. W02-E13–E15.

**Underwriting consequence.** R355's monthly surplus28 becomes annual deficit2667 under eleven paid months and recurring allowances. R359's ordinary-family base has annual deficit5374. **Strongest counter-thesis.** Apparent cheapness may be mainly package/identity distortion plus overstated accessible demand. Conversely, excluding all compact units because some larger units are dual-key would discard a real original small-unit family. Investigation must protect against both errors.

## Functional access and externalities

The existing MRT 3 official release in packet 01 concerns approval/land acquisition, not passenger opening. Keep current LRT/MRT/KTM access, proposed stations and ECRL timing separate. An operational line does not verify each building's safe pedestrian route or door-to-door advantage. No numerical station-distance adjustment is used to manufacture comparability.

Hospital, mall, university and employment-node references are demand leads. Rent transmission needs eligible occupants, competitive budgets, shift/commute timing and actual retained tenants. Household amenities may improve use while retail noise, access conflicts, lifts, privacy or fees cap project resale value. These are inspection hypotheses; no listing photo/video or physical site observation was claimed.

No parcel-level hazard conclusion is drawn from the words Ukay/hillside, Selayang/flood-prone, or an aggregator's crime label. Exact drainage/slope/insurance/maintenance evidence is still required. Unknown risk is not a favourable score.

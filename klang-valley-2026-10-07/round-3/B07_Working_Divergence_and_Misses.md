## D07-01 — Cheap compact expression versus unresolved rights/value

⚠ DIVERGENCE FLAG

**Observed evidence:** R340's230k/1350 scenario appears stronger than many family alternatives; the same sale says290k in its body, and the rental is487sf rather than459sf (E06/E07). Historical primary institutional/designer material raises a SOFO/component question (E29/E43).

**Contradicting judgment / observation:** An apparent low entry may not buy the residential rights or rental specification assumed. Both hidden mispricing and hidden structural/finance risk remain possible.

**Possible explanations investigated:** A—data limitation: header/body and area conflicts obtained, not resolved. B—judgment bias: choosing230k because it improves yield was challenged by calculating290k. C—omitted variable: exact title/use, financing and services unresolved; historical SOFO references are insufficient to decide legality. D—structural change: no evidence yet of changed approved use, bank acceptance or normalisation; no uplift credited.

**Falsifiable hypothesis:** After exact component/price/area reconciliation, the apparent cheapness will materially shrink or fail to transfer to an ordinary financeable whole-unit lease.

**Evidence required:** Exact signed price terms and parcel plan/title, lender valuation/use acceptance, independent matching lease/collections and current building charges. None obtained. If a verified230k transaction buys the same lawful/financeable residential use and comparable rent, the data-error explanation weakens and the existing Mispricing mechanism can be investigated.

**Falsification conditions:** Same-rights230k clearing and durable matching lease evidence with no omitted cost penalty; conversely290k or different use disproves the cheap residential scenario.

**Core routing:** G0 Comparable Set/Price Discovery; G4 Asset Fit/use; G5 Mispricing; G7 Financing Risk; G6 Exit Buyer.

**Current status:** Unresolved; R340 and component-uncertain R341 excluded from eligible ranking. More margin of safety cannot replace identity proof.

**Routing:** Governance/Asset Fit, Externality Ceiling if evidenced, Buyer Universe, Financing Risk, Exit Liquidity.

**Status:** Unresolved. Higher proof and margin-of-safety requirements; conditional research priority only, no automatic age-based Reject.

⚠ DIVERGENCE FLAG

**Observed evidence:** Prasarana confirms Shah Alam Line began operations2026; opening-day travel included promotional free rides (E36/E45). Impiria's600k/3000 scenario just reaches6% gross.

**Contradicting judgment / observation:** Operational rail may change actual housing choices, yet nearby ads still differ widely and a3000 furnished lease may not be durable. Continuing to call the line merely a future catalyst would also be wrong.

**A–D work:** A—train and feeder counts and promotional timing separated; no sustained paid commuting/household panel obtained. B—challenged both a rail-premium narrative and an outdated future-only narrative. C—door-to-station route, transfers, employment location, bay/furnishing differences unmeasured. D—network operation is a genuine dated change; rent and resale response remain unproven.

**Falsifiable hypothesis:** For the defined unit and tenant work route, delivered service reduces travel burden enough to improve ordinary lease absorption without an offsetting supply/cost increase.

**Required evidence:** Actual route/travel comparison, repeat matched leases/letting periods and competing-unit availability after the free period. No field route or achieved-lease series obtained.

**Falsifiers:** No improvement in paid-tenant absorption or replacement rents; inferior real last-mile route; increased competing supply absorbs the benefit.2100 PF and2400 whole-unit branches must remain in downside analysis.

**Routing:** Catalyst/Structural Change, Micro-location/Asset Fit, Supply Structure, Price Discovery, G6 exit.

**Status:** Partially resolved: operational status updated; financial transmission unresolved. No base rent growth.

## D07-05 — University anchor versus competing accommodation system

⚠ DIVERGENCE FLAG

**Observed evidence:** HELP/UOW/Sunway have identified accommodation alternatives, including provider houses/apartments, rooms and shuttle options (E30/E31/E34). Private whole-unit ads can imply higher rents than the retained cost model supports.

**Contradicting judgment / observation:** Proximity to a university is plausibly valuable, but an anchor alone does not identify which unit/landlord captures demand.

**A–D work:** A—historical2025 rates and inconsistent Nadayu rent/phase labels separated from current leases. B—challenged familiarity and campus-brand bias; four-room900 multiplication excluded. C—bundled services, shuttle, lease duration, calendar, household privacy and room-management workload considered but not priced as proven benefits. D—new phases/institution changes may alter supply; exact CCC, capacity and take-up remain open.

**Falsifiable hypothesis:** An ordinary whole-unit offer has a distinct household task and all-in cost advantage that supports its assumed rent despite institutional competition.

**Required evidence:** Current comparable whole-unit leases, provider terms/availability and exact operating model. No room letting, conversion or guarantee is adopted automatically.

**Falsifiers:** The tenant primarily purchases a cheaper serviced bed; assumed rent requires uncosted conversion, restricted use or management services; or replacement rents/paid months fall below threshold.

**Routing:** Buyer Universe, Asset Fit, Supply Structure, Lifecycle, Financing Risk, Exit Buyer.

**Status:** Unresolved; Nadayu identity hold remains. Genuine evidence of a workable room-rental business would require a separate versioned operating case.

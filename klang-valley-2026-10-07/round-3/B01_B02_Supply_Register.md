## Interpretation

For a five-year investment, newer stock can constrain both rent and eventual resale even when the project's starting PSF is higher: launch financing, fresh fit-out and usable layout can matter more than PSF. Conversely, announced expensive stock does not automatically displace a genuinely useful low-quantum older home. Test household overlap and net monthly costs.

No aggregate absorption rate, supply-exhaustion thesis or catalyst appreciation forecast is supported here. Land and planning capacity beyond the listed schemes remain outside quantified inventory; absence from this table does not mean no future supply.

Public source errors found here affect execution: planned versus delivered Trion 2, evolving M Aspira unit counts, mixed-use aliases, different price bases and expired/placeholder permit details. None is a new causal Core mechanism.

# Consolidated market Red Team questionnaire

Prepared 13 October 2026 at main `6edb9fb1f18190799ee03d11ec3f775682aa54c5`. This is the single current questionnaire for the completed bounded Klang Valley desk programme: 61 catchments, 122 project groups and 138 operating expressions. It supersedes the conversational three-question prompt by incorporating those follow-ups as RT-01, RT-04 and RT-05.

**User sequencing hold:** do not start (1) exact-unit evidence admission, (2) final G0-G9/financial/portfolio review, or (3) the final investment decision closeout until the user has answered this questionnaire, the current interpretation issues have been reconciled, and the user explicitly confirms that the review is closed and the three stages may begin. Replies alone do not automatically release the hold. This questionnaire is preparation only.

## How to answer and close the review

There are **24 consolidated questions**. They request market judgment, mechanisms, boundaries and counterexamples. A short answer by number is sufficient; explain the mechanism and a counterexample where useful. "No additional insight", "unfamiliar" or "uncertain" are valid responses and must not be converted into factual support or agreement.

The analyst owns source checks, routine facts, calculations and evidence collection. Current invoices, approved plans/title/use, paid rent, lease clauses, actual works, legal eligibility, lender terms and access measurements are not questions for the Senior Market Red Team to certify from memory. Personal capital commitments are relevant only if an actual allocation mandate is later requested; the synthetic budget does not become a user requirement.

Existing 90% purchase-price LTV / 4% / 35-year comparison, preferred 6% gross, layout-neutral eligibility, valid investor-led exits, lowest research priority for current shortfalls and evidence-led transition flexibility are already settled. They are not reopened here. Five-year holds, eleven paid months, the 8% equity hurdle and synthetic reserves remain labelled model illustrations/assumptions, not newly approved universal rules or user finances.

After responses, record attribution, scope, preferred and rival explanation, discriminating evidence/falsifier, affected gate and decision consequence. Reconcile overlaps and contradictions in one consolidated response review. Ask only necessary unresolved interpretation follow-ups; do not trickle out repeats of already answered questions. Do not change Core, numerical assumptions, G0 stops or G9 decisions through opinion alone.

**Closure concerns known interpretation questions at this cutoff.** It cannot certify that no future issue will ever arise. A later material new fact must be logged as a specific new conflict; it is not permission to bypass evidence or silently reopen the old questionnaire. A response-review close does not mean a factual hold, Divergence Flag or investment gate has passed.

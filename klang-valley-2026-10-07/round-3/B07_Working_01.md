Seri Pinang's family rent also fails the base standard. Bukit Raja industrial investment does not establish household income or rental absorption.
- A53: Impiria's600k/3000 scenario reaches6% gross but remains negative after costs. A2100 partly furnished replacement ask and2400 branch are material counterexamples. The new rail line is operational; rent transmission is still unmeasured.

## Stress, future buyer and portfolio

There are275 evaluations:19 expressions across14 scenarios plus9 specific branches. Amortisation, principal conservation, independent interest-plus-cost basis and discounted-exit identities pass. Scenarios include5.5% interest,20% lower rent,20% higher fee,12months zero rent,30k major works,15% lower bank valuation,25-year term,12-month delayed exit and unquoted-cost buffers. Combined scenarios join higher rate,lower rent/higher fee,six paid months and30k works. See [the full schedules](B07_Working_Financial_Results.json).

153 of171 two-expression synthetic cash paths retain150k from500k starting cash under simultaneous first-year shocks. These are **conditional funding comparisons only**. They include quarantined expressions to expose financial burdens, not eligible portfolios. Concentrated campus, industrial-employment, studio, shared-rail and local-landlord risks are not made independent by buying across area labels. G8 remains held for actual resources, liabilities, bank approvals and ownership limits.

Future resale must serve a funded owner or investor. For investors, compare actual net income at a more demanding required yield; for owners, verify functional alternatives and financing at the required sale price. Required nominal/8%-hurdle exits in the full schedules are burdens, never forecasts. Neither a development brand nor a rail opening supplies a future buyer. A12-month delay adds costs; debt amortisation is counted only once.

Seven paired low/base/high rent grids contain63 cells. Their leaders concern cash coverage only. R339/R341 remain quarantined even when a grid favours them. No numerical ranking can resolve title, use, condition or exit demand.

The report separates these categories. Its overhang definition concerns completed units with CCC remaining unsold more than nine months after launch; it is not all vacant homes or all resale advertisements.

Analytical implication: potential supply competition is material, but state totals neither prove a particular project's oversupply nor measure its tenant absorption. Do not add project pipelines to these aggregates as if mutually exclusive. Do not convert unsold-under-construction counts into all incoming supply, or commercial statistical classification into an exact parcel's legal-use conclusion.

The Central Region report link timed out. No district figures from it are assumed. Property-stock and serviced-apartment index reports were located but not substantively analysed in this installment.

## Demand context

[DOSM household income 2024](https://www.dosm.gov.my/portal-main/release-content/household-income-survey-report--malaysia--states-2024) gives state-level household aggregates; [DOSM basic amenities 2024](https://www.dosm.gov.my/portal-main/release-content/basic-amenities-survey-report-malaysia-2024) gives household tenure patterns. Neither supplies project-level young-owner demand, standalone-studio ownership preference, tenant affordability or lender approval. No income-to-price target is fabricated from them.

At the project level, require a task-specific mechanism: a verifiable daily route, employer/campus/retail utility, comparable total occupancy cost, and a buyer who can fund the exit. An institution's presence is evidence of an anchor, not the size of its residual off-campus rental pool.

The previous goal turn made progress by correcting authoritative status, adding evidence/identity holds and recording the compact-buyer hypothesis. This turn adds primary supply evidence, contrasting expressions and regional synthesis. Neither is a verified wait or a completion claim.

## Evidence versus interpretation

Observed: advertisement fields, dates and source-specific discrepancies; public masked transfer rows; official accommodation/transport terms; historical issuer disclosures. Interpreted: likely household tasks, competing explanations, priority of evidence and conditions for rent/exit support. Assumed: model fees/costs, collection pattern, finance stresses and any higher/lower rent scenario. Authenticated selected-unit title, management accounts, bank terms, executed ordinary leases, physical inspection and realised investment outcomes are absent. These categories must not be collapsed.

## ⚠ DIVERGENCE FLAG — R353 positive income arithmetic versus conditional offer and historical-contract concerns

**Observed Evidence:** ordinary ask-based220k/1500 scenario produces8.18% gross and820 annual carry. W01-E16 advertises purchase-package conditions; E23 records historical GRR losses/provisions. E17 offers a future ordinary tenancy, not guaranteed income.

**Contradicting Judgment / Observation:** the tempting high-yield interpretation may ignore net consideration, rights/contract residue, furnishing and recurring-cost floors; an equally tempting stigma-based dismissal may incorrectly transfer2015 facts to every2026 private resale.

**Possible Explanations:**
1. Data limitation: one syndicated offer and one old transfer record cannot identify net price or current clearing value; future rent is an ask and condition differs.
2. Judgment bias: bargain/high-yield anchoring versus narrative attachment to old guarantee failure; either can preselect the answer.
3. Omitted variable: current obligations, approved letting rights, usable parking, lease quality, vacancy, collection, capex and management.
4. Structural change: a specific unit may now transact and let independently after earlier arrangements, or current competition/operations may have worsened. Neither change is assumed.

**Falsifiable Hypothesis:** an independent ordinary-letting parcel is available at an unconditional220k equivalent and can sustain approximately1500 with costs low enough to retain positive annual carry.

**Evidence Required:** effective offer reconciliation and title/contract chain; signed ordinary lease plus collection/void history for the correct block/condition; current recurring bill and funded works; lender value and same-rights transfers.

**Falsification Conditions:** net price near285k at unchanged rent; annual unmodelled recurring cost over820; achieved rent below about1425 under base proxies; continuing obligations or works/voids that consume the thin margin. These thresholds are conditional equations, not a claim that any one shock is already observed.

**Core Module Routing:** Price Discovery, Comparable Set, Asset Fit, Lifecycle, Financing Risk, Exit Buyer and Exit Liquidity.

**Current Status:** Unresolved. Highest evidence value among the four new cases, no positive capital conviction. Required safety margin increases; no bid until a defensible reference and funded downside exist. Historical GRR is neither income nor a perpetual reject rule.

**Contradicting Judgment / Observation:** a naive reading of an official kit could reject the compact cohort entirely; a naive reading of current advertisements could accept every room label or bay claim.

1. Data limitation: historical kit revision/scope differs; recorded versus marketed sizes vary; newer exact-size amounts are masked.
2. Judgment bias: over-trusting an official but old document, or anchoring on an attractive small-unit yield and assuming matching rights.
3. Omitted variable: original plan, component, approved alterations, bay allocation and furnishing/circulation differences.

**Falsifiable Hypothesis:** the selected550sf expression corresponds to an independently financeable original compact residential-use parcel; its apparent coverage is not created by an invalid subdivision or mismatched bay/furnishing package.

**Falsification Conditions:** no independent title/use suitable for the proposed lease; room labels depend on unapproved conversion; title/component or bays differ materially; fit-out/fees/rent erase the apparent income advantage.

**Core Module Routing:** Comparable Set, Asset Fit, Buyer Universe, Financing Risk and Price Discovery.

**Current Status:** Partially Resolved. A size-near recorded transfer supports cohort existence, but original plan/rights and effective valuation remain unresolved. G0 STOP stays; the mixed median does not become a valuation anchor.

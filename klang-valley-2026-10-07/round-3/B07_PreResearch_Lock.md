## Competing propositions locked before new evidence

1. Compact units may offer lower acquisition quantum and stronger rent-to-price ratios, but furnishing, fee floors, component financeability and tenant concentration can reverse that advantage.
2. Mature family units may serve more durable household tasks at lower PSF, but that does not establish an owner-occupier premium or sufficient rent after costs.
3. Institutions and employment nodes may support demand; purpose-built accommodation, travel friction and academic/shift timing can capture or weaken that demand.
4. New infrastructure may change functional accessibility. Announcements, testing, opening and reliable service are distinct evidence states; no automatic rent uplift enters a base case.
5. Lower-priced old or distressed stock may be mispriced, or may carry restrictions, operating liabilities or an unsuitable buyer universe. Auction reserves and labelled transfers require normalisation.
6. New launches and new completions can improve an area's utility while increasing rental and resale competition. Phase totals and nested masterplans must be separated.

## Arc: separate the asset from the historic guarantee

The original 220k sale remains conditional on advertised finance/cash/package terms. The 248k alternative is directly accessible and has no explicit cashback/GRR in the viewed body; that absence does not prove clean economics or legal release. Its older search snapshot differs from its current direct page, so a transaction price reduction is not inferred. [A-S1](https://www.propertyguru.com.my/property-listing/the-arc-for-sale-by-wl-wan-500813136) · [A-S2](https://www.iproperty.com.my/property/cyberjaya/the-arc/sale-107605826/).

A separate 913-sf furnished whole-unit offer specifies a minimum one-year tenancy and future November intake. It markets to students and families, which proves neither family adoption nor tenant concentration. Its furnishing and block cannot be transferred automatically to either sale. [A-R1](https://www.mudah.my/the-arc-cyberjaya-for-rent-intake-november-2026-115832880.htm).

The issuer's FY2015 annual report documents historical GRR losses/provisions and describes fixed-period leaseback obligations. It is a historical counterparty-risk source, not a present guarantee, current court result or encumbrance on every unit. [A-H1, PDF text pages 7 and 113](https://www.meridianberhad.com.my/_files/ugd/a1c5d4_bacbe73f4619473eabd4466764075b56.pdf).

The strongest favourable rival explanation is that a released, usable unit bought cheaply can support ordinary income despite a failed historic scheme. The adverse explanation is that low headline price is offset by package terms, inventory replacement, occupation/assignment limits, arrears or exit friction. Neither is resolved. No assumed GRR, cashback, free transfer costs or bank valuation enters returns.

Before any admission, reconcile the current parcel's sale/assignment and possession terms, any option/lease/operator contract, expiry/termination/release, amounts payable and transferrable rights. Do not presume a buyer inherits either a benefit or a liability. Current management accounts, charge statement, major works and actual bank terms are separate. This is an evidence task, not a legal finding.

At 3,400 rent the combined fee could be at most about 496/month for annual neutrality; at 3,000 rent only 129/month. A cheaper fee alone does not rescue the lower-rent scenario.

These are breakpoints under stated assumptions, not fair values, offers, permanent filters or safety margins sufficient for purchase. Current simplified monthly shortfall retains lowest research priority rather than structural Reject.

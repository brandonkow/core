## PAL-P01 — Parkview ordinary-income hypothesis

Hypothesis: an original exclusive compact can repeatedly collect ordinary household rent sufficient for annual carry, independently of tourism or room aggregation. The current model needs about RM2,339/month across eleven paid months at the inherited price/costs.

Expected confirmation: a parcel-matched approved plan, defined private facilities and bays, an ordinary annual contract with itemised utilities/services, receipts and renewal/replacement history consistent with the required net income. Confirm current fees and works separately.

Falsifiers: matched ordinary collections persistently below the full-cost threshold; RM2,400 is available only with services/short terms or higher fit-out costs; approved configuration or practical use fails the supposed household task; recurring costs absorb the surplus. A different small unit's lower ask is not by itself falsification.

Four-family investigation:
- Data limitation: header/body sizes and rents disagree; old index/redirects; multiple listings may be one parcel; advertised term is missing or generic.
- Judgment bias: anchoring to prime location, freehold marketing or the initially positive model; alternatively assuming a serviced apartment can only earn tourist income.
- Omitted variable: bay rental, furnishing, utilities, lift/service quality, noise, visitor circulation, real private space and exact lease term.
- Structural change: verifiable access, rules, tenant-source or works changes could affect collections; none is established here.

Strongest counterexamples retained: older higher annual-term asking control; lower near-size deposit-based leasing lead; one extra void month reverses annual carry. These test the proposition without averaging unlike units.

Status: **Unresolved**. No stand-alone Evidence–Judgment flag is fabricated from missing evidence. Ordinary tenancy advertising and short-stay presence can coexist. Route to G0/G2/G3/G4/G6/G7; 5.26 Dynamic Comparable Mapping, 5.15 Operational Livability and 5.34 Usage Demand.

## PAL-A01 — Arc after the historical GRR

Hypothesis: a released, usable, properly furnished parcel acquired at a sufficiently low effective price can produce viable ordinary whole-unit income without guarantee payments.

Expected confirmation: current net consideration and all incentives/costs, title/assignment/possession and any surviving lease-option restrictions, a unit inventory, actual cost statements and ordinary collected leases. A future investor must be able to acquire the same usable rights and finance the price.

Falsifiers: genuine all-in terms exceed the thesis's tolerable price/costs; operator/occupation obligations materially prevent the proposed use; ordinary matched income is inadequate; works/arrears/credit requirements defeat funded carry or exit. Current shortfall alone does not establish a permanent structural failure.

Four-family investigation:
- Data limitation: old GRR copy, stale alternate asks, partly furnished sale versus furnished rent, unspecified net offer and contract cohort.
- Judgment bias: attraction to an 8%-plus gross calculation; opposite bias of rejecting every parcel because an old scheme had problems.
- Omitted variable: contract release/assignment, deposit liabilities, exclusive possession, inventory replacement, common-property charges, tenant concentration and next-buyer finance.
- Structural change: documented termination/release, improved governance or ordinary renting could alter the operating object; none is assumed from a portal advert.

The refreshed ordinary annual-term offer is a counterexample to treating every Arc income lead as GRR. It is not proof that R353 is legally and operationally interchangeable with that advertised rental. The 248k alternative tests higher entry without requiring a universal family-owner exit.

Status: **Unresolved**. No Evidence–Judgment flag merely because historical counterparty trouble and a current conditional surplus coexist; they are different propositions. Route to G0/G1/G3/G4/G6/G7, financing risk, 5.25 Governance and 5.29 Thesis–Expression. Do not import historical legal conclusions into an unseen current contract.

## ⚠ DIVERGENCE FLAG — PAL-D01: Lucentia charge interpretation

Affected G0–G9 Stage(s): G0, G3, G4, G7, G8, G9; any G5 assessment remains blocked.

Observed Evidence:
The selected sale gives RM1,165 maintenance without a period. The preserved monthly branch yields approximately -RM8,032 annual carry. Separately, older broker marketing publishes a markedly different per-square-foot fee scale. The ledger distinguishes their dates and authority.

Contradicting Judgment / Observation:
The analyst provisionally suspects the monthly interpretation may overstate recurring charges. This is an analyst hypothesis informed by an inconsistent published scale, not a user-supplied judgment or an authenticated correction. The advert never specified monthly; actual charges remain UNKNOWN.

Materiality:
A quarterly interpretation changes annual cash by RM9,320 at unchanged rent and changes the research-priority/coverage view. An incorrect favourable correction could conceal structural cost or governance deterioration. Both potential hidden mispricing and hidden risk must remain open.

Possible Explanations:
1. **Data limitation:** missing charge period, decimal/field transcription, bill balance rather than recurring rate, wrong parcel or area basis, stale marketing.
2. **Judgment bias:** desire to rescue an appealing central asset; anchoring to an old low fee; or excessive confidence in the conservative monthly branch.
3. **Omitted variable:** sinking allocation, parking/share-unit basis, utilities, arrears, insurance, special levy, operator services or other parcel-specific charges.
4. **Structural change:** genuine adopted rate increase, newly funded repairs, changes in services or collection shortfalls. No current governance change is established.

Falsifiable Hypothesis:
RM1,165 is not the selected parcel's recurring monthly service-plus-sinking charge; normalised current recurring charges are lower after separating period and one-off items.

Evidence Required:
A current dated invoice and account statement for the same parcel, charge period and itemisation, share-unit/bay basis, rate resolution/effective date, payment history and pending levies. Reconcile to the sale inventory and current lease package.

Falsification Conditions:
If current matching documents establish recurring monthly RM1,165, the lower-fee hypothesis fails. If lower regular charges are established but additional mandatory expenses restore the same burden, the favourable net-income thesis fails. If a quarterly invoice is proven, that resolves period only; it does not validate market value or ordinary rent.

Core Module Routing:
5.30 Reference-Price Validity, 5.26 Dynamic Comparable Mapping, 5.25 Management / Governance, G3/G4 cost/asset fit, G7 terminal risk, G8 opportunity cost and G9 decision. No new gate or causal promotion.

Current Status:
**Unresolved.** Preserve monthly, quarterly and alternative-rate branches. Require more evidence and greater margin of safety, reduce conviction and retain G0 STOP / G9 Defer. No arbitrary percentage discount is invented to replace the missing bill.

Investment Implication:
Possible input overstatement could improve carry if verified. Possible unrecorded cost escalation could instead signal ongoing deterioration. Even the favourable fee branches fail annual carry at the inherited RM3,000 rent downside. Neither interpretation independently justifies purchase.

## Pre-search scope and falsifiable proposition

The execution design was recorded in session RAM on 11 October before the new searches; it is reproduced in Financial_Inputs.json. It was not separately committed as a signed preregistration. Baseline main was 904574c38b6ecb57ef320f110d3b01bafe85951d. The previously published hypothesis remains the durable pre-investigation call.

Question: does a defined cohort's willingness to rent exclusive compact accommodation improve Eve's collected net income, letting time or retention enough to compensate for effective price, operating costs and exit burden? Maxim is a capital-allocation challenger. Local whole-unit and shared alternatives test the mechanism; the cross-region pair alone cannot identify a causal privacy effect.

Expected support: repeatable package-matched collections or retention/absorption advantage; measured exclusivity; viable ordinary household tenancy; costs and exit terms that preserve the benefit.

Falsification: the premium disappears after furnishing, services, parking and tenant-task matching; longer voids or concessions consume it; renewal/choice evidence favours alternatives at comparable budgets; or incremental capital and maintenance costs remove the investment advantage. Missing records are inconclusive, not adverse outcome evidence. No assumed rent growth or exit-yield compression was allowed.

Stopping rule: one bounded public-source pass plus price/rent/cost/exit sensitivity. If leases, payments, governance and matched net transfers cannot be authenticated through this access, retain Defer and stop expanding portal counts. That point has been reached.

## Divergence handling

No full DIVERGENCE FLAG is activated in this packet because no authenticated material same-proposition contradiction has been established. A higher unmatched Maxim asking rent does not contradict a claim about sustained net collections; a shared Ara offer does not measure the region's dominant preference. Simplified monthly coverage and annual cash are different definitions, not contradictory data.

All influential judgment has nevertheless been expressed as a falsifiable hypothesis and investigated through all four families. MJ-P01 remains Unresolved. If a comparable collected-rent/retention panel later contradicts the proposed advantage, the master's full flag must identify the observations, contradicting judgment, explanations, falsifier, missing evidence, existing routing and status. Neither favourable anecdotes nor personal confidence close it.

Uncertainty keeps conviction low and requires stronger evidence and margin of safety. No automatic discount percentage, universal studio bonus, region stereotype, owner-buyer requirement or extra gate is introduced.

## Misses and corrections recorded without changing history

1. **The prior under-RM2 annual difference already existed.** This replay confirms it; it is not a newly discovered investment edge. A strong rank claim from that arithmetic would be unsupported.
2. **Privacy premium remains unmeasured.** Public research did not produce achieved leases, collections, matched cohorts, time-to-let or renewals. The central market hypothesis has not passed validation.
3. **Marketplace classification is unreliable for this purpose.** Eve full-package offers may have room headers. Body interpretation is still not proof of the approved plan or actual occupancy. No portal median or listing count is promoted.
4. **The higher-rent challenger is mismatched.** Maxim's RM2,500 offer has two bays versus one in the RM380k sale. The sensitivity must not become a 7.89% executable-yield claim.
5. **The within-project control also has a mismatch.** Eve's RM565k larger-unit sale advertises one bay and the RM2,800 rent two. Its illustrative gross yield is directional only. No garage-value adjustment was invented.
6. **Lower asking price is an offer lead, not market evidence of mispricing.** Eve RM370k remains subject to effective consideration, availability, bay rights and condition. It is not a closed transfer or a change to the historical base.
7. **Transaction evidence was not fully accessed.** Brickz direct pages returned 403; indexed provider aggregates remain low-resolution context. Different time windows, sizes and stock counts prevent a liquidity comparison. No paid access or bypass.
8. **Dates are not absorption data.** Direct Mudah pages show day/month without year; index context suggests 2026 but is not independent authentication. Refreshes and delistings cannot establish vacancy length or letting.
9. **Scenario-wrapper check caught a label-selection error.** An initial suffix filter accidentally selected an explicitly non-base sensitivity as a base. The baseline cash assertion failed; selection was corrected to the two exact base labels and the full 90-run calculation rerun. The frozen engine and financial assumptions were not changed.
10. **Public desk completion is not empirical validation.** No actual tenant sample, project inspection, governance audit or funded resale was observed. File counts and arithmetic checks cannot substitute for those outcomes.

## Consequences and next finite evidence

Obtain, if available through authorised evidence access, the exact offer/plan and bay rights; matched ordinary lease plus collections/concessions/paid months; actual fee and works records; and normalised net resales with lender acceptance. Any panel must report independent units, period and denominator and preserve contradictory examples. One favourable unit does not establish a regional tendency. No new empirical promotion threshold is invented here.

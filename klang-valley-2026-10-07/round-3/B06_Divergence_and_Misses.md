# B06 divergence investigations and misses

Cutoff: 7 October 2026. This is a cross-gate investigation control, not G10 or a new market causal module. Divergence triggers investigation and cannot justify overriding evidence. Differences between simplified coverage and net cashflow are reconciled calculations, not automatically flags.

## ⚠ DIVERGENCE FLAG — Metroview discount versus reference reliability

**Observed Evidence:** A RM 350 k/1, 150-sf sale advertisement and a third-party RM 500 k/1, 152-sf recorded sale, with several similarly high records (F29). A separate landed Metroview series also surfaces (F30).

**Contradicting Judgment / Observation:** Similar size suggests a large discount, but metadata, form and consideration are insufficient to establish comparability. This skepticism is an interpretation, not observed property inferiority.

**Possible Explanations:**
1. **A — Data limitation:** mixed forms, duplicate or lagged records, incentives, or bad geocoding.
2. **B — Judgment bias:** anchoring on RM 500 k or preferring the bargain story.
3. **C — Omitted variable:** renovation, bays, condition, restrictions or unusual seller circumstances.
4. **D — Structural change:** new competing supply or altered local demand may make historical prices obsolete.

**Falsifiable Hypothesis:** The lower ask represents a seller-specific discount within an otherwise comparable ordinary resale cohort.

**Evidence Required:** Exact parcel and offer, independent normalised sale records, condition and bays, financing terms and credible current bids.

**Falsification Conditions:** Higher records concern different forms or effective consideration; matched bids clear near RM 350 k; major works or restricted exit explain the gap.

**Core Module Routing:** Comparable Set / Price Discovery → G0; Mispricing → G1/G5; Asset Fit / Exit Buyer → G4/G6; Financing → G7.

**Current Status:** Unresolved. No 30% discount accepted. Require more proof and margin of safety without inventing an adequate discount.

## ⚠ DIVERGENCE FLAG — Urban 360 yield versus executable economics

**Observed Evidence:** A RM 220 k studio header combined with the RM 1, 450 rent scenario can produce positive annual carry; the same advertisement body says RM 270 k (F14). Another RM 220 k partial-unit advertisement contains generic financing promotions (F16).

**Contradicting Judgment / Observation:** The attractive yield may reflect a price-reference error. The separate RM 1, 200 rental counterexample can eliminate monthly coverage.

**Possible Explanations:**
1. **A — Data limitation:** stale header/body, condition mixing, or net versus gross consideration.
2. **B — Judgment bias:** bargain anchoring and selecting the highest rental comparable.
3. **C — Omitted variable:** component, parking, furnishing, tenant budget or actual fee differences.
4. **D — Structural change:** genuine repricing, management improvement or temporary selling pressure may be missing from lagged records.

**Falsifiable Hypothesis:** A correctly identified ordinary 511-sf parcel is available at RM 220 k effectively and sustains RM 1, 450 whole-unit rent after costs.

**Evidence Required:** Written price reconciliation, approved use/title, ordinary lease and collections, current invoices, condition and bank valuation.

**Falsification Conditions:** RM 270 k or another effective price governs; higher rent requires an unfunded upgrade; permitted use or financing fails; achieved rents cluster near RM 1, 200; a fee reset consumes the buffer.

**Core Module Routing:** Price Discovery / Comparable Set → G0/G5; Buyer Universe / Asset Fit → G2/G4; Exit Liquidity / Financing → G6/G7.

**Current Status:** Unresolved. Both price branches remain diagnostics. Neither personal confidence nor choosing the lower number resolves the conflict.

## ⚠ DIVERGENCE FLAG —168 Park two-bedroom yield versus product fit

**Observed Evidence:** The RM 265 k/624-sf advertisement markets two bedrooms and discloses an owner-added master-room partition (F07). A separate 624-sf advertisement says one room (F35).

**Contradicting Judgment / Observation:** Attractive compact economics may not translate into original two-bedroom buyer demand, financing or resale. No unapproved or unsafe work is asserted.

**Possible Explanations:**
1. **A — Data limitation:** bedroom taxonomy or phase/plan mismatch.
2. **B — Judgment bias:** preferring new facilities or low entry price while underweighting usable privacy and fees.
3. **C — Omitted variable:** ventilation, layout, bays, approved use, management and lender acceptance.
4. **D — Structural change:** post-handover normalisation or revival could improve recognition; new phases could instead weaken resale.

**Falsifiable Hypothesis:** The actual approved configuration and alteration are acceptable to ordinary tenants, lenders and subsequent buyers at the underwritten rent and exit quantum.

**Evidence Required:** Approved and as-built plans, alteration authority, block CCC, inspection, valuation, comparable altered/unmodified leases and sales, current MC costs.

**Falsification Conditions:** Lost room utility or financeability, costly rectification, tenant discounts against true substitutes, or persistent inventory after stabilisation.

**Core Module Routing:** Asset Fit / Owner-Occupier Desirability → G3/G4; Comparable Set → G0; Buyer Universe / Exit Liquidity → G2/G6; Lifecycle / Financing → G1/G7.

**Current Status:** Partially Resolved on classification: an alteration is disclosed. Economic and structural implications remain Unresolved. No original two-bedroom ranking is granted.

## ⚠ DIVERGENCE FLAG — Kwasa ecosystem versus missing rent transmission

**Observed Evidence:** An identifiable primary Tujuh project, announced Kwasa schemes and existing regional rail. Tujuh's page contains conflicting product, price and date versions (F18); no verified occupied rental cohort is available for these controls.

**Contradicting Judgment / Observation:** A plausible neighbourhood upgrade can coexist with inadequate current rent proof and competing future delivery. Neither weak historical evidence nor a masterplan narrative settles forward value.

**Possible Explanations:**
1. **A — Data limitation:** outdated versions, unmatched starting prices or missing incentives.
2. **B — Judgment bias:** brand/TOD attachment, or excessive skepticism based only on historical conditions.
3. **C — Omitted variable:** first-mile access, job mix, operating retail, supply composition and financing.
4. **D — Structural change:** actual job/amenity completion and new residents, or construction and absorption delays.

**Falsifiable Hypothesis:** Delivered employment, services and usable access generate ordinary tenant demand fast enough to cross rent hurdles after handover without persistent concessions.

**Evidence Required:** Current project, offer and draw documents; operational milestones; eligible tenants; achieved leases and incentives; occupancy and competing completions.

**Falsification Conditions:** Delayed amenities or occupation, net rents below hurdles, continuous incentives, growing competing inventory or a funding gap before stabilisation.

**Core Module Routing:** Structural Change / Catalyst / Lifecycle → G1; Supply Structure / Buyer Universe → G2; Price Discovery → G0/G5; Exit Buyer / Terminal Risk → G6/G7.

**Current Status:** Unresolved. Thresholds only; more proof and funded carry required. No rent growth invented.

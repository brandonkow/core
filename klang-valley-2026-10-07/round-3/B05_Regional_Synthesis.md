## What changed the assessment

A prime employment district can support strong rent in absolute terms while failing ordinary income underwriting at its entry price. The selected TRX two-bedroom illustrates this: 1950k entry and7500rent produce only4.62%gross and about22.7knegative annual carry. That is a conditional income assessment, not a forecast that TRX prices must fall.

Smaller is not automatically better. Parkview's400k/2400scenario earns7.20%gross but only about672annual carry after the stated vacancy/cost provisions. Titiwangsa's500k/2700family scenario earns6.48%gross but has about810negative annual carry. The lower280k28Boulevard studio offer changes the earlier380kscenario materially, yet still has about400negative annual carry with the stated1650rent and allowances. None supports a quality/exit conclusion from yield alone.

The most decision-sensitive findings concern evidence identity and costs: Lucentia's unperiodised1165maintenance figure; the28Boulevard SOHO/service-apartment distinction; Titiwangsa's undisclosed current tenancy; Parkview's600/615-sf and studio/one-bedroom mismatch; and Seri Maya's older530koffer versus a newer650kexpression with different bays. Resolving these has more value than attaching confidence to an area label.

## A34 - KLCC and Ampang Park: central access versus purchase quantum

The functional split is between smaller households wanting central accommodation, ordinary work-linked tenants, and larger households valuing Ampang Park/embassy/amenity routines. Tourism operation is a separate business with different costs, rules and seasonality. It is not the fallback income for weak annual leases. The assumed owner-use channel for a compact unit remains unproven; income-led resale is admissible if net income, rights and lender acceptance survive.

Parkview R321 tests mature low-quantum space against Vortex R220's larger two-bedroom/study expression. At400k/2400, Parkview has the stronger conditional income arithmetic. At1900rent it loses that advantage: a high current ask is not an achieved ordinary lease. Repeated syndicated listings add no tenancy depth. Ten heterogeneous public transfers do not establish a615-sf clearing value or fast resale.

The counter-thesis is that central convenience and low quantum hide older-asset costs, visitor circulation, noise, parking limitations or a weaker annual tenant pool. A600/615-sf plan mismatch can also mean the assumed bedroom is not the product being sold. Hampshire and other Ampang Park family stock are lifecycle/product controls rather than financially matched cases; no whole-KLCC best project or family premium is established. The Corus-site landbank is a future competitive possibility, not delivered inventory.

**Disposition:** Parkview is a priority for identity, fee and ordinary-lease verification among the selected B05 compact expressions, not a cleared first choice. Vortex remains lower investment-research priority at the inherited shortfall scenario. Reopen on a matched plan/bay, effective live offer, charge/works evidence and ordinary lease; then adjust equivalent transfers.

## A36 - TRX: the second bedroom does not repair premium entry economics

TRX's employment, retail and rail functions can be useful. The question is how much that utility is already capitalised in purchase price, and which tenant budgets remain after competing towers offer similar convenience. Projected worker counts and retail footfall are not observed residential demand. Local and foreign household willingness and financing must be evidenced rather than assumed from district status.

R221474-sf one-bedroom and R323850-sf two-bedroom test the user's full layout flexibility. At the selected entries, neither reaches ordinary income coverage. The850-sf scenario needs about9568monthly rent for annual neutrality under the allowances, versus7500selected asking rent. Even the separate1860kprice branch retains approximately18.4knegative annual carry. Choosing a cheaper size can reduce capital at risk, but does not automatically turn the investment into a sound income asset.

A supposed550kTRX duplex was actively examined. Its header conflicts with a body describing another-sized duplex5kmfrom the centre. It is quarantined, not used as evidence of a huge district discount. The published850-sf transfer coded one room also needs plan and first-sale/resale normalisation. CORE Residence is a relevant named competitor; conflicting secondary handover dates remain unresolved. Lendlease's wider six-tower plan must not be counted as six completed towers.

**Disposition:** lowest ordinary-income investment-research priority at current inputs, while retaining an evidence-led transition option. Require feasible effective entry, matched fitted versus ordinary rent, charges and precise competing releases. No speculative future office-driven rent increase enters the base.

## Evidence priority, exit and capital

Within this selected set, the highest decision value is in resolving Parkview's plan/rent/fees, Titiwangsa's existing lease and live offer, 28Boulevard's component/use and lower price, and Lucentia's charge period. This is an evidence queue, not a capital-allocation ranking. Current-shortfall investments remain lowest priority unless a credible, dated change can remove the shortfall with funded carry.

All sixteen expressions retain G0 STOP and G9 Defer. No reliable normalised reference means no defensible numerical margin of safety yet. A personal belief in location quality cannot supply that missing reference. Investor exits remain permitted; test income/yield/credit contraction rather than inventing an owner buyer to rescue returns. The Parkview/Titiwangsa rent grid reverses their coverage lead, demonstrating why a confident overall winner is premature.

The120pair paths stress a common cash pool under simultaneous adverse terms;68retain the illustrative150kreserve. They include unverified/alternative parcels and are not an allocation proposal. Two properties exposed to the same tenant budgets, rates, vacancy and strata works are not diversified merely because their area names differ. No purchase is the explicit alternative.

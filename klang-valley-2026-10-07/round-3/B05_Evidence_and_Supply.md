# B05 evidence, comparable and supply ledger

Cutoff: 7 October 2026. Observations below are source claims, not inspected conditions, authenticated leases or verified net transactions. Interpretation is separated from those claims. Fifty raw web returns are retained losslessly as `b05-raw/*.json.gz`; read with gzip then JSON. Search failures and redirects are retained. No site inspection, paid record access, agent contact or title search occurred.

## Source treatment

Advertisements establish an advertised expression, not availability, consideration, rent achieved or quality. Index dates can differ from individual pages; a refreshed index does not refresh the underlying offer. Syndicated agent copy is one lead. Asking counts are neither distinct units nor absorption/exit velocity. Ordinary whole-unit leases are the income hypothesis; room-only, nightly and managed receipts are not interchangeable. Current title, remaining lease, use permission, charges and actual lending remain unverified. Inherited numeric inputs and source IDs remain in their original release; B05 adds overlays and new cases without rewriting them.

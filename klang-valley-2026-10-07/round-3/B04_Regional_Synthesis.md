# B04 regional underwriting: northwest and western KL/PJ

Cutoff: 7 October 2026. Scope: the nine canonical catchments A25-A33. This is a completed, bounded regional desk pass with capital decision **Defer** throughout. It is not a census of every development, an executable unit shortlist or completed underwriting of all Klang Valley. Completion is assessed in [the substantive audit](B04_Completion_Audit.md); the evidence and decision limitations below remain binding.

The most useful new result is that mature family apartments deserve the first ordinary-income verification work in this batch. At the stated asking-price/rent assumptions, Mont Kiara Pines and Windsor Tower produce better income coverage than the premium compact controls. This is a falsifiable research ordering, not proof of superior buildings, investment value or achievable returns. The ranking reverses under different matched rents. Neo and Cliveden retain potentially attractive compact economics but their offer/component evidence holds prevent an investable ranking.

The existing Core and G0-G9 remain unchanged. [Nineteen G0-G9 papers](B04_Cases_G0_G9.md), [financial underwriting](B04_Financial_Underwriting.md), [source and supply ledger](B04_Evidence_and_Supply.md) and [divergence/miss investigations](B04_Divergence_and_Misses.md) are integral to this report. Ten inherited expressions are retained with their original evidence dates; nine contrasting expressions are added. Downstream calculations continue diagnostically after a G0 stop and do not constitute gate passes.

## Decision comparison

All figures are RM and conditional on unverified rent, charges and costs. Standard coverage uses a 90% purchase-price loan, 4% interest, 35 years and the full instalment plus the stated management/sinking allowance. Annual carry also deducts other operating provisions and allows eleven collected months. It excludes initial acquisition/works and terminal costs, which enter the separate total-return model. Gross yield is assumed annual rent divided by asking price, not an observed investment return.

At the Pines assumptions, annual neutrality requires about RM3,807 rent and the five-year nominal recovery sale is about RM752,224. At Windsor, those amounts are about RM4,079 and RM791,595. The current gross-yield preference and simplified monthly pass therefore do not demonstrate complete-cost profitability. All nineteen expressions have a negative five-year profit at an unchanged resale price after modelled acquisition, works, operating and disposal costs. This result is conditional; it is neither a price forecast nor proof that all units in these projects fail.

Pines' RM700,000 sale and RM3,800 rental are indexed asks, with unverified fit-out equivalence. Windsor's RM750,000 sale has a more specific listing, but the RM4,000 rent is dated April. These differences are part of the research priority, not hidden inside a score. The [Pines source panel](https://www.propertyguru.com.my/property-for-sale/at-mont-kiara-pines-602), [Windsor sale](https://www.mudah.my/windsor-tower-2-jalan-sri-hartamas-1-taman-sri-hartamas-sri-hartamas-115178799.htm) and [Windsor rental lead](https://www.propertyguru.com.my/property-listing/windsor-tower-for-rent-by-ann-lim-501176696) do not authenticate collection or current negotiated terms.

Current standard shortfalls receive the lowest investment-research priority. They are not automatically permanent Rejects. Reopening requires an evidenced lower effective entry, sustainable net-rent change, cost change or a dated transition mechanism with funded carry. No generic rail, prestige or neighbourhood-upgrading uplift is included in base rent or exit price.

## A25: Bangsar South-Pantai-Kerinchi

South View's lower quantum is financially closer to the standard than the RM850,000 KL Gateway two-bedroom control: about +RM68 versus -RM547 per month. KL Gateway may offer a more useful second room, but this must be proved through the original plan, privacy and actual route; it cannot be inferred from the advertised bedroom label. Its RM3,200 assumed rent implies a RM640,000 price at 6% gross, which is a financial constraint, not a supported value or feasible purchase offer.

The family alternative was not ignored: Park Residences 1,260/1,485 sf rental leads and a separate 1,485 sf sale were screened, but timing and package differences prevent a paired yield. Goodwood serves a different household form. More materially, UOA's annual report places Laurel's 1,260 units as completed at end-FY2024. That is delivered competitive stock, not merely a future catalyst. It challenges a compact scarcity story without proving current vacant inventory. See ledger B4-E01/E02 and its dated supply entries.

The positive mechanism is convenient recurring use supporting ordinary rents. Its strongest rival is that many landlords supply functionally similar furnished accommodation while fees and repeated fit-out absorb the rent premium. Current evidence does not establish office-to-unit tenant capture, rent growth or buyer depth. Defer; prioritise exact South View plan/rent/cost matching over another generic locality search. The wider Pantai/Kerinchi affordability segment is not represented sufficiently to claim a best project for every budget.

## A26: TTDI

The compact test does not rescue the income case automatically. Ascencia at RM690,000/RM2,500 gives about 4.35% gross and -RM550 standard monthly carry; Sinaran at RM948,000/RM3,500 gives about 4.43% and -RM728. The lower total entry reduces absolute exposure while leaving the basic yield problem. Ascencia's studio/one-bedroom and package descriptions conflict, so an enclosed-bedroom premium is unproved. A separate RM668,000 lead is tested financially but is not a negotiated discount to the same unit.

Mas Kiara's 1,260 sf three-bedroom RM550,000 lead is a material affordability counterexample to treating these two projects as all TTDI. It lacks matched rent/cost evidence here. Sri TTDI's restricted-land label prevents its price from becoming an unrestricted comparable. Glomac's developer describes an 876-2,529 sf range and marketed MRT proximity; sold out does not establish occupation or pedestrian quality. No defensible comprehensive future-supply count was found, so scarcity is not awarded.

Established residential appeal could sustain prices without making current leveraged income attractive. Conversely, a matched lower effective entry or genuine rent improvement could reopen an investment case. Both expressions remain lowest-priority Defer on present coverage. Next evidence is plan/rights, ordinary leases, actual fees and adjusted effective transfers; landed is a household substitution test at the exit budget, not a universal apartment price cap.

## A27: Damansara Heights

DC's larger control tests the assumption that a second room necessarily improves investment economics. At RM1.8 million/RM5,400, the 1,152 sf expression gives 3.60% gross and roughly -RM2,573 per month on the standard. The 904 sf compact at RM1.135 million/RM4,000 is also below coverage, at about -RM1,173. The extra rent does not cover the modelled acquisition increment. The larger sale remains index-only with room/net-package ambiguity, so these are conditional diagnostics rather than executable offers.

Guoco's 370-residence mixed development supplies a credible product context. Its offices, hotel and prime address do not measure ordinary residential demand, owner willingness or operating quality. Different 1,194 sf and 904 sf offers cannot quietly price the 1,152 sf case. Developer incentives and resale terms need reconstruction before any discount can be called mispricing.

The strongest counter to rejecting the product is that users may rationally pay for prime-address utility. The strongest counter to investing is that a future financed buyer needs enough income and equity to support a high recovery price, while newer prime substitutes remain available. Do not use owner desirability as a substitute for rent or demonstrated exit depth. Defer at lowest current income priority. Other Damansara Heights apartments and landed options remain unmatched, preventing a district-wide investment verdict.

## A28: Mont Kiara

Pines is the main challenge to a compact-first search. Its RM700,000/RM3,800 three-bedroom scenario produces 6.51% gross, versus 4.94% for OOAK's RM850,000/RM3,500 one-bedroom scenario. A mature larger apartment can require less capital while serving a different household. That does not establish better management, superior total return or a broad family premium.

There is some transaction context: the visible Pines 1,292 sf April transfer at RM700,000 and nineteen records in the reported window merit attention. Area, floor, rights and condition are not normalised; neither that record nor the mixed median validates a RM700,000 1,268 sf investment. The main danger is pairing the cheapest unrenovated purchase with premium fitted rent. At RM3,200 rent the standard buffer becomes negative, before complete operating costs. The assumed RM40,000 works allowance is not a quote or known building liability.

UEM confirms Allevia handover in FY2025; its larger modern family stock is a competing cohort, not an undelivered future line item. Portal Sri Hartamas MRT proximity cannot be treated as present access: the MRT Corp evidence concerns a future scheme. The first useful next step is matched ordinary leases, actual sale condition, fees and funded works, followed by adjusted transfers and real journeys. Defer. Investor-led exits remain valid hypotheses; family owner adoption is a possible additional channel, not an assumed base premium.

## A29: Sri Hartamas

Windsor's family-income case challenges the idea that only the cheapest Cliveden studio is worth studying. RM750,000/RM4,000 produces 6.40% gross and +RM511 standard monthly coverage. The rental lead is from April and is syndicated, not multiple independent leases. With eleven paid months and other operating costs, annual carry is already about -RM865. One bay for a three-bedroom household and mixed-use charges can materially alter desirability and income.

Cliveden's old RM250,000 expression remains held because the exact sale is not freshly authenticated. The September RM285,888 lead is a different offer; at the same RM1,500 rent it further weakens already-negative annual carry. Neither the small price nor a sparse mixed transaction panel proves liquidity. Dorsett's developer product range provides a compact/family contrast, but hotel or managed letting must not validate an ordinary residential lease.

Windsor ranks second for family-income verification, with no preference for size by itself. The counter-thesis is that the apparent premium requires exceptional furnishing, rent concessions or operating expenditure absent from the base inputs. The nine-point Pines/Windsor rent grid reverses their coverage ordering. Defer until current ordinary rent, sale condition, approved use, bay utility and actual charges/works are reconciled. No present railway uplift or unverified management praise is credited.

## A30: Dutamas-Segambut

United Point's RM568,000, 958 sf three-bedroom expression tests Prima Duta's much larger 1,614 sf/RM600,000 alternative. At the same RM2,500 assumed stabilised rent, United Point loses about RM113 monthly on the standard versus RM371 for Prima Duta. Smaller and newer can therefore have a lower carrying burden without being a better home or a cleared investment.

The sale is advertised tenanted until March 2027, but no contractual rent is disclosed. RM2,500 comes from a different fully furnished rental lead; the sale is partly fitted with tandem bays. Actual income and fit-out timing must be established before a tenancy cashflow can use that stabilised number. A separate six-month no-income-credit sensitivity bounds the uncertainty without alleging nonpayment. This is a timing/identity issue, not an assumed vacancy event.

UOA's 887 + 887 + 735 units establish 2,509 units of project stock, not 2,509 empty or active rental units. Proposed/direct rail wording conflicts; no existing doorstep link or future rent uplift is assumed. Local household function and total cost must beat the rival explanation of heavy similar supply, inconvenient parking and weak current lease economics. Defer, with actual lease/collections and rights the next evidence priority. This does not finish a project-by-project Dutamas compact comparison.

## A31: Desa ParkCity-Menjalara

Three expressions cover a real price/use ladder: Westside One RM840,000/RM3,000 compact, Westside Three RM1.25 million/RM5,000 two-bedroom, and Menjalara 18 RM700,000/RM2,500 family. Their standard monthly deficits are approximately RM767, RM481 and RM689 respectively. Buying a lower-quantum ParkCity home does not necessarily improve yield, and nearby Menjalara cannot inherit the township's buyer willingness by proximity.

A striking RM749,000 Westside Three ad was actively investigated. Its header names the project, while its body describes Bukit Jalil and a township inspired by Desa ParkCity. That project-price pairing is quarantined; it proves neither a bargain nor that the retained RM1.25 million expression is correctly priced. It is not evidence of fraud or a verified auction.

Park Place and Noora remain material supply comparisons, but available official expected dates and site progress do not settle actual present VP. An old expected date cannot become delivered rental inventory, while uncertain timing cannot justify assuming no competition. Compact Noora substitution and larger lifestyle product competition belong in different panels. Defer at lowest income priority. Reopen through an exact coherent offer, ordinary lease, matched transfers and verified supply timing. Product appeal may be strong while the current financed income remains weak; neither observation cancels the other.

## A32: Kepong-Jinjang

First Residence is the lower-quantum family control: about 6.00% gross at RM389,999/RM1,950, but only +RM96 standard monthly buffer and -RM3,440 annual carry. Fortune Centra's RM480,000/RM2,100 two-bedroom expression gives 5.25% gross and -RM113 standard monthly. The selected two-bedroom costs more; fewer rooms alone do not secure the preferred return.

Fortune's sale is partly furnished, its photographs are labelled display, and rental leads differ in furnishings and bay count. The RM1,700 partial-furnished contrast matters more than an unadjusted upper rent. A separate RM470,000 sale lead does not resolve the fit-out problem. Tiny 250/300 sf advertisements may be rooms or subdivisions; they were not accepted as separately financeable studios. Advertised 100% financing does not alter the user's common 90% baseline or establish effective consideration.

AVA's confirmed FY2025 handover adds newer family competition in the broader household task. Kiara Bay branding does not confer Mont Kiara buyer depth. The rival explanation is that a cheap third room generates insufficient rent to pay for age, repairs and bay constraints. Defer; verify equal-condition First Residence leases and works before treating it as an affordable winner. Jinjang low-cost/restricted stock and unpaired new packages remain explicit limits, so no area-wide statement that three bedrooms always win is supported.

## A33: Damansara Perdana-Mutiara Damansara

Neo's compact arithmetic is tempting: 7.63% gross and +RM549 annual carry at the inherited RM220,000/RM1,399. The live-offer/component/bay hold still dominates. New RM210,000/RM220,000 ads incorrectly sit under rental headings but say for sale in the body; they are sale leads, not astronomical rents or proof of the original unit's availability. A RM1,800 furnished two-bay rent must not be paired with an unverified cheapest sale.

Surian Mutiara's RM720,000/RM3,000, 850 sf control is around 5.00% gross and -RM269 standard monthly. The same area is labelled one, two or two-plus-one bedrooms. A visible RM630,000, 850 sf one-room transfer is relevant counterevidence, but rights and condition are not adjusted. Boustead's 2012 report says 311 Surian units were completed; portal 2016 metadata is not a sound lifecycle basis. Age itself does not demonstrate deterioration.

Larger Surian homes and D'Quince/Central Park Damansara form family alternatives. Actual phase timing must be separated; neither township totals nor uncertain VP are current vacant inventory. Wrong-state Sabah Surian results are excluded. Defer. Resolve Neo's exact title/use/offer and ordinary rent first; resolve Surian's original plan and comparable set before awarding Mutiara or extra-bedroom value. These two products serve different tasks and cannot establish a universal Perdana-Mutiara price spread.

## Portfolio, uncertainty and finite next evidence

The financial release reconciles 251 scenario evaluations and 171 diagnostic two-property paths using a synthetic RM500,000 cash pool and RM150,000 protected reserve. Only 49 pairs preserve that reserve under the combined rate, rent, vacancy, fee and works shock. Those numbers do not describe the user's capital, probabilities or an approved allocation; held cases remain held. The programme-wide portfolio replay remains outstanding.

Unresolved divergence raises uncertainty, lowers conviction and demands a larger evidenced margin of safety. No numeric safety discount is invented from an unvalidated fair value. Evidence that changes a decision takes priority: authenticated exact offers and rights, matched ordinary leases, itemised charges/works, adjusted completed transfers, actual lease timing and consequential supply delivery. Generic listing counts cannot clear these stops.

This batch is closed as bounded desk research because material public conflicts and contrasting products were investigated, the consequences of unworked segments are explicit, and another undirected search would not remove the specific capital stops. Private unit readiness and future validation remain open. No new causal mechanism or Core promotion is proposed.

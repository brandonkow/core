## C06 — Riana South

**Observed evidence.** UCSI University's official page identifies its Cheras/Taman Connaught campus, student facilities and residential halls, and reports a campus population above 15,000 ([UCSI campus](https://www.ucsiuniversity.edu.my/kuala-lumpur-campus/about-the-campus)). A property directory lists UCSI campus wings about 0.4 km and shuttle waiting points about 0.3 km from Riana South ([Riana South amenities](https://www2.durianproperty.com.my/property/bukit_manda%27rina/riana_south?college=UCSI-University-University-College-Sedaya-International-Kuala-Lumpur-Campur-North-Wing&pg=1)); route quality is not verified. A 947-sq-ft, three-bedroom rental with two bays was advertised at RM3,300, but its stated move-in date (15 October 2026) had passed when accessed, so it is only a possibly stale asking lead ([rental advertisement](https://speedhome.com/details/riana-south-condominium-cheras-vobfxiyr)).

**Mechanism.** Campus-linked students, staff or academic households may rent nearby private three-bedroom housing for campus access and local daily needs. This is a tenant-use route to investigate, not a family resale conclusion.

**Falsification test.** Verify campus-linked tenancy or repeat conventional whole-unit leases, inspect the actual walking/shuttle route, and check whether current advertised units are being taken up. If proximity does not translate into practical access, offers remain stale, or the renter channel depends on an unsupported operating model, withdraw the mechanism.

**Unresolved.** Actual tenant identities, collected rents, occupancy, route practicality and owner-occupier demand. G2 must establish a credible buyer path separately; campus users are not presumed to be future owners.

## R305 — PJ Midtown

**Observed evidence.** The developer locates PJ Midtown in Section 13 and describes the area as a residential/commercial hub ([IOI Properties project page](https://www.ioiproperties.com.my/pj-midtown/pj-midtown-commercial)). Its commercial brochure describes office and retail uses within the mixed commercial/residential development ([IOI commercial brochure](https://files.ioiproperties.com.my/dsites/s3fs-public/2024-02/PJ%20Midtown-Commercial_eBrochure_Feb24.pdf)). These establish a work/service context, not current office occupancy or tenant demand.

**Mechanism.** A professional, couple or small household with work or service routines in Section 13 may value a two-bedroom residence that shortens travel to that activity cluster.

**Falsification test.** Check whether the office/retail component is functioning, whether relevant workplaces are practically reachable from the residence, and whether renters or buyers actually choose the two-bedroom product for those routines. If the activity cluster is inactive or inaccessible, the mechanism fails.

**Unresolved.** Employer/office occupancy, household work locations, commuting routes, collected rents and buyer affordability. G2 must identify one credible buyer path, budget, task and alternatives; the existing small-household and investor narratives are not yet verified buyer evidence.

## R308 — Kelana Mahkota

**Observed evidence.** PropertyGuru showed 1,260-sq-ft, three-bedroom asking rentals around RM2,300–RM2,500/month, with several similar rows dated 28–29 September 2026. Repeated rows may be reposts or duplicates, so they count as one asking cluster, not independent leases. The same portal and Mudah identify local grocery/retail, school, healthcare and bus options ([PropertyGuru results](https://www.propertyguru.com.my/property-for-rent/at-kelana-mahkota-1201), [Mudah results](https://www.mudah.my/property/properties-in-kelana-mahkota-selangor)). Those portal amenity and access entries have not been route-checked.

**Evidence–judgment alert.** The PropertyGuru page labels “SS7 station” about 200 m away and describes an upcoming rail link. Do not use this as rail/TOD evidence: the official Rapid Bus notice identifies Taman Bahagia–SS6/SS7 as a Rapid On-Demand service area ([Rapid Bus notice](https://myrapid.com.my/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/RAPID-BUS-SIARAN-MEDIA-APLIKASI-RAPID-ON-DEMAND-CAPAI-FASA-AKHIR-PENYELARASAN-APLIKASI_V2.pdf)). Station type, exact stop and current connection remain unverified; neither the G1 mechanism nor a valuation assumes nearby rail.

**Mechanism.** A local household needing a conventional three-bedroom home may use Kelana Jaya's everyday service network and the project's residential facilities. Advertised family-sized rentals show a marketed tenant-use channel, not proven absorption.

**Falsification test.** Deduplicate the offers, confirm whole-unit tenancy and collected rents, verify the actual paths to daily services, and compare the family task with realistic lower-cost alternatives. If current ads are stale/duplicated, take-up relies on room partitioning, or daily services are impractical, withdraw the mechanism.

**Unresolved.** Unique available units, occupancy, family tenant/buyer depth, route access, management and exact-unit condition. G2 must establish one credible buyer path. The lower-quantum comparison with Sterling is not a G1 pass of income superiority.

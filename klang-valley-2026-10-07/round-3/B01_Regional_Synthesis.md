## Decision view

The most useful present investigation is still the low-quantum usable-home contest in Maluri and Connaught, but the evidence does not support a universal three-bedroom preference. Maxim's 825-sf two-bedroom scenario requires less entry capital than its 1,050-sf family scenario and has slightly better standard coverage. M Vertica's central access is a distinct proposition. Either may lose once parking, actual rent, condition or operating costs are reconciled.

Outer Cheras is not automatically inferior. It contains local-family, institutional-accommodation and low-quantum compact tasks, with different substitutes. The new Silk Sky studio diagnostic improves headline income arithmetic but carries a component-use hold. The Landmark compact barely covers the simplified standard under its assumptions. Neither may inherit campus demand or a family exit premium merely from an area label.

All 18 B01 expressions in the current comparison have negative cost-complete annual carry. This is not a universal rejection of Cheras: it limits what can be claimed at these particular inputs. Future growth requires a supported mechanism and funded waiting period. The 6% preference is not a prediction that rents must reach that level.

## Clearing evidence and decisive exclusions

The [inherited evidence register](../../cheras/Cheras_Evidence_Register.md) supplies source-level plans, rent asks, transfer windows and landed diagnostics. New raw 003/004/020 records add narrower questions:

- M Vertica 850-sf reports at 500,000, 555,000 and 645,000 do not make every 489,000 ask a 29% bargain. One intermediary address is wrong and primary/secondary/incentive basis remains unclear.
- Akasa 850-sf records in April2024 at 445,000 and 833,000 are too heterogeneous for an unqualified reference floor. Same size is necessary but insufficient.
- YouVista's eleven May2025-May2026 transfers are a mixed-layout liquidity observation, not a validated 847-sf value.
- Public Landmark transfer data found in this pass are old (2019-2020); Emerald rows are 2021-2022 and do not match the 1,161-sf target. Do not use them as current exit depth.
- Maxim's headline count contains a commercial record. Remove the mixed headline before considering residential comparables; the low-tail transfer may reflect condition or transaction circumstances.

These observations establish limits and rank sensitivity, not a probability of mispricing. G0 remains unresolved with an exact-unit route. No numerical valuation margin is fabricated.

## Demand, supply and exit integration

The [selected supply register](B01_B02_Supply_Register.md) distinguishes mature delivered stock, recent delivery, future permit phases and uncertain pipeline leads. For Maluri, multiple upcoming two-/three-bedroom cohorts directly challenge a 'supply exhausted' thesis. Their advertised starting prices cannot be treated as the exit floor for older homes. For outer Cheras, a new family product can compete for the same household budget even without sharing the same station or campus.

MRT, UCSI and UTAR are real anchors. Useful tenancy depends on the actual route, competing accommodation and household budget. The earlier campus sources remain authoritative for presence/distance only. Future demand is not forecast from institutional existence. Small-unit exits provisionally depend on investors; an evidenced young owner channel can later widen them, with no base-case premium today.

Windows is the strongest available counterexample to a simplistic age penalty: reported management improvements and collection coexist with future maintenance obligations. The 2026 interview/award record is positive but not audited accounts. No fee conversion from share units to floor area is permitted.

## Portfolio and four conclusions

**Product:** family capability is plausible in Aster, Maxim and park-led Windows; project/actual-unit operations remain unevenly evidenced. A three-bedroom label does not establish three useful private rooms.

**Investment:** no selected expression has validated clearing and complete proof for Deploy. A modest standard surplus does not cover eleven-month occupancy, operating allowances and entry/exit friction. Terminal recovery prices in the current comparison are burdens to test, not forecasts.

**Priority:** compare Maxim825, Maxim1050 and MVertica850 first on matched rent/rights/operations. Investigate SilkSky only through its identity gate. Current shortfall expressions remain lowest priority without being discarded. Lavile's higher capital and Landmark's tiny surplus make their apparent passes less robust.

**Capital:** Defer under the current evidence packet. The old Windows marginal-reserve Preserve Capacity rationale and old 'zero of36 pairs pass' result are superseded by the approved 90%/35-year replay. Do not repeat them.

## Bounded close and remaining coverage

This synthesis integrates all ten functional catchments, compact/family contrasts, relevant future stock and explicit adverse explanations. It does not constitute a unit census or site visit. Material remaining gaps include exact standalone-compact availability in some family nodes, current matched voluntary transfers, governance/engineering evidence, and actual buyer motives. New-launch net packages and executed distressed sales are not normalised; auction reserves cannot rank as completed prices.

Further unfiltered advertisements have limited value for the existing expressions; exact public plan/parcel and operation records remain preferable. These limitations restrict project-wide quality rankings and prevent buy readiness. The final programme audit must assess this package with the other seven batches rather than turn this paragraph into an automatic completion badge.

## Completion review for every catchment

A catchment can close only after the following have been assessed with explicit evidence, interpretation and consequence:

1. Functional boundaries and actual household/tenant tasks, without transferring city-wide demand to every project.
2. Material budget, layout and lifecycle choices, with excluded or unavailable segments and their decision implications.
3. Relevant competing stock and dated delivery/lifecycle evidence. Announced supply, completed inventory and active resale supply are different.
4. Comparable identity, record dates, source lineage, condition/incentive limitations and a justified reference-price conclusion or a specific G0 stop.
5. Exact role of rent, management, complete costs, financeability, price thresholds and stress. Scenario prices are not market values.
6. Buyer willingness and ability to pay, true functional substitutes, concentrated exit risk, terminal downside and ranking reversals.
7. Strongest counter-thesis, material divergence investigation, misses, and a finite next evidence route.

The original protocol permits desk completion with material private evidence gaps if their implications are explicit and further public investigation has low decision value. It does not require every project, personal portfolio access, physical inspection, paid records or future realised outcomes. Equally, a copy-pasted Defer and three generic document requests do not satisfy it.

## Execution-template miss

Round 2 repeatedly described compact units' primary buyer as a local owner household and required a 'surviving non-investor exit' for an investor buyer. That requirement was stronger than the frozen architecture and insufficiently evidenced. Supersede it for current analysis:

- Investor-led resale is an eligible channel. Test actual net income, acquisition quantum, financing and adverse required yields.
- Do not count young owner buyers as established redundant exit demand.
- Do not infer that investor dependence alone is a universal Reject. Record the concentration and whether the thesis can survive it.
- Owner adoption remains a falsifiable, project-specific pathway; it cannot rescue weak income arithmetic in advance.

The Core's buyer-pool-transfer rule and exit-risk tests stay authoritative. This correction belongs in execution workpapers.

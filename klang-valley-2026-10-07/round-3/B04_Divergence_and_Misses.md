# B04 Evidence-Judgment Divergence and misses

Cutoff: 7 October 2026. Applied cross-module control, not a new Core mechanism or G10. Sources and exact limitations are in [the evidence ledger](B04_Evidence_and_Supply.md); calculations are in [the financial report](B04_Financial_Underwriting.md). These are pre-outcome investigations, not validated forecasting successes.

Divergence is a trigger for investigation, not permission to override evidence. An investment thesis must ultimately be explainable through an identifiable market mechanism rather than personal conviction alone. Strong conviction without a falsifiable mechanism increases risk rather than analytical confidence.

## B4-D01: apparent ParkCity discount versus contradictory identity

**⚠ DIVERGENCE FLAG**

**Observed Evidence:** Listing 501228691 has a Westside Three header, 1,076 sf and RM749,000, apparently much cheaper than the retained 1,099 sf/RM1.25 million expression. Its body refers to Bukit Jalil and a township inspired by Desa ParkCity. See B4-E12 and raw022/030.

**Contradicting Judgment / Observation:** A large apparent discount could overturn the entry comparison, but the geographic description does not coherently identify the alleged asset. This is potential hidden mispricing competing with source corruption, not evidence that the higher-priced expression is fair.

**Possible Explanations:**

1. **Data limitation:** Cross-project template, wrong header/category, wrong area or stale multi-unit marketing.
2. **Judgment bias:** Bargain attraction might cause us to accept the low price; attachment to the expensive benchmark might cause us to dismiss a real discount too quickly.
3. **Omitted variable:** Different title, location, possession, parking, condition or restrictions could explain a genuine price gap.
4. **Structural change:** An actual supply release or distressed seller cohort could move prices, but no coherent matched evidence currently demonstrates that explanation.

**Falsifiable Hypothesis:** The apparent discount is primarily a project-identification mismatch rather than a verified lower Westside Three clearing opportunity.

**Evidence Required:** A coherent current exact-parcel offer with project address, original plan, title/rights, bay package and consideration; matched independent completed transfers; any possession/arrears conditions.

**Falsification Conditions:** A verified equivalent Westside Three parcel available at the stated effective price would falsify the mismatch explanation and require repricing the comparison. A generic repost of the same conflicting copy would not.

**Core Module Routing:** G0 Reference-Price Validity; Comparable Set; Price Discovery; G5 Price / Mispricing; G6 Exit Architecture.

**Current Status: Partially Resolved.** The published project-price pairing is quarantined on a directly observed contradiction. The actual asset and price are unresolved; neither fraud nor undervaluation is established. No RM749,000 financial case enters the eligible comparison. The retained higher offer remains unvalidated too.

## B4-D02: useful older family stock versus a compact-first income expectation

**⚠ DIVERGENCE FLAG**

**Observed Evidence:** Conditional Pines and Windsor three-bedroom calculations give 6.51% and 6.40% gross, versus OOAK compact at 4.94%. Both family cases pass the simplified monthly standard while annual operating carry remains slightly negative. Pines and Windsor ordering reverses across the rent grid.

**Contradicting Judgment / Observation:** A smaller purchase may seem more likely to meet the user's income standard. These particular larger, older apartments challenge that expectation. They do not establish a universal family-unit advantage.

**Possible Explanations:**

1. **Data limitation:** Indexed sale freshness, dated rental asks, unpaired condition/bays and small transfer samples inflate comparability.
2. **Judgment bias:** Preference for family utility or cheap older space could make us rationalise age and capex; a compact preference could make us ignore genuine income alternatives.
3. **Omitted variable:** Usable rooms, school/work routines, parking, privacy, actual maintenance and renovation costs may account for the spread.
4. **Structural change:** Allevia's confirmed handover changes larger-family competition. A shift in household demand or rent could help mature stock, but that transmission is not measured and receives no base uplift.

**Falsifiable Hypothesis:** At equivalent serviceable condition and ordinary lease terms, useful mature family units can produce better sustainable net income per capital ringgit than selected premium compact substitutes.

**Evidence Required:** Exact available sale condition; independent fit-out/major-works estimates; ordinary same-layout leases net of concessions; actual charges; adjusted transfers and achieved vacancy/collection evidence. Buyer motives must be measured separately if an owner-exit premium is proposed.

**Falsification Conditions:** Matched rent falls toward the lower scenarios, costs/works consume the spread, or the presumed family utility fails on bays/layout/routes. A premium renovated rent that cannot be reproduced within the cost budget also falsifies the claimed advantage at that entry.

**Core Module Routing:** Asset Fit; Buyer Universe; Owner-Occupier Desirability; Lifecycle; Supply Structure; G5 Price / Mispricing; G6 Exit Architecture; G7 Financing / Terminal Risk.

**Current Status: Unresolved.** The selected financial counterexample is real arithmetic, while investability and sustainable relative performance remain unproved. Pines is the first verification lead, Windsor the second; no Deploy. Uncertainty requires lower conviction and a defensible safety margin after reference value is established, not an invented percentage discount.

## B4-D03: attractive compact yields versus weak offer and component evidence

**⚠ DIVERGENCE FLAG**

**Observed Evidence:** Neo's inherited RM220,000/RM1,399 gives 7.63% gross and about RM549 positive annual operating carry. Cliveden's RM250,000/RM1,500 gives 7.20% gross but negative annual carry. Both original offers remain freshness-held. New Neo ads mix sale copy with rental-category amounts, and Cliveden has a different higher-price September lead.

**Contradicting Judgment / Observation:** These figures could signal genuine affordable investor-led accommodation. They could also be poor data, an unfinanceable component or an understated cost burden. A mandatory owner-occupier exit would wrongly predetermine the answer.

**Possible Explanations:**

1. **Data limitation:** Stale low asks, sale/rent categorisation, mixed components and bay mismatch. The RM1,800 Neo two-bay rent is not equivalent to the cheapest unverified sale.
2. **Judgment bias:** Yield anchoring favours the cheapest price/highest rent; discomfort with investor-led compact exits could unfairly reject a viable channel.
3. **Omitted variable:** Permitted use, financeability, tenure, parking rights, concessions, service costs, arrears and major works can change both income and buyer universe.
4. **Structural change:** Management improvement, cheaper genuine resale supply or changed accommodation demand could alter ordinary economics, but no such current transition is established.

**Falsifiable Hypothesis:** An identifiable eligible compact parcel at a current effective low entry can support ordinary tenancy and a financeable investor-led exit after complete costs.

**Evidence Required:** Exact live offer and title/use/bays; lender valuation and terms; independent ordinary leases/collection; itemised charges/works; matched completed transfers. Re-run entry, carry and terminal cashflows on the same parcel.

**Falsification Conditions:** The low-priced parcel is not the claimed eligible component, expected income depends on a different package or letting model, finance/works consume the thin buffer, or a matched exit buyer cannot finance a supported price. Missing proof alone is not a structural failure.

**Core Module Routing:** G0; Comparable Set; Buyer Universe; Financing Risk; Lifecycle; Exit Buyer; Exit Liquidity; G7/G8.

**Current Status: Unresolved.** Both hidden mispricing and hidden structural risk remain plausible. Hold the offers outside an executable shortlist. Do not reward the only positive annual arithmetic with higher conviction. Investor-led exits remain permissible and unvalidated.

## B4-D04: tenanted sale versus hypothetical stabilised income

**⚠ DIVERGENCE FLAG**

**Observed Evidence:** United Point's sale states an existing tenancy until March 2027 but does not disclose rent. The RM2,500 assumption comes from a different furnished rental advertisement. The sale is partly fitted and has tandem bays.

**Contradicting Judgment / Observation:** A tenanted property may appear to provide immediate known cashflow. That conclusion conflicts with the missing contract and different future furnishing package.

**Possible Explanations:**

1. **Data limitation:** Asking rent for another unit is mistaken for contracted income; concessions, dates and fit-out differences are missing.
2. **Judgment bias:** Preference for an already-let purchase can obscure weak lease terms or collection; equally, uncertainty must not be turned into an allegation of nonpayment.
3. **Omitted variable:** Actual rent, arrears, deposit, renewal/termination rights, repair obligations, tenancy transfer and the timing of works.
4. **Structural change:** Lease expiry could permit reletting at a changed market rent, but the price, timing and absorption must be evidenced rather than assumed.

**Falsifiable Hypothesis:** The advertised stabilised rent overstates near-term distributable income unless the current contract and necessary works schedule support it.

**Evidence Required:** Actual lease and receipts, vacant-possession/transfer terms, ordinary equal-condition rents, fit-out quotations and downtime schedule.

**Falsification Conditions:** Verified current net receipts at or above the assumed ordinary rent, with condition and lease terms requiring no omitted transition expenditure, would rebut the overstatement concern. A new advertisement does not verify receipts.

**Core Module Routing:** Price Discovery; Lifecycle; Asset Fit; Financing Risk; G7 cashflow timing; G8 funded carry.

**Current Status: Unresolved.** The base case is explicitly a hypothetical stabilised scenario. The six-month no-income-credit stress bounds uncertainty and is not a forecast of nonpayment. No executable underwriting substitutes hypothetical future rent for current contractual income.

## B4-D05: reported station access and completion dates versus dated primary evidence

**⚠ DIVERGENCE FLAG**

**Observed Evidence:** Portal material can show Sri Hartamas MRT proximity as if current. MRT Corp's dated scheme record concerns future MRT3 infrastructure. Secondary metadata conflicts with primary historical completion for Surian and Laurel; old expected dates do not settle present Park Place/Noora VP. Allevia and AVA have primary FY2025 handover confirmation.

**Contradicting Judgment / Observation:** Present accessibility, ageing costs and competing supply materially affect rent, value and exits. Neither historical metadata nor a development narrative can be assumed forward-looking truth.

**Possible Explanations:**

1. **Data limitation:** Stale templates, projected dates relabelled as actual completion, phase aggregation and automatic proximity labels.
2. **Judgment bias:** Rail optimism, preference for a newer age label, or scarcity narratives can favour the convenient date.
3. **Omitted variable:** Actual route, station/phase opening, CCC/VP, competing stock mix and timing of landlord releases.
4. **Structural change:** New infrastructure and delivered stock can change market structure; occupancy, rent and transaction response still need separate evidence.

**Falsifiable Hypothesis:** Treating future access as present and expected completion as confirmed stock misstates the relevant product's current use and supply position.

**Evidence Required:** Official operating/VP records for the specific route/phase, physical route evidence and a dated matched rental/sales panel showing transmission. Separate stock from active vacancy and net absorption.

**Falsification Conditions:** Verified current station operation/route or specific actual VP can overturn a timing hold; a credible matched rent/transaction response may validate a forward change. Announcements, take-up and township totals alone cannot.

**Core Module Routing:** Catalyst; Structural Change; Lifecycle; Supply Structure; Comparable Set; Exit Liquidity.

**Current Status: Partially Resolved.** The listed primary historical/hand-over corrections are usable and current-station treatment is rejected. Park Place/Noora and D'Quince exact present delivery details remain unresolved. No forecast opening year or rental uplift is supplied to fill the gaps.

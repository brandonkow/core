## A18 - PJ Old/New Town

The useful contrast is low acquisition quantum against ageing mixed-use costs and component risk. PJ8's inherited RM390k/RM2k scenario gives6.15%gross and+RM116/month under the simple standard, but annual carry is-RM3,010. Its rental comparables differ in size. The screened two-bedroom evidence likewise does not justify borrowing a900-sf rent for800-sf sale stock.

Amcorp's225k/1600scenario produces8.53%gross and+RM1,681annual carry after operating allowances. It is still component/freshness-held, and a flat-price five-year exit produces a loss after entry/exit costs. The alternative explanation is a genuine low-quantum income opportunity; the adverse explanation is a stale/unmatched or less financeable parcel with underestimated works. Both remain open.

Product quality is ungraded. PJ8 remains a conditional ordinary-income research lead; Amcorp receives identity-first investigation, outside eligible ranking. Next decisive evidence is exact component/title and live terms, followed by comparable collected rent and project liabilities. Further generic PJ-location descriptions will not resolve this.

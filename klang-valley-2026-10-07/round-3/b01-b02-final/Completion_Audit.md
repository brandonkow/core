## Why the four public gaps may close

1. **A02:** a lower-ticket older family home is now priced and costed against both Shamelin formats. It does not rescue income at the observed asking level; this negative result is substantive, not a reason to keep looking until a preferred answer appears.
2. **A03:** issuer historical evidence separates480condominiums from34terraces and undermines the352portal field. A mature family unit is costed against the duplex. Current title/plan/operations remain unit holds; no density or management premium is assumed.
3. **A05:** Ketumbar failed an automatic catchment match. Damai Hillpark supplies the local family control, while townhouse/landed leads, auction rights and contradictory offers constrain interpretation. An index-only exact offer remains an investment hold, explicitly excluded from validated ranking.
4. **A16:** a separately described residential two-bedroom control is added. An unsupported550-sf studio assumption is dropped; the studio/one-bedroom category remains eligible. Bath/fitout/fee problems are tested rather than suppressed to produce a positive yield.

For the other13catchments, existing public work already distinguishes material household choices, supply, contrary transfer/rental evidence and finite decision routes. No new one-day refresh was required to reproduce every prior advertisement. Upper-quality family, distressed, restricted, new-launch net-package and obscure original compact cohorts remain scoped screens where stated. They are not falsely presented as exhaustive research or eligible ranking populations.

## Residual uncertainty and reopening conditions

Private/exact-parcel title, plan, bay, rental collection, fee, engineering, lender and MC records were not inspected. Their absence blocks deployment, not necessarily bounded regional research completion. Public records that become available later can reopen a conclusion: a materially different executable net offer; a like-for-like achieved rent; a lawful component/rights clarification; a dated new competing supply phase; or a evidenced change in access, management or tenant/buyer structure.

No automatic forward outcome validation is claimed; no paid database, outreach, title search or site visit was performed. More generic listings are presently lower information value than those specific records. This is a finite research judgment, not a claim that private evidence is impossible to obtain.

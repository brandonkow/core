## ⚠ DIVERGENCE FLAG F-D01 — older-family affordability versus income

**Observed Evidence:** Astaka's388k1115-sf ask is materially below Shamelin's selected515k/700k expressions; Prima offers1460-sf family space at550k, near the520kduplex quantum. Whole-unit rent controls and complete-cost replay nevertheless produce substantial deficits. [SourcesF01-F15](Evidence.json).

**Contradicting Judgment / Observation:** The audit hypothesis was that overlooked older family utility and lower entry could materially improve the investment choice. That remains plausible at the product level but is not demonstrated as hidden investment mispricing.

**Possible Explanations:**
1. **Data limitation:** Astaka's rent is older and slightly size-mismatched; PrimaPF/FF asks differ widely. Stale or unmatched packages may understate attainable rent, or high asks may overstate it. Portal count/alias errors cannot be averaged away.
2. **Judgment bias:** Searching specifically for a cheap old-family winner risks anchoring on PSF and familiar family preferences. Try to show that rent and buyer budgets rationally justify the lower price.
3. **Omitted variable:** Original rooms, parking, capex, access, maintenance and rights can explain both affordability and weak income. More bedrooms may not translate into more paying tenant demand.
4. **Structural change:** Better management or a changed household pool could raise rent/buyer willingness, but no dated achieved transition is established. Historical reputation is not forward proof.

**Falsifiable Hypothesis:** At a valid net entry, the older family format's practical utility is under-recognised by current asking/lease evidence enough to compensate its operating and capital costs.

**Evidence Required:** Original plans, actual rights/bays, same-condition leases, net voluntary transfers, fee/capex records and observed buyer alternatives. Prima's historical issuer count supports480condos; it does not supply a rent premium.

**Falsification Conditions:** Matching preserves rents near1600/1800; extra fit-out or maintenance absorbs the difference; the owner pool prefers alternatives at the required recovery price; or the low purchase basis is not executable. Even Prima's2900upperFFbranch is annualnegative with funded fit-out.

**Core Module Routing:** G0/G5 Comparable Set, Price Discovery, Mispricing; G2Buyer Universe; G3/G4Asset Fit and Owner-Occupier Desirability; G6Exit Buyer; G7Finance/Terminal.

**Current Status:** **Partially Resolved.** Useful product alternatives established and historical count clarified; hidden mispricing unvalidated. All cases Defer, with increased evidence burden and no invented margin percentage.

**Observed Evidence:** The local family stock includes390kordinary-index leads and216300auction-reserve rows, alongside much higher asks. A low-reserve diagnostic can become cash-positive after the initial vacancy period. [SourcesF17-F25,F33-F34](Evidence.json).

2. **Judgment bias:** Searching for a lower-ticket rescue can select favourable fields from different listings. Reject that practice even if the result falls below the required yield boundary.
3. **Omitted variable:** Buyer eligibility, title, occupation, arrears, repairs, financing deadline, bays and commercial interface can explain lower price and narrower exit.
4. **Structural change:** A genuine distressed supply release or later normalisation could create an opportunity, but there is no observed winning sale/absorption sequence proving it.

**Falsifiable Hypothesis:** A specific ordinary or lawfully eligible distressed acquisition remains materially cheaper after rights, net costs, possession and rent are normalised, without relying on a different household pool.

**Evidence Required:** Exact ordinary or auction parcel, full net terms and eligibility, actual outcome/possession where relevant, costed condition, original plan and ordinary lease. Distressed and voluntary cases must retain separate evidence.

**Falsification Conditions:** Low price is non-executable/restricted to another buyer, winning/all-in basis rises materially, vacancy/capex erases income, or same-condition voluntary evidence explains the spread. The selected390kordinary-index case remains cash-negative.

**Core Module Routing:** G0Comparable Set/Price Discovery; G2Buyer Universe; G4Asset Fit; G5Mispricing; G6Exit Liquidity; G7Financing; G9Defer.

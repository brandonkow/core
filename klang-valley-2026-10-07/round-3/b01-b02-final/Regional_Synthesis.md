## Differentiated geographic conclusions

Inner Cheras has credible local and access-led housing tasks, but upcoming Maluri/Cochrane phases constrain a generic scarcity thesis. Connaught's lower-entry two-bedroom/family choices deserve comparison on actual room utility, route, bays and net leases; Aster's family potential is retained without assuming a premium.

Midah/Shamelin's mature family choice is broader than the previously selected newer expressions. Yet lower PSF alone is a poor capital test. A household may prefer more original rooms, privacy or quieter surroundings while the investor still needs substantially more net rent or a lower executable acquisition basis.

Alam Damai, BTHO, Mahkota and Sungai Long require separate local-family, rail and institution-linked catchments. A familiar area name does not transfer tenancy from the nearest campus or MRT. Windows' positive governance reporting remains a counterexample to an automatic age penalty, while its high entry and carrying costs remain financial constraints. Landed competition depends on actual rights, condition and household budget; no universal high-rise price ceiling is set.

Balakong's compact income lane remains relevant, but exact component and lawful use precede yield ranking. SilkSky cannot inherit Akasa's household utility or central-Cheras rents. A studio that is only a partition or unverified component is not a lower-priced independently tradable home.

Kuchai/OUG/OKR offer a broad total-budget ladder. A low asking price can be a valid opportunity or an impaired/incorrectly described unit; dispersed recorded prices do not decide which. Residence8's contradictory listing is retained as a failed clean control, not used to suppress the family cohort.

Taman Desa and Sri Petaling show the cost of a second room or newer project. The additional utility can be real while rent remains insufficient for the added price. M Aspira future competition, completed M Oscar and delivered Trion2 belong in the forward choice set; their prices are not resale floors for older homes. Trion's seller-tenancy claim is not authenticated income.

Bukit Jalil now includes an older residential two-bedroom at much lower quantum than Tropika, avoiding a false choice between an expensive new product and Aurora's held component. It is not yet a demonstrated superior investment: affordable buyer entry, practical one-bath use and future capex are separate tests.

## Reversals and finite evidence priorities

A shortfall can become researchable through a lower genuine entry or a supported transition mechanism. No present weak case receives automatic growth.

The next evidence routes are specific: exact unit/rights and consideration; original plan and bays; same-package ordinary lease and collection; actual service/sinking/arrears and funded works; lender acceptance; and a transaction/substitute comparison at the required exit burden.

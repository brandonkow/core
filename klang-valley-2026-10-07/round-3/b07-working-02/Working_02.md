**Contradicting Judgment / Observation:** industrial growth and cheap large space might conceal rental upside.
**Possible Explanations:** Data limitation—asking rents and missing employee counts; judgment bias—anchoring on7.2%/growth headlines; omitted variables—employer housing, bays, transport and landed supply; structural change—actual employment/access gains or new competing inventory.

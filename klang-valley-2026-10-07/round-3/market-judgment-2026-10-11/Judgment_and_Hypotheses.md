A related layout hypothesis is that self-contained separation can produce a net premium within a genuinely matched parcel/catchment comparison. Cross-region Eve/Maxim comparisons alone cannot identify the causal privacy premium.

| Explanation family | Investigation |
|---|---|
| Data limitation | Distinguish whole units from rooms; deduplicate offers; control furniture, utility bundles, tenancy term, bays, usable space and dates. Verify whether incomes and tenant tasks actually match. |
| Judgment bias | Attempt to disprove an area stereotype and personal privacy preference. Actively retain whole-unit Cheras and room-rental Ara counterexamples. |
| Omitted variable | Test exclusivity, co-residents, household composition, practical commute, operating service and total housing budget. |

**Confirming evidence required:** Independent matched leases/collections and renewal or absorption observations with explicit sample, period and package; original plans and privacy configuration; observed choice against relevant local substitutes. For split operations, reconcile net income, vacancies and costs across the entire parcel.

**Falsifiers:** The apparent premium disappears after package/cost matching; exclusive units require longer voids or more concessions; retention does not improve; the specific cohort chooses shared alternatives despite comparable budgets; or incremental fit-out and operating costs consume the gain. An isolated room advertisement does not falsify a relative preference claim.

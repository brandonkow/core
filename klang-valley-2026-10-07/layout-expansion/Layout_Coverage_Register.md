# Regional layout coverage register

Version: KV-REG-2026-10-07-LAYOUT-v1. Read with [the scope clarification](Scope_Clarification.md). This re-indexes existing research; it adds no new market evidence, completed cases or gate clearance.

## Reading the register

- **NC** means not covered by a first-round selected case; it does not mean the layout is unavailable or uninvestable. Older work may exist in the inherited-reference column.
- **KVxx** identifies conditional G0–G9 work on a marketed layout. Every first-round case remains G0 stopped and G9 Defer; original parcel-plan verification remains outstanding.
- **Inherited C03** is the previously studied marketed one-bedroom EkoCheras duplex. Other inherited cases are retained as references rather than exhaustively reclassified by this update.
- The 14 first-round cases comprise three marketed one-bedroom, eight marketed two-bedroom and three marketed three-bedroom expressions. There is no dedicated original-studio gate case. These counts describe research selection, not market supply.
- Studios and one-bedroom units are now explicitly eligible for proactive sampling in every catchment. Do not infer regional suitability or rejection from these sparse columns.

## Pre-outcome competing propositions

1. Compact two-bedroom stock may improve total-quantum access and rental coverage, but high PSF, fees and an investor-heavy resale channel may offset this. Compare original bedrooms and usable area; do not substitute partitions.
2. Mature household nodes may support more durable owner exits, but higher acquisition quantum and ageing capital works can make the best living product a weak income investment.
3. Outer-corridor lower prices may create genuine entry options, or merely compensate for weaker high-rise adoption, supply and landed competition. Neither interpretation is presumed.
4. Strong advertised rents may represent furnishing, room/short-stay income, stale asks or distinct unit cohorts. Test achievable whole-unit rent before rewarding headline yield.
5. A delivered transit/commercial catalyst is existing utility unless incremental demand, transmission, timing and incomplete price recognition are evidenced.
6. High reported transaction volume may reflect new sales, mixed layouts or financing-driven transactions. It does not establish ordinary secondary exit liquidity.

Each proposition can fail. An unknown data field does not create a Divergence Flag; flags concern material conflicting interpretations or observations of the same decision proposition.

## Evidence and deliverable boundary

Research sequentially by batch after a programme-wide source scan. Record all examined projects and unworked catchments, product/lifecycle coverage, source accessibility and reasons for case selection. Full G0–G9 applies only to explicitly selected expressions; screening is labelled screening. No candidate quota or forced best buy.

For each worked expression preserve source dates, original ask/rent, exact layout differences, independent source lineage, G0 stop, conditional downstream analysis, full financial chain, counter-thesis and finite next evidence. Registered-price portal summaries are intermediated evidence, not inspected transfer instruments. A website's future date or misleading crawl time cannot create evidence from after the research cutoff.

Output completion separately for geography mapping, project screening, selected-case desk underwriting, decision readiness and realised validation. The programme is not complete merely because all eight batches have headings. Remaining gaps carry decision implications. No automatic later execution is implied by a work queue.

## D01 — Compact yield versus product and price identity

⚠ DIVERGENCE FLAG

Observed Evidence: R208 produces a conditional 12% gross yield using a RM 150k advertised amount and RM 1,500 asking rent. Other compact advertisements appear attractive on price/rent.

Contradicting Judgment / Observation: Centrestage has office and serviced-suite components; the selected listing differs between 301/306 sf and furnishing labels. Horizon 478-sf advertisements mix one-bath/two-bath and dual-key descriptions. Habitus 451-sf advertisements describe SOVO. These conflicts can invalidate the residential investment expression before valuation.

Possible Explanations:
1. A — Data limitation. Attempted component, plan-description and transaction searches. Obtained: contradictory labels and a separate Centrestage office transaction page. No authenticated plan/title.
2. B — Judgment bias. Tested the analyst's temptation to prefer the highest yield. Removed these examples from eligible research ranking instead of explaining discrepancies away.
3. C — Omitted variable. Permitted use, whole-parcel ownership, allocated bays, lending terms and actual operating costs could explain the apparent yield. Not obtained from binding documents.
4. D — Structural change. A lawful change of use or genuine conversion could alter eligibility; no evidence obtained. This explanation is not assumed.

Falsifiable Hypothesis: The exceptionally high apparent residential yield is substantially driven by component/identity mismatch rather than a verified cheap whole residential parcel.

Evidence Required: Authenticated exact-title and approved-plan identity, effective sale amount, ordinary whole-unit lease and the relevant lender's treatment. Public searches are completed for this pass; authenticated documents remain unavailable.

Falsification Conditions: A matching legal residential parcel, genuine sale and repeatable ordinary lease reconcile the mismatch with no hidden operating-cost burden. Conversely, office-only or non-separate-title identity stops this expression under the residential mandate.

Core Module Routing: Reference-Price Validity; Dynamic Comparable Mapping; Asset Fit; Buyer Universe; Financing Risk; G0/G2/G4/G7.

Current Status: Partially Resolved. Error pathways identified; specific rights unresolved. Higher proof burden; no Deploy and no blanket rejection of all SOHO or mixed-use properties.

Sources: retrievals 003/040/078/099/112/119 and [Centrestage office records](https://www.brickz.my/transactions/commercial/selangor/petaling-jaya/centrestage/non-landed/office/). Office evidence is rejected as a residential price comparable.

Falsifiable Hypothesis: Current income cases materially relying on these future stations overstate present utility.

Falsification Conditions: Verified existing relevant service and functional route show that the case never depended on the future station; or later opening produces independently observed demand transmission. A station name alone cannot falsify the warning.

Core Module Routing: TOD; Catalyst; Second-Order Beneficiary Transmission; Lifecycle; G1/G3/G6.

Current Status: Partially Resolved. Current underwriting excludes unverified future service. Future transmission unresolved. Existing Kuchai/Taman Equine rail is not rejected merely because a generic guide or another station is wrong.

Contradicting Judgment / Observation: The standard model shows current shortfalls at the selected purchase prices. The analyst's plausible positive use-quality judgment does not establish attractive income investment or a supported capital-growth exit.

Possible Explanations:
1. A — Data limitation. Asking prices may exceed effective consideration; furnished rents may overstate collections. No matched clearing interval obtained.
2. B — Judgment bias. Tested prestige/familiarity and family-location preference by adding lower-quantum and non-family comparisons. Did not mark prime districts automatic winners.

Evidence Required: Verified net entry, achieved long-term rents, true substitute sales and future-buyer budgets. For a transition thesis: event, unit-level transmission and funded carry.

Falsification Conditions: Credible lower net entry or durable collected rent clears actual costs; or independently evidenced ownership demand supports a viable exit without an assumed premium. Market disagreement alone is not falsification.

Core Module Routing: Asset Fit; Owner-Occupier Desirability; Price Ceiling; Buyer-Pool Transfer; Thesis–Expression; G2/G5/G6/G8.

Current Status: Unresolved. Lowest income research priority at current shortfall; use quality is ungraded, not declared poor. No hard rejection or universal 6% rule.

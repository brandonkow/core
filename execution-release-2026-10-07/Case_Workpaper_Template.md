## Financial chain

Current rent and evidence; verified management fee/sinking separately or disclosed proxy; 90%/4%/35-year instalment and coverage; gross yield; entry and complete costs; monthly collection and loan schedule; five-year or declared-horizon debt; cash returned on sale; nominal break-even and explicitly selected return hurdle; future buyer financing; joint shock and minimum reserve path. Actual bank/valuation terms separately. State missing taxes/costs and show assumed bounds rather than treating unknown as verified zero.

If currently shortfall: lowest research tier; fill the transition packet in the execution standard. Do not add unproven future rent to the base model.

## Material divergence investigation

Use the master flag contract. For each A-D explanation: hypothesis, discriminatory evidence, planned/attempted/obtained state, result and remaining unknown. State exact buyer/unit/horizon, price domain if relevant, review deadline or bounded event window, falsifiers and consequence. Otherwise state why no material divergence exists. Unknown alone does not create polarity.

## Four separate conclusions and rank robustness

Product-quality preference; investment attractiveness at stated price; investigation priority; G9. For each state confidence basis, coverage limits and strongest reversal condition. Report which plausible rent, fee, condition, entry or exit changes reverse rank. Scenario coverage is not a probability estimate.

## Next evidence and closeout

At most three immediate decision-changing questions: evidence route, expected decision impact, effort/access limit, deadline/event and action if unresolved. Broader risk ledger remains available. Work-queue disposition: advance, event-watch or stop active research. Preserve the G9 category separately.

Record original call, subsequent evidence, versioned revision, material miss and whether an actual outcome matured. No source-error detection or paper profit counts as an investment hit. No regional-completion claim beyond declared coverage.

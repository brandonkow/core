# Klang Valley regional underwriting protocol

Release: KV-EXEC-2026-10-07. Status: preparation only. No additional area underwriting has started. The user must be notified before the full-area programme begins; this is a notification requirement, not an invented additional approval gate.

## Scope and coverage contract

The investable universe remains residential high-rise. Landed, commercial and institutional assets may be diagnostic substitutes or demand anchors; they are not silently added as investment recommendations. Klang Valley is a functional housing/commuting scope, not all Selangor or an unsupported exhaustive administrative definition.

Before research, publish a bounded area register stating each catchment, included submarkets, boundary rationale, overlaps and exclusions. Deduplicate cross-boundary projects with one canonical identity and multiple demand links. Coverage additions must be versioned, not silently expand the completion target. The initial area register will be built at programme launch after the user's notification, not by claiming an unresearched universal list in this protocol.

For each catchment track, rather than mechanically fill, these dimensions:

- total acquisition budget segments derived from local buyer choices;
- genuine one-, two- and three-bedroom / larger / special layouts, with approved bedroom versus partition/study identified;
- new launch, near-completion/first resale, mature secondary and distressed expressions;
- ordinary income, defensive utility, compounding and evidenced special-situation roles;
- rail access, car-based local routines and institutional demand only where actually relevant;
- active, excluded with reason, research stopped, screened only and not covered.

There is no minimum quota forcing a poor or nonexistent product into a cell. Unavailable evidence is a coverage gap, not a negative quality rating. Cheapness, search visibility, familiar projects and the founder's layout preferences must not determine the whole sample.

## Sequenced research

1. Lock scope and provisional competing propositions before project-level research; distinguish inherited practitioner knowledge from independent hypotheses.
2. Establish functional buyer tasks and evidence for demand/credit/supply mechanisms, with clear historical/current/forward distinctions.
3. Build the broad project/product universe, record evidence accessibility and exclusions, then select contrasting expressions without only choosing presumed winners.
4. Apply the unchanged G0-G9. Preserve stops while permitting labelled conditional diagnostics and independent vetoes.
5. Use the common 90%/4%/35-year model, complete costs and stress. Current shortfall goes to the lowest research tier with explicit transition evidence requirements.
6. Separate product quality, investment attractiveness, investigation priority and G9. Compare actual two-bedroom alternatives where the buyer task makes them relevant.
7. Red-team the leading interpretation, weakest input and omitted substitutes, including relevant landed budget choices without pooling incompatible values.
8. Close with bounded next evidence, rank-switch conditions, manual review triggers and coverage limits; preserve the pre-outcome call.

## Required regional package

- Functional market map/table with boundaries, source and buyer-task rationale.
- Coverage matrix and exclusions, including unworked segments; do not title a sample as a complete unit census.
- Evidence/claim and comparable ledger with dates and source lineage.
- Candidate gate sheets using [the common template](Case_Workpaper_Template.md).
- Versioned financial results, rank sensitivity and current-shortfall transition records.
- Separate product, price-dependent investment and research-priority conclusions.
- Original thesis, strongest counter-thesis, Divergence work completed versus planned, misses and outcome status.
- Completion statement distinguishing desk coverage, decision readiness and realised validation.

## When a region is sufficiently complete

Completion means the declared functional scope and material product/budget segments have either been investigated to the agreed depth or explicitly recorded as coverage gaps with a decision implication. It does not require every development or listing. All conclusions must be reproducible from the evidence and configuration at cutoff. A region can be desk-complete without any Deploy.

Do not pause all regional work while waiting years for existing forward calls to mature. Conversely, do not claim predictive validation because the report is complete. Move to the next declared region when further public research has low decision value and the remaining exact evidence route is documented. Revisit on evidence change, not to fill an arbitrary count of buys.

## Programme release boundary

This protocol, the decision standard, common financial configuration, finite validation record and read-only replays are the prerequisites being completed now. They are not a new round of regional research. The final completion message must state that all-area underwriting has not started and that a start notification will precede it. No contacts, paid data, offers or monitoring automations are authorised by this protocol.

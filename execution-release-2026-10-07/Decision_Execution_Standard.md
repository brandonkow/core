# Decision execution standard - 2026-10-07

Status: approved implementation of the user's ten-point review response. This document operationalises the unchanged master and supplements the existing SOP. It is not a second Core, a new gate, or a claim of validated investment accuracy. The master remains authoritative for causal logic and G0-G9. This release supersedes earlier execution ambiguity on research ranking and current shortfalls.

## 1. Fixed comparison basis and changing market conditions

Every standard comparison uses 90% of acquisition price financed at 4% over 35 years. Standard monthly coverage is rent minus full loan instalment minus management fee. Gross yield preferably reaches 6%; it is not a universal rejection threshold. Keep actual lending, valuation shortfall, complete costs and stress separate. Never reduce comparison LTV or increase assumed rent to rescue a case.

The user explicitly allows market-dependent review. A changed environment can change evidence, an interpretation or a justified execution parameter. It cannot retroactively change what was known. Record old and new observations, the mechanism, affected outputs, effective version/date and counterevidence. Changes to the user's approved baseline require explicit user direction; a new causal Core principle still faces the existing promotion threshold. No Core content is changed in this release.

## 2. Current shortfall and transition opportunities

Current standard shortfall is not an automatic structural Reject. Retain the case in the **lowest research-priority tier**, with only bounded event-triggered investigation while the shortfall remains. Do not remove it from the coverage universe or infer permanent poor quality. An attractive growth story alone cannot raise that tier. This applies even when the investor can personally fund the gap.

For an ordinary Income Engine purchase, non-negative standard coverage is the default entry condition. It is a default, not an immutable market law. A current-shortfall exception can be considered only through an explicit transition case. The exception never changes the current negative result or clears another gate. A fully evidenced exception can be presented as a reasoned G9 proposal, but an unverified forecast remains Defer at lowest research priority. Purchase execution remains a separate authorised action.

Required transition record:

1. Current achieved or defensibly evidenced rent, fee and shortfall; identify any proxy.
2. Rent required to cover the approved standard, and separately the higher rent required for complete costs/collection assumptions.
3. Specific causal transmission from a funded/operating change to tenant demand and willingness to pay for this unit. A catalyst name or proximity is insufficient.
4. Independent evidence discriminating that explanation from competing supply, seasonality, incentives or unit upgrades.
5. Earliest credible benefit date, delay scenario and a justified review/expiry event. Do not use an arbitrary annual growth input as proof.
6. Cumulative carry and additional capital expenditure through that date, plus no-uplift/delay stress. Ring-fence the ability to hold without forced sale.
7. Competing tenant alternatives and net rent after any extra furnishing/operating burden; test whether the uplift is already reflected in entry price.
8. Falsifiers, fallback and precise affected gates. Expected future coverage does not equal current coverage.
9. Accurate existing investment role. Do not rename an unsupported income thesis a Special Situation to bypass the condition; that role requires validated mispricing.

Passing this record is not a guaranteed Deploy. G0-G8 must still support the investment and portfolio decision. Until sufficient discriminating evidence exists, list the upside only as a scenario. Event-driven review is a manual workflow, not an unattended alert service.

## 3. Four distinct conclusions

Always state these separately; do not issue one ambiguous 'best' label:

| Conclusion | Required basis |
|---|---|
| Product / living-quality preference | Named buyer task, observable unit/project experience, evidence limits; it does not establish a fair entry price |
| Investment attractiveness at the stated price | Normalised reference evidence, complete return and downside, future buyer and actual expression |
| Research priority | Decision value of resolving the next unknown, access/cost, shortfall tier and explicit rank fragility |
| G9 capital decision | Deploy / Defer / Reject / Preserve Capacity only; preserve upstream stops |

A forced single-choice answer must identify which conclusion is being ranked and name the strongest reversal condition. If no investment passes, do not turn the least weak one into a buy. Price-dependent ranking must include price and unit expression, not only a development name.

## 4. Evidence sufficiency by decision

Use four workpaper statuses: **Supported**, **Bounded estimate**, **Unknown**, **Contrary evidence**. These are evidence annotations, not new investment outputs. State why evidence is decision-sufficient; a label or a count cannot prove it. Critical unresolved facts cannot become Supported through a low price or analyst confidence.

| Gate | Minimum decision-relevant packet | What remains permissible when incomplete |
|---|---|---|
| G0 | Resolved project/unit identity, consideration type/date, comparable inclusion/exclusion ledger, material rights/size/condition differences, source independence and a supported normalised range | Investigate, identify a clearly unsupported ask, or conditional diagnostics; no validated discount if material normalisation remains unresolved |
| G1 | Actual use/ownership mechanism, relevant demand sources, lifecycle and price recognition, strongest alternative explanation | A clearly labelled mechanism hypothesis |
| G2 | Specific primary/secondary buyer tasks, false borrowed buyer, genuine motive and functional substitution evidence | Candidate buyer hypotheses, not measured depth or automatic transfer |
| G3 | Relevant operating design, current governance/funding and competing supply evidence proportionate to the risk | Project research preference with unknowns; no assumed healthy management |
| G4 | Identifiable unit, plan/rights/bays, permanent exposures and authentic condition evidence | Marketed-expression analysis; no inspected-unit claim |
| G5 | G0-G4 sufficiently resolved, evidenced gap, explanation and costs/time needed to recover it | No mispricing conclusion when prerequisites fail; fair-priced quality can still be analysed without manufacturing a discount |
| G6 | Future buyer, realistic alternative budgets, exit timing/finance, return burden, weak-exit and no-uplift cases | Conditional exit scenarios; required price is not a forecast |
| G7 | Actual finance/title restrictions when relevant, costs, works and forced-hold resources; standard plus actual finance views | Synthetic survival comparison, not bank approval or verified personal affordability |
| G8 | Actual mandate/capital constraints for an executable decision, relevant opportunity cost and correlated stress | A labelled synthetic allocation experiment only |
| G9 | Reconciled evidence and critical unknowns, independent vetoes, role and prior gate status | Defer for decision-critical unknowns; Reject only for established structural failure; Preserve Capacity for a demonstrated allocation reason |

This is not a demand for an arbitrary minimum of three or five transactions. Assess independence, recency, comparability and materiality. Different conclusions require different evidence: rejecting a purported discount may require less than certifying a precise fair price. A cheap listing with missing rights remains unknown, not good value.

G0 may yield a justified interval rather than false precision. Keep empirical range, justified adjustment range and uncertainty separate. Without the evidence to bound material adjustments, report unbounded/unknown instead of inventing an interval. Any claimed discount must survive plausible normalisation and costs; the interval method does not lower the original gate.

## 5. Claim and comparable ledger

Each material claim carries an ID, proposition, asset scope, buyer/horizon, source link, source origin/lineage, publication/event/retrieval dates, observed text/data, access method, interpretation, adjustment, uncertainty, gate and freshness trigger. An issuer report can establish declared completion while remaining insufficient to establish buyer preference; source authority is claim-specific.

For each comparable retain identity, original/effective price, area basis, floor/stack, layout, parking, condition, tenure/rights, motive, event date, inclusion role and exclusions. Do not average auction reserves, voluntary sales and developer packages. Duplicated sources count once. Unknown unit differences must appear in the valuation uncertainty rather than silently receiving zero adjustment.

Use versioned evidence cutoffs and recheck volatile availability, fees, lender terms and unit condition before action. Do not impose a universal freshness period on all data. A disappeared listing is neither an achieved sale nor a withdrawn thesis.

## 6. Falsifiable investigation and stopping rules

An existing Divergence Flag must include all four explanation families. For each distinguish **test planned**, **test attempted but unavailable**, **evidence obtained** and **conclusion**. Completing a form is not completing the investigation.

Before collecting outcome evidence, specify the unit/buyer/horizon, preferred and rival mechanisms, a discriminating observation, expected direction/range when defensible, source route, review date or dated event window, falsifier and decision consequence. Avoid unfalsifiable phrases such as 'at a sufficiently low price' unless an actual price boundary and buyer task are declared. Do not invent false statistical precision when evidence supports only a directional test.

Each active case gets at most three immediate decision-changing unknowns, with independent collection steps, effort/access limit and consequence of resolution. This is a focus limit, not permission to ignore other risks. If the required packet is not publicly available, identify the exact private/field evidence needed; do not send the user a generic task to gather everything. Third-party contacts and paid services require applicable authority.

At the predeclared checkpoint choose: advance investigation, event-watch, stop active research, or rerun affected gates. These are work-queue states, not new G9 decisions. Unresolved cases may remain Defer while active research stops. Reopen on identified evidence, not elapsed time alone. A confirmed structural failure can produce Reject independently of other unknowns; do not leave it in endless Defer.

## 7. Rank robustness and research value

State the target role and buyer before ranking. Show plausible input ranges and which changes reverse the order. No invented probability distribution is needed. A scenario grid is not a probability forecast.

Assess rent, fee, condition/capex, unit rights, effective entry and exit references together. A small model advantage inside the input uncertainty cannot establish strong superiority. Report stable dominance, conditional preference or unresolved tie in the relevant comparison; these are rank descriptions, not extra G9 labels. Never conceal a known disadvantage inside an unsupported weighted total.

Prioritise evidence likely to change a capital or rank decision, available through a credible route at proportionate effort. Current-shortfall cases remain the lowest priority until material evidence changes their status; a dramatic unverified catalyst story does not jump the queue.

## 8. Interpretation safeguards for existing Core notation

Core notation is explanatory unless a workpaper supplies measurable variables, compatible units and evidence-supported operations. Do not add a set of comparables to a monetary premium as arithmetic. Do not sum overlapping buyer pools as independent people. Separate buyer breadth from depth.

'No proven buyer transfer' means no underwriting credit yet, not proof that transfer is impossible. Recoverable share must have evidence plus costs/time/risks; a guessed percentage cannot establish alpha. Avoid double-counting one mechanism across rent, buyer depth and terminal premium. Show its separate cashflow/exit consequences and dependencies rather than adding several subjective scores.

## 9. Version, output and validation discipline

Every report declares master identifier/hash, SOP release, financial configuration, evidence cutoff, current/retrospective status, universe covered and decision role. Current release uses `KV-EXEC-2026-10-07` and `KV-FIN-90-4-35-v1`. Cases import common parameters; old reports retain old versions.

Validation distinguishes arithmetic correctness, decision-path consistency, practitioner review, historical replay and matured forward investment performance. Preserve existing historical scores with their original meanings. Logical fixtures, replayed known cases and a new model review cannot create an empirical hit rate.

Track false-positive purchases and false-negative exclusions as well as source errors. Record a missed opportunity only when availability, effective basis, then-knowable condition and comparable realised outcome support that conclusion. Multiple Defer outputs are not automatically good performance.

Follow the finite release check before regional scale-up: implement this standard; reconcile the full financial chain and run bounded pathway tests; freeze the regional coverage/reporting protocol. Notify the user before starting all-area underwriting. This release does not start it.

**Effective:** 12 November 2026  
**Authority:** The user approved all five proposed G0 execution refinements in this conversation.

Apply with the [master framework](../Residential_Investment_Framework.md), [Decision-proportionate evidence SOP](Decision_Proportionate_Evidence_2026-10-18.md) and [sequential funnel SOP](Funnel_Execution_Reporting_2026-10-16.md). ## Approved G0 interpretation

1. **Separate the reference product from the selected parcel.** G0 may establish a defensible market-price reference for a defined project/product cohort before an exact unit is chosen. A research conclusion at that scope can continue to G1. It does not pass G4 or establish an individual unit's price, condition or investment suitability. Once a specific unit is considered, assess its layout, floor, exposure, bay rights, condition and package at G4; route differences that change the comparison cohort back to G0.
2. **Use a supported interval, including a broad interval.** G0 does not require one precise estimate, complete removal of uncertainty or a fixed number of comparables. A single high-quality observation may be informative; repeated rows or copied publisher records are not independent evidence. Build the widest interval the observations and defensible adjustments support. Do not add arbitrary margins or haircuts to turn one point into a range. Mark unbounded material differences explicitly.
3. **G0 tests price-reference validity, not undervaluation.** A supported reference lets the analysis compare the stated price. It does not require the acquisition price to sit below the interval. A price within or above the reference can pass G0 and still show no recoverable discount or fail investment tests later. G5 remains after G0-G4. A cash-neutral threshold, target appreciation or rent-capitalised price is not a reference value.
4. **Route unknowns by the claim they affect.** Do not stop G0 solely because a selected unit's floor, view, exact bay use, furnishing, current rent, fee invoice or borrower approval is unknown. G4, G1-G3 and G7 govern those matters. Keep an item at G0 when its uncertainty changes the price cohort, legal/physical product identity, rights population, effective consideration or a material normalization adjustment. Carry both branches when their effect is bounded; keep G0 unresolved if a material adjustment or price branch remains unbounded.
5. **New projects may use triangulated references.** A pre-launch or new project without its own resale history may be compared using its effective stated package and clearly relevant delivered substitutes. Record the package's offer status, rebate/payer/timing and the substitutes' lifecycle, location, product, rights and supply differences. The launch/headline alone is not market-clearing evidence; future catalysts and appreciation targets cannot fill the gap.

## Minimum record and disposition

For the declared scope, retain: subject/product definition; source, event and access dates; price type; comparable cohort; observed values; supported adjustments; the resulting reference interval or reason it cannot be bounded; the strongest counterexample; and the claim the evidence supports. Repeated records from the same underlying event count once. Asking, achieved voluntary transfer, auction reserve/sale and effective developer package remain separately labelled.

A supported project/product interval may clear G0 for further research even while the exact parcel remains unknown. Record **“G0 pass — cohort reference only; G4 pending”** in that case. Do not describe it as a unit-level normalized value or a discount. If the research question itself depends on the selected unit's rights, lawful use or material physical condition to choose the cohort, G0 stays unresolved until that identity is sufficiently established.

Keep unresolved cases as G0 holds when the retained evidence still cannot identify an applicable cohort, reconcile material price branches, separate restricted/auction/commercial product from ordinary residential reference, or bound a material adjustment. No automatic promotion follows from missing data or a lack of contradiction.

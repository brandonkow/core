# Decision-proportionate evidence and gate execution

Effective: 18 October 2026, after the user's approval of the three execution diagnoses: research versus purchase evidence was conflated; unknowns were insufficiently differentiated by decision impact; and G0 was applied with excessive demands for price certainty.

This is a reusable SOP clarification, not a causal Core change, new gate, new G9 decision or numerical parameter revision. The single master, especially section 5.30 and G0-G9, remains authoritative. Read with [sequential funnel reporting](Funnel_Execution_Reporting_2026-10-16.md) and the [execution standard](../execution-release-2026-10-07/Decision_Execution_Standard.md). The latter already permits bounded estimates and justified price intervals. This document corrects implementation and reporting; it does not imply those safeguards were absent.

## 1. Declare the decision being made

Each workpaper states the asset scope (project, product cohort, marketed expression or selected parcel), evidence cutoff and immediate decision:
- product / living-quality comparison;
- research-priority choice;
- investment attractiveness at a stated effective price;
- executable G9 capital decision.

Do not require a signed purchase contract or borrower approval merely to compare products. Do not present a product preference or research-priority ranking as investment admission. A project-level reference cannot be copied to every unit without testing material differences.

There is one sequential G0-G9 admission process. Research annotations below do not create another funnel or allow later conditional diagnostics to pass an earlier unresolved gate. Report the actual passed prefix separately from the permitted research action. Only the existing four G9 decisions apply.

## 2. G0: supported interval, not perfect certainty

G0 can be supported by a defensible normalised clearing interval, with explicitly bounded residual uncertainty. Neither an executed purchase contract for the subject nor a fixed number of transactions is universally required.

Required record:
1. Defined subject scope and relevant comparable cohort; unresolved component/use/rights differences must not be silently merged.
2. Source provenance, event and access dates, independence and price type: asking, effective developer package, voluntary completed sale, auction reserve or auction sold.
3. Included/excluded comparisons; relevant size, condition, rights, bay, timing and package adjustments with their support.
4. Empirical observations, supported adjustments and residual uncertainty shown separately. Explain why the interval describes clearing rather than merely advertised supply.
5. The claim the interval can support, its limits, strongest contrary explanation and a reopening trigger.

Use public transaction evidence and other defensible triangulation where available; private evidence is not universally the only acceptable source. Source quality is claim-specific. An advertisement may establish a price at which stock is marketed, but not a completed sale. A proposed developer rebate may define an offer scenario without validating either its availability or its investment discount.

Reject false precision: do not invent an uncertainty percentage, haircut or minimum comparable count. A justified wide interval is preferable to an unsupported exact value. If the evidence cannot bound material normalisation, G0 remains unresolved. No discount is validated by price-policy approval, target appreciation, rental capitalisation or repeated asking prices alone.

A known current offer is not a mandatory prerequisite to estimating a project/product reference. Conversely, a defined offer does not by itself establish that reference. Distinguish the two.

## 3. Test the decision impact of each unknown

Use the established Supported / Bounded estimate / Unknown / Contrary evidence annotations, supplemented with:
- proposition and evidence;
- affected gate(s);
- plausible alternatives or evidence-supported range (or explicitly unbounded);
- which current conclusion may change;
- next resolving observation, responsible source and expiry/reopening condition.

Classify by consequence, not by how easy a document is to obtain:
- **Decision-changing now:** plausible alternatives reverse the claimed conclusion, invalidate the subject/comparable identity, or prevent a defensible reference. Hold that conclusion at its relevant gate. Continue useful conditional comparisons with both branches visible.
- **Bounded and non-decisive for the stated conclusion:** show why the conclusion survives the plausible adverse alternative, including interacting uncertainties where material. It need not stop that particular conclusion. Revisit if the price, scope or evidence changes.
- **Execution-dependent:** actual offer availability, contractual payer, lender offer or physical confirmation needed before commitment. Route to the relevant gate and retain a pre-commitment verification item. Do not let its absence automatically block unrelated research.
- **Unbounded:** do not label it immaterial. Restrict claims depending on it; specify a finite evidence task rather than multiplying scenarios.

An unknown can belong to more than one consequence category. A pre-commitment fact may also be decision-changing today. Known adverse evidence is not postponed merely because its original document would normally be collected later.

## 4. Keep requirements at their existing gates

| Requirement | Primary treatment |
|---|---|
| Price type, component, comparable rights and normalisation | G0; require enough identity to make the reference meaningful, not a full purchase file |
| Actual location mechanism and buyer task/depth | G1-G2; candidate mechanisms are hypotheses until sufficiently supported |
| Governance, funding, competing supply and operating quality | G3; current fee/works facts can also affect G0 normalisation and G7 funding |
| Unit plan, permanent exposures, permitted configuration and bay usability | G4; material differences that destroy comparability also affect G0 |
| Recoverable discount | G5 only after G0-G4 survive; no favourable branch selection to bypass them |
| Buyer alternatives, exit depth and resale burden | G6; asking-based exit target is not established market value |
| Actual lender terms, title/lease constraints and funded forced hold | G7; standard financing remains a comparison assumption |
| Actual capital, commitments and correlated opportunity cost | G8; do not demand private balance-sheet details for a project comparison |
| Capital action | G9; Deploy still needs sufficient asset and actual portfolio evidence |

A signed tenancy or invoice is valuable evidence, not a universal G0 document. Rental/fee uncertainty may still be decisive for income, valuation normalisation or survival; show that dependency. Never route a fact away merely to make a pass possible.

G4 is unit-specific. A project preference cannot silently pass G4. G7 and G8 cannot become actual financing/capital approval through a synthetic scenario. Independent structural failure and demonstrated allocation constraints retain their existing Reject / Preserve Capacity precedence.

## 5. Sequential decision and useful reporting

For each candidate show:
1. Best supported conclusion now, at its stated price/product scope.
2. Actual contiguous passed prefix and first unresolved gate, supported by a specific reason rather than a generic document checklist.
3. Conditional downstream findings and the strongest reversal condition.
4. At most three immediate decision-changing evidence tasks; preserve the full unresolved-risk ledger.
5. Pre-commitment confirmations that are not prerequisites to the narrower research conclusion.

An unchanged generic G0 STOP must not be copied indefinitely as if revalidated. Record whether the cause is missing normalised-reference support, contradictory identity/package, or only a historical unreviewed label. If not reassessed, say so. Do not infer pass from absence of a recorded contradiction.

Rank research where useful, explain conditional ties, and state price boundaries supported by the model. A cash-neutral entry threshold is not a fair-value interval or an automatic bid recommendation. A low-price or high-yield outlier does not displace the existing product-quality and buyer-task comparisons.

## 6. Migration and verification

Preserve all historical gate decisions, numerical inputs, financial parameters and validation records. Apply a dated overlay that identifies the old reason, evidence actually reviewed, revised routing, changed claim permissions and any genuine gate change. A policy clarification alone does not change a candidate's pass status.

Reassess the existing evidence before seeking new evidence. When a source-backed interval already suffices, document it and advance G0; do not wait for the entire purchase file. When it does not, identify the precise missing link. Avoid another broad portal sweep without a discriminating task.

Track both overconfident admissions and unnecessary research exclusions. An overbroad label is a process miss; it is not proof of a missed profitable investment. Realised outcome evidence is still required for an investment-performance miss.

No outreach, paid access, loan application, purchase or background monitoring is authorised by this SOP.

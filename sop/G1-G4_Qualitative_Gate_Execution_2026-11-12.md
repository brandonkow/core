## Approved G1-G4 interpretation

1. **G1 — one evidence-supported use/demand mechanism is enough to continue research.** State one concrete, falsifiable mechanism linked to an actual housing task or use, with supporting observation. Do not require proof of appreciation. A station, mall, brand, or amenity label by itself is not a market mechanism. A G1 pass identifies a reason to investigate; it does not establish demand depth, occupancy, rent collection, resale liquidity, or investment merit.
2. **G2 — one credible buyer path is enough.** The path may be an owner-occupier or an investor; both are not mandatory. Identify that buyer's plausible budget, housing/investment task and realistic alternatives. Do not convert tenants into owner-buyers without evidence, or investor rent into buyer capacity by assumption.
3. **G3 — unknown is not adverse evidence.** Missing management, maintenance, occupancy, or governance information is not itself proof of project failure. If an unknown could materially change use or exit, retain it as an unresolved risk and specify the resolving observation. It does not become a positive finding or automatic pass.
4. **G4 — separate cohort research from unit selection.** Earlier project/product research may use a defined cohort. At G4, inspect the selected unit's layout, floor/exposure, condition, lawful/usable rights, parking, access and permanent disutility where those can change its use, price or exit. Do not copy project averages onto the selected unit. Route a material identity or rights difference back to G0 where it changes the comparison cohort.

## Application record

For each gate, record the proposition, observations, interpretation, strongest falsifier, uncertainty that remains, and next gate. Use “pass to next gate” only for the passed sequential prefix. Keep project-level conclusions distinct from selected-unit admission. A pass under this supplement never changes G9 by itself.

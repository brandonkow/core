## A. Demand-origin accounting: who actually adds willingness to pay?

Separate **change in occupied dwelling demand**, **transactions reallocating existing households or ownership**, and **price-band expansion**. At each project/product cohort, classify the supported principal mechanism and overlapping channels transparently:

| Demand pathway | Observation needed | Common false inference |
|---|---|---|
| Local household upgrades / downsizes | Origin and intended next home, closed higher-utility transactions, released older stock | Each upgrade creates one *net additional* household |
| New independent households | Formation/independent occupancy evidence, product/budget fit | Population count directly equals new purchases |
| Net qualified in-migration | Net rather than gross moves, feasible finances and residence/tenancy duration | Every incoming employee is a private-home buyer |
| Cross-node buyer capture | Actual alternative areas shortlisted and completed comparable transfers | Travel proximity or launch price automatically changes prestige |
| Estate/replacement supply | Estate retain/rent/sell patterns and financeable bottom-of-chain buyers | Every inherited dwelling is a forced sale |
| Investor/corporate occupancy or resale | Collected rents, employer source, investor yield/financing and repeat trades | A rented parcel necessarily has a deep owner-occupier exit |

**Wealth support versus buyer-pool expansion.** Accumulated local equity/business capital can preserve a premium despite low population growth. Higher *future* prices require additional willingness to pay at the intended price tier; distinguish persistence from expansion. Ask whether the household upgrade chain closes at its lowest released-stock tier rather than creating stranded inventory. The inquiry may be qualitative when transfer-level origin information is unavailable; do not invent buyer shares.

**Cross-node demand is not automatically net metropolitan demand.** Capturing a buyer from a competing node can lift the subject's relative willingness-to-pay without increasing total Klang Valley households; examine where the rival node loses demand and whether the switch is repeatable. Do not infer legal rights, finance capacity, behaviour or project quality from nationality, ethnicity or an age label.

Route to existing **G1 (mechanism), G2 (buyer universe)** and **G6 (future buyer and substitute set)**. Record origin evidence, contradictory cases and the precise projected buyer-price tier.

## B. Forward product lifecycle and substitution at the proposed sale date

Test the subject **at intended exit age** against (i) current direct substitutes, (ii) documented future competing completions/incentives, and (iii) alternative locations, layouts and housing forms the *future marginal buyer* genuinely considers at the same total budget. Do not treat all nearby units as fungible or assume every new project is better.

Retain the existing product and governance tests: usable layout and circulation, parking rights, title/lease financeability, access friction versus residential penalty, privacy, permanent nuisances, lifts and M&E, fire systems, waterproofing/façade, repairs, insurance, MC/JMB collection and reserves, approved capital works and plausible special levies. Separate an individually renovated parcel from the quality and financing of its whole building.

**Forward competition countertest:** Would a substitutable newer asset at the future date offer equal/better effective lived utility at similar all-in quantum? If yes, what *evidence-backed* purchase discount or useful scarcity would protect the older subject? If no, identify which quality, size, access, management or finance advantages remain difficult to copy. No universal old-stock depreciation, modernity premium, parking quota or tenure cutoff. New-project area re-rating does not automatically transmit to legacy stock.

Route to existing **G3/G4 quality**, **G6 terminal competing set** and **G7 future capital works / funding**. Use supported intervals and scenario capex rather than invented premium percentages.

## C. Price formation: stickiness, resilience and liquidity are separate

Always preserve the existing **G0 normalised clearing price** distinction among developer headline/effective terms, asks, completed voluntary secondary transfers, auction reserve and auction sold. Do not compute a validated discount to launch SPA or a vendor's unexecuted asking anchor.

Keep three separate observations:

1. **Seller reservation / price stickiness:** owners withdraw or refuse lower bids.
2. **Repeat paid price resilience:** relevant, independently executed, condition-adjusted sales hold the same price band over time.
3. **Execution liquidity:** financeable competing buyers complete transactions in acceptable time and with tolerable price concessions.

No transactions can signify seller reluctance *or* absent buyers; it does not establish a stable clearing price. One unusually renovated premium sale does not revalue the whole stock. Check initial and final ask-to-close gaps, listing age, withdrawals/re-lists and material seller-related transactions where observable. Treat forced-sale completed prices as relevant executions under disclosed exposure, rights and condition, neither automatically normal nor automatically artificial.

**Value-bridge anti-double-count:** validated entry mispricing is the difference between the actual effective acquisition basis and the **current** supported normalised clearing range. If future terminal price is independently valued against future buyers and substitutes, do not add this entry discount again as an additional resale gain. A narrowing relative-value gap can be caused by the superior competitor falling, not by the subject rising; evaluate net absolute proceeds.

Route to existing **G0, G5** and **G6**; do not manufacture a single precise interval from unmatched projects or sparse mixed-product medians.

## D. Exit depth at each target *total quantum*

At each proposed selling tier identify the actual financeable target investor and/or owner-occupier buyer segments, competing resale/developer supply, normalised achieved sale levels and execution path. **Investor-led exits remain eligible** when net rent, sustainable investment yield, legal form and financeable purchase price support them. Neither a fashionable neighbourhood nor owner-occupier desirability establishes a mandatory owner-only exit.

Preferred *decision-proportionate* observations, if accessible:

- Comparable **completed** transfers near, below and above the target tier, with date, rights, condition and financing limits; distinguish a cohort reference from the selected parcel.
- Deduplicated actively competing **for-sale** units, withdrawn/re-listed adverts and developer incentive alternatives; keep denominator/time windows aligned.
- Comparable standing stock, where bounded; annual comparable turnover = confirmed comparable annual sales / comparable standing stock. Counts without a denominator do not support cross-market liquidity ranking.
- Indicative months of competing supply = credible active comparable sale listings / matched monthly completed sales; undefined if zero observations and unreliable with thin samples.
- Listing exposure through agreement/SPA, including unsold/censored listings, and both initial and final asking-price concessions where supported.
- Buyer qualification at proposed total price: deposit, financeable valuation, age/term, other debts and credible cross-housing-form substitutions. Never infer specific lender DSR approval without actual borrower/bank evidence.

State unknowns explicitly; a blank public field is not zero and not a permanent screening veto. Avoid mandatory two-/three-persona quotas. Strong current trading at one price tier does not prove depth at a higher terminal tier. Evaluate adverse **price *and time-to-sale*** branches with financed carrying obligations; no universal 12/24-month forecast, no invented scenario probabilities.

Route to existing **G2/G6 (buyer universe and exit)**, **G7 (terminal finance and hold)** and **G8 (opportunity cost)**. Use the approved **90% LTV, 4%, 35 years** standard only in its applicable comparison, with actual terms and financial SOP v2 lease-event logic in separate authentic/conditional cases.

## E. Evidence and decision reporting

For a material node, product or parcel hypothesis report: *mechanism → plausible contrary explanation → dated source and scope → observed price/rent status → eligible direct substitutes → future product competitiveness → supported current normalised interval → target-tier buyers/finance → holding and exit sensitivities → existing gate status / first unresolved gate / next material evidence*.

Preserve evidence and assessments with distinct dates and versions. A source from 2026 is not a live quote in 2027; new buyer-origin stories or area premiums are hypotheses until repeated validated observations. Previously assessed cases are **not automatically rerun, promoted or reclassified** because this addendum exists; the retained-record replay SOP governs any separately authorised calibration.

No new G10, Core principle, numerical return preset, area ranking, permanent lifestyle filter, automatic owner-only exit or alternative G9 action is introduced.

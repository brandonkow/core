# Layout-neutral regional sampling — approved execution clarification

Effective: 7 October 2026, after the first Klang Valley comparative release. Authority: the user's explicit instruction to include studios and one-bedroom units because two-/three-bedroom expressions may not satisfy the investment standard in some areas. English artifact; Mandarin discussion.

This is an execution clarification to the existing regional protocol and approved SOP. It changes candidate selection and coverage tracking, not the frozen Core, G0–G9, financial configuration or existing decisions. Read alongside [the execution standard](../execution-release-2026-10-07/Decision_Execution_Standard.md) and [the original regional protocol](../execution-release-2026-10-07/Regional_Underwriting_Protocol.md). Historical release and preregistration files retain their original wording and dates.

## Candidate universe and regional conclusions

Studios, genuine one-bedroom, two-bedroom, three-bedroom and larger residential high-rise expressions are eligible. No room count is the default definition of investability, owner appeal or product quality. Select by actual tenant/buyer task, entry quantum, sustainable ordinary rent, financeability, operations and exit mechanism.

Studios and one-bedroom units can be the first expressions investigated where evidence supports their buyer/tenant task. They are not merely fallback products after larger layouts fail. Conversely, lower total price and higher advertised gross yield do not automatically make them superior.

A two-/three-bedroom standard shortfall applies to the tested expression at its stated price/rent/fees. It cannot establish that the entire region lacks viable investments. Before making a region-wide conclusion, investigate material compact alternatives or explicitly record the missing coverage and restrict the conclusion. Do not force a studio case into a catchment where no relevant product/channel has been evidenced.

Maintain separate studio, one-bedroom, two-bedroom and three-bedroom/larger coverage fields for each catchment. Record identified, screened, conditional gate work, excluded with reason or not covered; absence of research is not absence of supply or demand. Keep historical findings and new observations separately dated.

## Original configuration and investment scope

- Studio: marketed open-plan living/sleeping configuration; record whether an original plan confirms the configuration.
- One bedroom: marketed separate bedroom; verify the original plan and usable room before adopting the label as established.
- Study, utility, enclosed balcony, partition or “1+1” does not automatically become a second original bedroom.
- Floor area alone cannot distinguish a studio from a one-bedroom unit. Do not reclassify an existing case without evidence.
- SOHO, SOFO, serviced apartment and hotel-suite marketing labels do not resolve the exact parcel's use, rights, permitted occupation/letting or financeability. Retain the existing residential high-rise mandate and investigate the component before admitting it.
- Whole-unit ordinary rent is the baseline. Room rents, short stays and accommodation packages require separate evidenced operating analysis; do not insert their gross receipts into a conventional whole-unit rental case.

These are reporting and verification rules, not new legal definitions or causal Core mechanisms.

## Equal underwriting standard

Every selected layout receives the same G0–G9 process and Evidence–Judgment Divergence control. Keep the user's comparison basis: 90% of acquisition price financed at 4% over 35 years, plus verified management fee; preferred gross rental yield 6%. Unverified combined management/sinking allowances remain labelled proxies. Actual bank valuation, lender margin and eligible term are separate financing constraints, not reasons to lower the comparison LTV.

Compare full costs and collected rent, vacancy/turnover, furnishing replacement, management/sinking, fees, works, entry equity, weak/delayed exit and holding capacity. Do not assume smaller units necessarily have greater turnover or weaker ownership demand; test those propositions for the specific cohort.

Identify primary and secondary tenants and resale buyers for the actual layout. Singles, couples, local owner-occupiers and income investors are distinct candidate channels to validate. Do not borrow growing-family demand for a studio, and do not reject an otherwise evidenced income-investor exit merely because it is not a family buyer. Test financed resale depth and resilience independently of tenant demand.

Current standard shortfall retains the lowest research priority under the existing transition protocol. A qualifying compact expression can earn higher investigation priority without erasing a larger unit's negative result. Positive coverage or 6% gross alone cannot clear missing gates.

## Comparative output and follow-through

Within each relevant catchment report the original layout/area, net entry-price evidence, rent basis, fees, standard coverage, full holding cashflow, tenant channel, exit buyer, actual-financing constraints and four separate conclusions. Compare total budget and functional substitutes; do not pool their PSFs or rents without justified adjustments.

For the next research tranche, actively test compact opportunities alongside the previously queued family countercases. Existing first-round one-bedroom cases remain conditional and do not constitute studio coverage. Reopen ranks only when new evidence or stated input revisions justify it. No case is promoted, rejected or reclassified by this scope clarification alone.

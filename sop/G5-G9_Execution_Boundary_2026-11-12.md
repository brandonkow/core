# G5-G9 execution boundaries — approved supplement

**Effective:** 12 November 2026  
**Authority:** The user approved all five G5-G9 application clarifications in this conversation.  
**Scope:** Application of existing G5-G9 only. This is an SOP supplement. The frozen Core, gate names and order, G9 decision set, and financial parameters remain unchanged. It does not reassess or promote any case.

Apply with the [master framework](../Residential_Investment_Framework.md), [Decision-proportionate evidence SOP](Decision_Proportionate_Evidence_2026-10-18.md), [sequential funnel SOP](Funnel_Execution_Reporting_2026-10-16.md), [Decision Execution Standard](../execution-release-2026-10-07/Decision_Execution_Standard.md), and current [Financial Execution SOP v2](Financial_Execution_SOP_v2.md). The master remains authoritative for Core mechanisms and gate definitions. Financial SOP v2 governs current reusable financial assumptions and stress magnitudes for new or materially reopened cases.

## Shared application rule

Keep one sequential G0-G9 admission funnel. A downstream calculation may be used as a clearly labelled conditional diagnostic while an earlier gate is unresolved, but it cannot clear that gate or enter the candidate into a final eligible shortlist. Report the passed contiguous prefix and first unresolved or failed gate.

For each G5-G9 conclusion, record the decision-relevant proposition, evidence and dates, interpretation, strongest counterexample, material unknowns, and the consequence for the next gate or G9. Use evidence statuses such as Supported, Bounded estimate, Unknown, and Contrary evidence. Do not create minimum comparable counts, return cutoffs, or other numerical pass rules that the approved sources do not set.

## Approved G5-G9 interpretation

1. **G5 — distinguish a supported entry-price thesis from a claimed discount.** Assess G5 only after G0-G4 survive. If the thesis claims mispricing, establish the applicable normalised clearing reference, effective acquisition consideration, reason for the gap, evidence-supported reversible share, and recovery costs, time and risks. Do not assign a guessed recoverable percentage, treat a target appreciation scenario as evidence, or call a developer headline a discount. A fair-priced quality or utility thesis may still be assessed without manufacturing a discount; record explicitly that no discount is claimed. A Special Situation role still requires a validated, recoverable discount. Earlier conditional price work does not pass G5.

2. **G6 — establish a realistic primary exit path and test its failure.** Identify the future buyer and housing/investment task, plausible budget and alternatives, future comparable set, exit timing, financeability, required price and resale burden. A required exit price is a condition to be supported, not a forecast. Test a weak-exit and no-uplift case, and ask whether the thesis survives if its strongest support disappears. Do not impose an arbitrary minimum number of buyer groups or exit routes. If the path depends on one buyer or catalyst, flag Single-Point Exit Failure; this flag alone is not an automatic Reject. An unsupported primary exit path remains unresolved at G6; an established structural exit failure may support Reject under existing G9 rules.

3. **G7 — keep standard financing, actual financing and forced-hold capacity separate.** Standard comparison assumptions are not a lender approval, verified borrower affordability, or proof that the selected unit qualifies for that financing. Where material, assess actual lender/title/lease restrictions, bank valuation, costs and works, the marginal future buyer's financeability, and evidence of resources for a forced hold. A stress loss is not by itself a structural failure or automatic Reject. Treat a decision-critical funding or terminal-risk unknown as unresolved; route an established structural failure through the existing G9 precedence.

   For future or materially reopened work, use the current Financial Execution SOP v2 stress register. It supersedes conflicting inherited numerical stress magnitudes, including older examples in the master SOP diagnostics. Do not restore removed stress cases. Add another sensitivity only when decision-relevant evidence justifies it; label the evidence, timing and decision effect.

4. **G8 — label synthetic allocation work; reserve actual clearance for actual constraints.** A hypothetical portfolio comparison is a research diagnostic, not an actual G8 pass. An executable capital decision requires relevant actual mandate, capital, liabilities/commitments, liquidity, financing capacity, correlated exposures and opportunity cost. Do not request private balance-sheet details merely to continue area or product research. Preserve Capacity requires a demonstrated allocation constraint or opportunity cost; where other gates are unresolved, state clearly that this does not pass them.

5. **G9 — apply only the four existing capital decisions.**  
   - **Deploy:** G0-G8 sufficiently support the asset and actual portfolio decision, with a declared role. Any remaining uncertainty must be compatible with the supported margin of safety and funding plan; unresolved divergence is not positive evidence.  
   - **Defer:** a decision-relevant gate or evidence item remains unresolved. Active research may stop with a finite reopening trigger; Defer is not permission to leave a confirmed structural failure unresolved indefinitely.  
   - **Reject:** an established structural Gate failure supports rejection. Missing evidence, a negative cashflow result, an unvalidated catalyst, or an unresolved divergence alone is not structural proof.  
   - **Preserve Capacity:** the asset may be acceptable, but a demonstrated actual allocation constraint or opportunity cost makes consuming the capital inferior. This label does not imply that unresolved asset gates pass.

   Report product preference, attractiveness at the stated price, research priority and G9 decision separately. A G9 recommendation is an analytical capital decision, not authority to purchase, contact a third party or execute a transaction.

## Minimum reporting for each gate

For G5-G8, show:
- the current conclusion and its scope;
- the actual passed prefix and first unresolved gate;
- conditional downstream results separately from admission;
- the strongest reversal condition;
- at most three immediate decision-changing evidence tasks, with source route and consequence;
- actual, standard and synthetic inputs distinctly where applicable.

A G5-G9 pass under this supplement does not by itself change the candidate's previous G9 decision. Case replay requires a separately scoped instruction and retained-evidence process.

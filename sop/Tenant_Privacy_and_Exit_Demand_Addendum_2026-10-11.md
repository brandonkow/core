# Tenant privacy, household adoption and exit demand - execution addendum

Effective 11 October 2026. Authority: the user's three market Red Team answers and express instruction to incorporate the insight into future decisions. Read with the [single master](../Residential_Investment_Framework.md), [approved execution supplement](Approved_Execution_Supplement.md), [layout-neutral sampling](Layout_Neutral_Regional_Sampling.md) and [execution standard](../execution-release-2026-10-07/Decision_Execution_Standard.md).

This is a reusable SOP addition applying existing mechanisms, not a new causal Core principle, gate, score or screening veto. The master, G0-G9, existing SOP releases, financial engine and historical cases remain unchanged. Populated cases are in the [dated research packet](../klang-valley-2026-10-07/round-3/market-judgment-2026-10-11/README.md).

## 1. Identify the accommodation task before comparing rent

For every new or materially reopened case, record the target tenant's actual accommodation task and feasible substitutes. Separate ability to pay from willingness to pay for a particular living arrangement. Similar incomes need not produce identical housing choices, but a claimed income match or neighbourhood preference must be evidenced rather than assumed.

Test catchment-specific demand for an entire household unit, exclusive self-contained compact, room with private bathroom, room with shared facilities, and other relevant lawful operating forms. Select alternatives on total recurring housing cost, practical journeys, privacy and household needs. Area reputation and cultural narratives are hypotheses about behaviour, not permanent attributes of every resident.

"Privacy" requires an operational description: entrance and circulation; sharing with unrelated residents; bathroom, kitchen and laundry exclusivity; visual/acoustic separation; visitor and occupancy arrangements; usable parking; utility inclusion and metering. A room with an en-suite is not automatically a self-contained dwelling. A household renting a two-bedroom unit can have greater privacy than strangers separately renting its rooms.

Studios and one-, two-, three-bedroom and larger products remain eligible. No universal studio, dual-key, family or area bonus. An original dual-key parcel, an independently titled unit and an owner-created partition are different legal and operating objects. Verify approved form, actual configuration, use/rules and financing before using the proposed income model; do not infer permission from advertisements.

## 2. Test a privacy premium on matched evidence

The testable proposition is that a defined tenant cohort pays more, rents faster or stays longer for a defined increase in privacy, after controlling for package and alternatives. Specify the comparison and rival explanation before collecting more evidence.

Match dates, micro-location, area, usable space, original configuration, condition, furnishing, bays, utilities, tenancy duration and service package. Compare both the tenant's all-in bill and the owner's collected net income. Total rent, rent per square foot, acquisition yield and return on additional fit-out capital are distinct measures.

Record advertised, agreed, collected and renewed rent separately. Use independent, deduplicated observations with dates and denominators. Repeated advertisements for one unit are not independent demand. A delisted advertisement is not proof of letting; a room listing is not proof of partitioning or district dominance. Prefer matched within-project or within-catchment controls for valuation; cross-region alternatives compete for capital without becoming valuation comparables.

For split operations, reconcile income and costs to the entire purchased parcel. Count vacant components, concessions, bundled utilities, agent/operator charges, cleaning, turnover, fit-out replacement and increased wear. Do not sum full asking room rents at assumed continuous occupancy and call that an achieved whole-unit lease. Do not claim a numerical privacy premium while the matched observations are missing.

Retain the ordinary-tenancy downside and a no-premium case. Only a supported change in collected rent, voids, costs or recoverable exit value may enter a new financial version. Re-run affected financial, terminal and portfolio checks at that point.

## 3. Separate family-capable layout from demonstrated family adoption

Maintain distinct fields for physical family suitability, actual household tenancy, renewals, owner-occupier willingness and financeable resale demand. Size or bedroom count alone establishes none of the latter. A large unit can serve shared rental demand; a small unit can serve an entire household.

Investigate the practical school/work/amenity routine, usable parking, circulation, noise, privacy, maintenance and competing housing budgets. School presence supports an ecosystem hypothesis; it does not allocate families to every nearby project. Family tenancy does not prove family purchase demand or affordability.

Management quality must be supported by service outcomes and governance evidence. Expectations may create incentives for better service, but they do not establish that the response occurs or is adequately funded. Separate ability, funding and results, as already required by the approved supplement. Nationality is not a proxy for resident quality, payment behaviour or sound management.

Similar building ages do not cancel repair exposure. Keep condition, reserves, arrears, planned works and special assessments separate from demand preference. Strong household appeal can coexist with an unattractive acquisition price or unfunded capital works.

## 4. Separate rental income from investor exit depth

Equal current or assumed rent does not imply equal resale appeal. Test how repeatable that income is, how concentrated its sources are, and whether the future investor can finance the purchase at a defensible price. Investor-led exits remain valid without inventing a mandatory owner-occupier exit.

Record actual tenant-demand sources, dependence on individual institutions/employers, seasonality, turnover, competing accommodation, ordinary collections and demonstrated replacement demand. A mixed neighbourhood is not proof that the subject project captures diverse tenants. A university nearby does not prove exclusive student dependence.

Treat rail and other anchors as a transmission question: door-to-platform or door-to-destination route, access restrictions, walking conditions, transfers, service hours, total time/cost and actual tenant use. A station in an area or a straight-line distance is insufficient. Test rival projects' access with the same method.

Keep observed buyer demand separate from an assumed exit yield. If testing investor yield sensitivities, consistently match gross rent with gross yield or net income with net yield, and label the result a conditional capitalisation sensitivity rather than a validated clearing price. No arbitrary "diversity premium", rent uplift or exit-yield reduction is permitted.

## 5. Investigation and decision control

At each relevant gate, test whether evidence and informed judgment materially conflict on the same proposition. Alignment, missing evidence and hypotheses about another outcome are not automatically divergence. For example, cheaper entry and stronger possible resale appeal can both be true.

When a material same-proposition conflict exists, use the master's **DIVERGENCE FLAG** template and test all four explanation families: data limitation, judgment bias, omitted variable and structural change. Record observed evidence, contradicting judgment, alternatives, falsifiable hypothesis, evidence required, falsifiers, existing-mechanism routing and Resolved / Partially Resolved / Unresolved status. Do not use a favourable anecdote or agreement to close it.

Every informed intuition influencing the case must be translated into a falsifiable mechanism. Test the strongest rival account, including that the apparent premium is furnishing, tenant selection, unrecorded services, lifecycle timing or analyst preference. Missing evidence is inconclusive, not automatically a falsifier.

Unresolved material uncertainty increases the evidence burden and required margin of safety, and reduces conviction. It cannot waive a gate or independently justify investment. Keep "research first" separate from "buy first". Preserve G0 stops and the four existing G9 decisions.

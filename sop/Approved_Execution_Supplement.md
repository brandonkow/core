## 1. Standard property cash-coverage test

Run this comparable property-level test before investor-specific affordability. A higher personal salary or a willingness to subsidise the asset cannot turn a shortfall into a pass.

User-approved definition:

`Standard monthly coverage = monthly whole-unit rent - mortgage instalment on 90% of acquisition price at 4% annual interest over 35 years - monthly management fee`

- Positive: rent covers the defined recurring payment with a surplus.
- Zero: break-even under the defined standard.
- Negative: shortfall under the defined standard.
- Missing reliable rent, loan basis or fee: unverified result; an assumed-input calculation is explicitly scenario-only.

Use full principal-and-interest instalments, not interest alone. With loan principal L and monthly rate r = 0.04/12:

`Instalment = L*r / [1 - (1+r)^(-420)]`

Declare the loan principal, acquisition price, valuation basis and LTV beside every result. The user's subsequent instruction on 6 October 2026 fixes 90% LTV for all future baseline comparisons: standard principal is 90% of acquisition price. Keep this same basis across candidates; do not lower leverage to make a favoured property pass. Other LTVs may appear only as explicitly labelled actual-financing or stress sensitivities, never as a substitute baseline. A lower bank valuation, lender margin or shorter eligible term requires a separate actual-financing cash requirement and stress assessment. The 90%/4%/35-year convention is an analytical standard, not a promise of lender eligibility.

Use the verified management charge and state what it includes. Identify sinking-fund contributions separately where charged separately; do not silently omit them from complete costs or claim that the user explicitly included them in the narrower definition. If only a combined service/sinking allowance is available, label it as a proxy and keep the pure management-fee result unverified until disaggregated.

Present three distinct lines:

1. Standard monthly coverage, using the approved definition.
2. Cost-complete operating/holding cashflow, with vacancy, rent collection, management, sinking fund, maintenance, insurance, taxes, letting/turnover and other applicable costs; disclose estimates and exclusions.
3. Actual investor and stress affordability, using realistic finance terms, reserves, liabilities and the existing G7/G8 stress requirements.

Do not call line 1 a net profit or a stress pass. Do not redefine line 1 to include every line-2 item. Passing line 1 cannot clear unknown G0-G4 evidence or authorise Deploy. A shortfall must be visible in G7/G8 and the decision rationale; personal wealth cannot erase it. It is not, by itself, proof of a structural asset failure requiring Reject. Any proposed exception to the screening standard must be explicitly identified and cannot be inferred from the old interview's negative-cashflow allowances.

User clarification, 7 October 2026: retain current-shortfall properties as research candidates but put them in the lowest priority tier. Ordinary income investment defaults to non-negative standard coverage at entry, with evidence-led flexibility rather than an absolute permanent ban. A proposed exception must document the rent-transmission mechanism, additional evidence, timing, funded carry, delay/no-uplift scenario and falsifiers under the new execution standard. Forecast rent does not replace the current negative number. Market-driven revisions remain possible and versioned; no causal Core rule is changed here.

## 2. Gross rental yield preference

`Gross rental yield = 12 * monthly whole-unit rent / acquisition price`

The user prefers approximately 6% gross rental yield for a good investment project. Record this as a preferred target, not an automatic rejection threshold, a guaranteed result, a net yield or a forecast. Display the actual unrounded calculation when testing whether 6% is reached; rounding 5.9% to 6% must not manufacture attainment.

Also show net operating yield and investment returns with explicit denominators, costs, financing and holding periods. Acquisition-price gross yield and all-in-cost yield are different measures. Historical 6% net ROI and 10% first-year appreciation answers are not adopted as current targets or model inputs. Never increase rent to make the target hold.

## 3. Evidence, judgment and decision quality

Apply the existing Divergence control when a material conflict arises. Site observations can challenge a data interpretation by exposing a specific omission or error; neither a number nor a feeling receives unconditional priority. Convert a preference-based objection into a target-buyer hypothesis before drawing a market conclusion.

Record decision process, causal mechanism and realised outcome separately. A profitable outcome does not prove the process sound; an adverse outcome does not alone prove the original process unsound. Record mistakes and missing mechanisms even when the eventual investment makes money. Following a framework never guarantees an error-free result.

Distinguish the investor's personal refusal to own an asset from demonstrated rejection by its target buyers. Do not transform the founder's taste into a permanent product filter. Compare relevant one-, two- and three-bedroom products where buyer tasks overlap. Do not make family-sized or three-bedroom stock the default definition of investable owner appeal. Explicitly report sampling gaps and rank only within the researched universe.

## 4. Field and governance evidence packet

Use the useful observational detail in EX-35-62 to execute existing G3/G4, not to create a new quality theory. For each observation retain date, time, location/tower, observer/source, method, observed fact, interpretation, alternative explanation, affected buyer task and affected gate.

| Field | Record | Do not infer automatically |
|---|---|---|
| Arrival/security/lobby | Actual access procedure, service behaviour, circulation and usable waiting space | Attractive lobby means sound governance |
| Lifts | Waiting observations at stated times, lift zone, operating cars and interruptions | One quiet visit proves peak-hour adequacy |
| Parking | Exact legal bays, dimensions, clearance, ramps, lighting and route | Advertised two bays means two usable independent bays |
| Corridors/refuse | Width, rain exposure, ventilation, odour, maintenance and unit adjacency | A clean marketing photograph proves ordinary conditions |
| Facilities | Condition, use/loading and maintenance evidence | Variety of facilities offsets all operational crowding |
| Management response | Response timing, substantive answer, resolution and follow-through | Fast replies establish audited financial health |
| Governance/funding | Collection and arrears, reserves, approved/committed works, completion records and service continuity | Good intentions mean sufficient funding; complaints alone prove collapse |

Separate management ability, financial capacity and actual outcomes. Distinguish normal wear from consequential degradation using appropriate evidence, not age alone. Apply the existing severity ladder. Use observed conduct, payment/compliance records and operating loads rather than treating income, occupation or nationality as intrinsic resident quality.

Public records and remote evidence can support desk investigation. Do not claim a visit, engineering inspection or private-account review that did not occur. An unavailable field item remains unknown and can block a strong unit recommendation. Research independently where possible; do not turn this checklist into a requirement that the user supply every answer. External contacts, paid services and actions on a property require applicable authorisation.

## 5. Cost and improvement budget

Count applicable acquisition, financing, legal, refurbishment, holding and exit costs. A mandatory or financed cost is still a cost. Avoid double-counting loan principal; separate economic return from cash movements and borrowing proceeds.

Split proposed works into basic repair, rental configuration and resale improvement. The historical RM20k and 5%-of-price figures are reference points only, not newly approved universal ceilings. Identify the target user, itemised scope, incremental cost, useful life, expected rent/absorption/resale effect and evidence supporting it. Test a lower-cost alternative and a no-premium outcome. Different personal design preferences do not establish recoverable resale value.

## 6. Holding and post-purchase review

Declare planned hold, affordable forced-hold duration and thesis-review points separately. Do not import a universal three-, five-, seven- or eight-year rule. An extended hold must be justified by current evidence and funded carrying capacity, not merely by a desire to avoid recognising a failed thesis.

At tenancy renewal or a material event, compare actual collections, vacancy, costs, condition, tenant conduct, competing offers, financeability and prospective exits with the original case. Event triggers include arrears, sustained enquiry/conversion change, governance deterioration, major works, competing supply delivery and changes in credit or the expected buyer pool. No unattended monitoring is implied.

When rent is weak, investigate asking level, package, condition, marketing coverage, seasonality and competing inventory before declaring structural decline. When deciding whether to reduce rent or wait, compare expected collected income and incremental vacancy/carry using explicit assumptions; do not automatically preserve the old rent or accept any tenant. Re-underwrite G1-G8 and record a G9 capital decision. Holding, rent-setting and sale execution actions are operating actions, not additional G9 categories.

## 7. Sourcing, transaction and supply controls

Seller urgency can motivate an offer but does not prove undervaluation or a clean transaction. Agent contact frequency, public advertising and promotional excitement are weak signals with competing explanations. Verify identity, availability, comparable consideration and seller/transaction constraints before an offer rationale. A deadline cannot waive evidence gates.

Separate genuine refinancing/equity release from a marked-up consideration claim. Count borrowing as debt, not investment profit. Evaluate actual loan terms, cash retained, simultaneous portfolio stress, correlation and management capacity before any additional acquisition. Cheap prices, property counts and spare lender capacity cannot establish safe expansion.

Record responsible parties, required documents, unresolved issues, financing/valuation conditions and transaction milestones. Legal, lender and contractual assertions in the historical interview remain unverified until checked against current authoritative evidence and the actual transaction. Do not turn old generic answers into legal or lending rules.

Track the demand funnel: attention, enquiry, viewing, reservation, financing, completed sale/tenancy and cancellation. Sales-gallery crowds or enquiries alone establish neither completed demand nor its absence. Deduplicate and identify the relevant denominator before interpreting conversion.

Track competing supply before VP, separating announcements, credible delivery, actual release and effective competition. Compare actual buyer tasks, packages, prices and operations. Density counts and nearby new launches trigger investigation; they do not create automatic Reject. Test both substitution and complementarity. Market distress, developer reputation or a catalyst never substitutes for recoverability and financing evidence.

## 8. Required case output and routing

Every new or materially revised case records:

- approved 4%/35-year monthly coverage, loan basis, fee scope and evidence status;
- gross yield versus the preferred 6% target, separately from complete costs and return;
- relevant field/governance gaps, alternative explanations and independent buyer evidence;
- planned and forced holding periods, review triggers and failure conditions;
- source question IDs for any imported Founder observation, and whether it is observed, hypothetical, historical preference or assistant interpretation;
- effects on existing gates, confidence, rank and the four permitted G9 decisions.

Route price/source checks to G0/G5, demand and supply to G1-G3, irreversible field conditions to G4, future buyers and improvement recovery to G6, finance/holding risk to G7 and allocation to G8. No score, yield target, site impression or approved SOP can override an unresolved required gate. Use the existing Core promotion threshold for any later genuinely new causal proposal.

## Boundary-scenario walkthrough

These are anonymous logical checks against the written control, not market case studies, executable software tests or claims of investment accuracy. No test below is inserted into the master as a historical example.

| Scenario | Required result | Review outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Positive evidence and judgment, but title/governance evidence missing | Continue normal underwriting, no automatic Deploy | Consistent with four-state table and G9 precedence |
| Negative evidence and judgment, but no established structural failure | Deprioritise or Defer as appropriate; do not force structural Reject | Consistent with explicit negative-alignment boundary |
| Weak resale data and positive informed supply-normalisation judgment | Material flag; investigate A-D, predeclare falsifiers, resolve G0 before a discount claim | Consistent with hypothesis and routing rules |
| Strong headline liquidity and negative informed future-buyer judgment | Equal attention flag; test motive, financeable depth, substitutes and exit; do not dismiss concern or fabricate a failure | Consistent with hidden-risk and G2/G6 routes |
| Unknown price evidence and no contrary observation | UNKNOWN, not a fabricated Negative/Positive conflict; retain ordinary gate stop | Consistent with materiality and polarity definitions |
| Positive rent and weak ownership preference concern different propositions | Identify the inferential bridge; flag only if a material investment interpretation is in conflict | Consistent with proposition and materiality controls |
| Duplicate/stale data corrected | May resolve measurement disagreement; rerun affected gates; correction alone does not establish investable mispricing | Consistent with A and resolution rules |
| Preferred judgment disproven by discriminating evidence | Withdraw/narrow it, preserve audit trail, reroute and re-evaluate rank/decision | Consistent with bias, falsification and closure rules |
| Structural change announced but not transmitted into utility/adoption | Keep future explanation conditional and test delay/already-priced risk | Consistent with D and existing transmission mechanisms |
| Unresolved material conflict with unknown G0, offered deeper discount | Higher required protection but no invented normalised margin; Defer if required gate unresolved | Consistent with unresolved treatment |
| Independently proven critical structural failure despite optimistic judgment | Existing Reject gate can apply; a flag, low price or smaller position cannot waive it | Consistent with non-override safeguards |
| Asset evidence unresolved but a clear capital constraint exists | Preserve Capacity may independently apply; no false claim that asset gates passed | Consistent with G8/G9 and conditional diagnostics |

No scenario licenses “trust intuition and invest.” Partial resolution retains residual uncertainty. Later material evidence reopens a resolved flag. A material flag is never closed by user agreement, elapsed time or rising asking prices alone.

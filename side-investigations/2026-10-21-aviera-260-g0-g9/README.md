## Falsification, divergence and next steps

Hypothesis: a sufficiently deep carless-single tenant cohort pays for independent private functionality and useful access, allowing this small no-bay suite to sustain rent/retention needed for full costs. Confirm with matched collected packages, ordinary-tenancy use, actual fee and retention. Falsify with materially lower package-adjusted lettings, defective daily route, absent essential private functionality, prohibitive operating/use constraints or persistently weak replacement demand. Missing data alone is inconclusive.

Four rival explanations were tested: data limitations (asks versus collections, component mix); analyst bias (early-bird/zero-entry anchoring); omitted variables (privacy, bays, tariffs, lender/management); structural change (delivery/access and supply byVP). None independently validates mispricing. No established same-proposition conflict is asserted at this cutoff; unknown rights/fees are not negative evidence. Monthly coverage versus full cash are different measures, not invented market divergence. Existing DIVERGENCE FLAG remains ready if actual evidence materially contradicts informed judgment.

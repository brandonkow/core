## Decision in practical terms

If one Brightwood expression deserves the next focused evidence effort, select **standard D at the lowest executable nett/gross package**, subject to a genuinely private and permissible delivered split. It has the strongest tested income expression within this project. This is not a purchase recommendation or a declaration that all compact dual-key products dominate families.

Keep **B with two legal usable bays** as the flexibility challenger, only if that actual configuration exists at a competitive package. Its whole-unit fallback is more conventional, but2,300 working rent produces a monthly shortfall and approximately9,146 annual cash drain. A two-bay B may require a higher floor/package price; the minimum is not assumed available with two bays. Do not make B's three-bedroom label stand in for useful family accommodation.

The preference can reverse: at equal2,600 rent, D's annual cash advantage is only275 while B's assumed lower fit-out gives a lower recovery exit. D2,200 versus B2,600 reverses the income ranking too. D is first because its two-suite mechanism is the most relevant hypothesis to test, not because its overall investment superiority is established.

A/C have clearer family capability but higher capital and income burdens. They are lower ordinary-income research priority, not proven structurally bad investments. PES variants add capital and outdoor maintenance/utility uncertainty without an evidenced income premium; their nett terms are unknown. No preferred PES variant is selected.

## Why Defer rather than the other three outputs

- **Deploy:** unavailable. G0 clearing reference, G4 exact product, realistic income/delivery costs, funding and exit evidence are not cleared.
- **Defer:** appropriate. A real privacy-income mechanism may work, and stronger matched evidence could change the result. Working cashflow and exit economics are currently insufficient.
- **Reject:** not established. Indicative plan deficiencies, unknown management and current shortfall are not proven irreparable structural failures.
- **Preserve Capacity:** describes the prudent present behaviour, but is not substituted for the case's formal Defer. Actual portfolio constraints have not been supplied or authenticated.

The decision does not depend on further questionnaire approval, nor on a repeated project-identity investigation. Routine facts remain analyst-owned.

## What could change my conclusion

The most positive credible change would be demonstrably full private functionality, competitive tenant total cost, verified rent/renewal comparables around or above the complete annual threshold with a real buffer, and a credible investor resale at the required quantum. Lower independently evidenced costs or a lower gross/debt basis could also help. A nett-only rebate does not lower gross-based monthly debt.

The most damaging change would be room-like tenant experience priced as a studio, inadequate second-tenant parking/access, underfunded service delivery, correlated VP rental competition or a required exit supported only by speculative appreciation.

## What the actual drawings support

P01 was text-extracted in full and relevant layouts/site/floor plans were rendered and visually inspected. Rendered visuals, not text extraction alone, support these observations:

- D, page29: two kitchen labels, two bathrooms and two balconies. A door is drawn into the left suite. The right kitchen/room is open to the shared entrance foyer; a second independently enclosed entrance arrangement is not established by this drawing. This is a promising dual-living concept, not proof of two fully private delivered studios. Confirm actual partitions/locks, acoustic separation, ventilation, cooking provision, utilities, laundry, permission and escape arrangements. No engineering or legal noncompliance is inferred from an indicative drawing.
- B, page26: three bedrooms/two bathrooms with a conventional shared living/kitchen arrangement. The image does not establish two independent studio kitchens. Model ordinary whole-unit use; do not silently add a dual-studio premium or an extra permitted bedroom.
- A/C, pages24/27: family-oriented three-room layouts with conventional shared living. Larger area and two bays improve the potential task fit; they do not establish family adoption or price appreciation.
- Typical floors, pages12/13: 12 parcels per floor; 17 residential floors give204. The core shows two ordinary lift labels and a fire-lift label. Operating capacity, waiting time and everyday availability are not authenticated. Multiple tenancies can make actual load greater than a parcel count suggests.
- D(A), page30, adds accessory outdoor area and a second bay at a higher gross budget. Its enclosure/terrace relationship requires a separate privacy check; no outdoor-area rent premium is booked.

## Admission result

A product-level conditional case can be analysed now. No exact unit is selected; no normalised clearing-price interval, permissible delivered split, contractual size, bay schedule, actual bank offer or complete package is authenticated. Therefore G0 remains STOP and G4 is not cleared. Later stages are explicit conditional diagnostics, not a bypass of gate order.

A prelaunch case does not need impossible future MC history to be studied: substitute a costed operating/reserve budget, enforceable specification, appropriately matched comparable leases, relevant completed-project delivery evidence and a funded delay case.

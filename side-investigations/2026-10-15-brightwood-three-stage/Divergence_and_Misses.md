## Interpretation discipline

BW-DIV-01 is populated in Stage2 and remains Unresolved. Both hidden mispricing and hidden structural risk are investigated through four explanation families. Unknown current management or unknown rent alone is not a negative fact or a new Divergence Flag.

Counterexamples do not prove that Brightwood is bad: a small new private suite can outperform a larger older unit for a specific tenant task. Conversely, a successful project-level launch-to-resale trajectory does not guarantee this purchaser's profit after construction interest, fit-out, vacancies and exit costs.

No achieved rent, site visit, bank approval, contractual product audit, complete local-supply census or realised investment outcome was obtained. The research does establish the questions that materially move the decision and their numerical thresholds. Further generic listing searches cannot turn those unknowns into verified facts.

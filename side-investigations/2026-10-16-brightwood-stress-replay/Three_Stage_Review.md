## Material divergence: BW-DIV-01 updated, still Unresolved

Calibrated arithmetic shows headline nett yields above7%, yet annual cash is D-negative and B only about513positive before additional bills. B's whole-unit fallback at2,500 does not reliably recover modelled costs. These financial outputs are conditional calculations, not observed market rent.

**Contradicting Judgment / Observation:** Informed market judgment expects compact privacy and separate household tasks to monetise the project sufficiently for a good investment. The unresolved proposition is the strength and robustness of that return, not whether privacy has any value.

**Possible Explanations:**
1. **Data Limitation:** actual package coverage, dated draw schedule and invoice timing may improve or worsen results; current rent substitutes are unmatched and future incomes unknowable. A small data set cannot validate a premium.
2. **Judgment Bias:** two-income framing and zero-downpayment framing may overstate return; analyst cost pessimism may also unfairly penalise a legitimately covered package. Model both explicit fee branches and retain the strongest whole-unit counterexample.
3. **Omitted Variable:** effective independent privacy, bay usability, operating permissions, renewal and management may change rent/void/costs. B's feasible split versus room-only experience is economically material.
4. **Structural Change:** delivery may improve local product choice or create simultaneous competing supply. Current rent carried forward has no growth, but future affordability, employment or competing supply could change. No uplift is booked without a transmission case.

**Falsifiable Hypothesis:** B can execute the proposed privacy/household split within the approved budget and sustain combined3,200ordinary rent with replacement/commission costs close to the calibrated calendar, leaving a financeable investor exit near the target price.

**Evidence Required:** matched contemporary accommodation evidence; detailed permitted layout/services and works specification; itemised buyer-paid package and loan offer; explicit construction billing and exit-substitute/funding comparison. After VP, update with collections, actual voids/renewals, AGM charges and actual buyer responses.

**Falsification Conditions:** lawful/practical split cannot be delivered; the small component competes only as a substantially cheaper room; required combined rent persistently misses the model; materially greater owner costs erase margin; comparable investor budgets cannot support the assumed exit. Missing evidence alone is inconclusive, not proof of failure.

**Core Module Routing:** Buyer Universe, Asset Fit, Comparable Set, Supply Structure, Lifecycle, Governance, Financing/Terminal Risk, Exit Architecture; existingG0-G8.

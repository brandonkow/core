## Interpretation

B first for conditional investment research; D is weaker on annual carry and lacks a calibrated alternative operation. At the user's20%exit target neither is mechanically impossible. Nevertheless B's base return is modest over nine years and dependent on split rents, fees, construction timing and exit appreciation. This supports focused execution/evidence work, not automatic Deploy or permanent SOP relaxation.

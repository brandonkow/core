## Event and duration discipline

The base leases begin in months 2,15,28,41,54; vacant months are 1,14,27,40,53. One-year leases are twelve paid months, not eleven-month contracts. The five-year window contains 55 paid months and five new-tenancy commissions per stream. The first fit-out and marketing month is already the first vacant month.

S8 once starts in month3 after two initial void months, then follows normal replacements. The persistent bound uses two months at every replacement and contains 50 paid months over60months. It is not calculated by blindly multiplying nine net months by five calendar years. The last partial lease and event boundaries matter.

Under the 8% chargeable-invoice reference, S8's ten paid months less one-month commission leaves 8.92 rent equivalents before other costs. The user-specified nine is correct before that invoice tax. A separately labelled invoice-inclusive/not-chargeable diagnostic removes the incremental SST; no assumption that tax is always additional or can simply be waived.

S24 timing is a bounded near-expiry default, not a claim that all defaults occur then. If an actual default occurs earlier, reschedule the lease and replacement events rather than adding a new void and commission on top of an unchanged base calendar. Deposits are liabilities until legitimately applied; none is booked as free income.

## Diagnostics, not new admission hurdles

Flat-price exit, year3 +10%, same-tenant renewal, invoice-tax treatment and B whole-unit restoration expose dependencies. Flat-price profitability is not a newly imposed gate. Neither a loss in a selected stress nor failure to reach20% appreciation automatically produces Reject. Funding, likelihood, evidence and opportunity cost remain separate existing-engine judgments.

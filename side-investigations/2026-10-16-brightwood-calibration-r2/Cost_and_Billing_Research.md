# Billing and transaction-cost research

Checked16October2026. MYR. Sources in [register](Sources.json); current rules are references, not future invoices.

## P19: general billing

[LAM GC2/2017](https://classic.lam.gov.my/sites/default/files/form/GC-02-2017.pdf), ScheduleH: foundation10%, structure15%, walls10%, roofing/ceiling/services10%, finishes10%, sewerage5%, drains2.5%, roads2.5% of gross consideration. Construction stages total65%. The circular also identifies17.5%at vacant possession and2.5%for title/transfer delivery. These are certification triggers, not monthly dates or a complete project-specific payment ledger.

Retain user-approved50%average loan utilisation over48months at4%: D39,672/B39,960 interest. The generic schedule does not prove that average. Actual dated disbursements replace it when available. User's10%rebate offsets initial payment and90%gross debt funds the balance. Do not charge another deposit or multiply every bank-funded gross stage invoice by90%again. Bank disbursement and later release of stakeholder funds are different events.

## P17: acquisition

Remove blanket5%gross allowance. Itemise buyer-paid purchase/loan legal fees, transfer/loan duties, applicable service tax, registration/searches/disbursements and later perfection if applicable. Developer coverage is recorded by item, never presumed from agreement to avoid double counting. Unknown total is not zero.

HDA purchase and financing use relevant SRO TableB provisions, distinct from ordinary resale TableA. Do not infer a duty exemption from advertised nett below500k, or assume which price/valuation is assessed without the instrument. Original title perfection, if paid later, is counted once as acquisition/title completion and is not the next purchaser's duty.

## P24: seller costs

[SRO2023](https://www.malaysianbar.org.my/cms/upload_files/document/Solicitors%20Remuneration%20Order%202023.pdf): ordinary sale scale1.25%on first500k, then1%on next7m. TableA permits up to25%discount; none presumed. First-title discharge legal fee400, or reassignment500 plus200if revoking power of attorney. CKHT1A preparation minimum500. Add only required filing/consent and actual disbursements. Normal work is included; do not stack alternative routes or duplicate ordinary work. These are reference scales, not an authenticated seller engagement.

| Component | Case treatment |
|---|---|
| Agent |3%of sale per user; not total exit cost.|
| Seller legal |Actual representation scope/quote; undiscounted scale reference below.|
| Discharge OR reassignment |Choose applicable route.|
| Filing/consent/searches/registration |Required work only, itemised; no invented percentage.|
| SST |Current8%on taxable services by a registered provider, if not already included. Not8%of property price or automatically of all reimbursements.|
| Bank charges |Actual redemption/lock-in charges only; no invented penalty. Debt repayment is financing cashflow, not another expense.|
| Closing adjustments |Unpaid rates/charges and deposits reconciled once. Tenant deposits are liabilities, not profit.|
| Purchaser transfer duty |Generally transferee's obligation, not automatically seller cost.|
| Tax retention |Separate cash timing/tax credit/refund; not automatically final loss or a second tax expense.|

SST: [Customs current background](https://mysst.customs.gov.my/background/) and [professional-service scope guide](https://mysst.customs.gov.my/assets/document/1.%20Guide%20on%20Professional%20S~1.pdf). The2018guide is used for scope, not its old tax rate.

Duty allocation: [current HASiL introduction](https://www.hasil.gov.my/en/duti-setem/pengenalan-duti-setem/) links the [2014StampAct reprint](https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/akta-setem-1949-01072014.pdf), whose ThirdSchedule assigns conveyance duty to the transferee. The reprint is not a complete2026rate/exemption guide.

[HASiL retention guidance](https://www.hasil.gov.my/en/ckht/pegangan-dan-remitan-wang-oleh-pemeroleh/) distinguishes remittance from final tax and describes a2026deemed-assessment option. Pretax reporting excludes personalised income/RPGT calculations, not payable invoice SST. Handover-based investment holding time is not automatically the statutory tax acquisition-date rule.
